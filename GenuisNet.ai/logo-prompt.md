# Logo Design Prompt for GenuisNet.ai

## Project Overview
Create a modern, futuristic logo for **GenuisNet.ai** - a premium AI tools directory and comparison platform. The logo should reflect innovation, intelligence, and cutting-edge technology while maintaining a sleek, professional aesthetic.

---

## Brand Identity

**Name:** GenuisNet.ai
**Tagline:** "Your Ultimate Guide to AI Tools"
**Industry:** AI Technology, SaaS Directory, Tech Education
**Target Audience:** Developers, Tech Professionals, AI Enthusiasts, Network Engineers, Business Decision Makers

**Brand Personality:**
- Innovative & Futuristic
- Professional & Trustworthy
- Tech-Forward & Premium
- Intelligent & Sophisticated
- Clean & Modern

---

## Visual Style Requirements

### Color Palette (Primary)
- **Neon Cyan:** #00D9FF (Primary brand color - represents innovation)
- **Deep Purple:** #7C3AED (Secondary - represents intelligence)
- **Electric Green:** #10B981 (Accent - represents growth/AI)

### Color Palette (Supporting)
- **Background Dark:** #0A0A0F (Deep space black)
- **Gradient:** Linear gradient from #00D9FF to #7C3AED at 135° angle
- **Glow Effects:** Neon glow with 0-40px blur, rgba opacity 0.3-0.8

### Typography Style
- **Font Family:** Inter (sans-serif) - Clean, modern, tech-forward
- **Logo Text:** "Genuis**Net**.ai"
  - "Genuis" in white/cyan
  - "Net" highlighted in gradient or contrasting color
  - ".ai" in cyan accent

## Logo Concept & Elements

### Core Design Elements

1. **Neural Network Motif**
   - Interconnected nodes representing AI neural networks
   - Flowing data connections between nodes
   - Abstract representation of machine learning pathways
   - Geometric precision with organic flow

2. **Technology Symbols**
   - Circuit board patterns (subtle, minimalist)
   - Digital grid or matrix elements
   - Hexagonal or geometric tech patterns
   - Data flow visualization

3. **AI/Intelligence Symbolism**
   - Brain-inspired abstract shapes
   - Light bulb concept (innovation)
   - Network hub/connection point
   - Abstract "G" or "AI" lettermark

### Preferred Logo Styles

**Option 1: Neural Network Icon + Wordmark**
```

- Central hub with 3-5 nodes around it
- Gradient connections between nodes (cyan to purple)
- Neon glow effect on connections

```

**Option 2: Letter Mark "G" + Network**
```
[Stylized "G" made of connected nodes] GenuisNet.ai
- Letter "G" formed by neural pathways
- Glowing connection points at intersections

```

**Option 3: Circuit Board Badge**
```

- Hexagon/shield shape (represents security & technology)

```

## Technical Specifications

### Format Requirements
- **Primary Logo:** Full color on dark background
- **File Formats:** SVG (vector), PNG (transparent), AI/EPS
- **Sizes:** Scalable from 32px (favicon) to 1024px (high-res)
- **Variations Needed:**
  - Horizontal lockup (icon + text)
  - Vertical lockup (icon above text)
  - Icon only (app icon, favicon)
  - Monochrome version (white on dark)

### Design Constraints
- Must work at small sizes (16x16px favicon)
- Must be recognizable in grayscale
- Should have clear icon/symbol that works standalone
- Text must remain legible at all sizes
- Neon glow effects should be subtle enough for professional use

---

## Style References & Mood

### Visual Inspiration
- **Futuristic Tech:** Tron-inspired neon aesthetics
- **Neural Networks:** Abstract data visualization, connected nodes
- **Premium SaaS:** Stripe, Vercel, Linear app logos (clean, modern)
- **AI Brands:** OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind aesthetics (intelligent, sophisticated)
- **Neon Cyberpunk:** Glowing edges, electric colors, dark backgrounds

### What to AVOID
- ❌ Generic brain icons (too cliché)
- ❌ Overly complex illustrations (won't scale)
- ❌ Bright/saturated backgrounds (we use dark mode)
- ❌ Corporate/traditional business aesthetics
- ❌ Cluttered designs with too many elements
- ❌ Comic sans or playful fonts
- ❌ Gradients that don't match brand colors

---

## Logo Animation Potential
The logo should be designed with animation in mind:
- Nodes could pulse with energy
- Connections could flow with data
- Glow effects could intensify on hover
- Elements could assemble/construct on load
