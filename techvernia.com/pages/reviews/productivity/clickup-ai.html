Overview

ClickUp AI represents the next evolution of project management, combining powerful task organization with intelligent AI assistance. As one of the most comprehensive productivity platforms available, ClickUp has integrated AI throughout its interface to help teams work smarter, not harder.

What sets ClickUp AI apart is its seamless integration into every aspect of the platform. From automatically generating task descriptions and subtasks to writing meeting summaries and polishing documentation, the AI assistant is there whenever you need it. It's not a separate tool - it's woven into the fabric of ClickUp's workflow.

With over 10 million users worldwide, ClickUp has proven itself as a versatile solution for teams of all sizes. The addition of AI capabilities transforms it from a great project management tool into an intelligent workspace that anticipates your needs and accelerates your productivity.

Key Features

AI Task Generation

Automatically create tasks, subtasks, and action items from meetings, documents, or simple prompts. AI understands context and generates structured work.

AI Writing Assistant

Write and polish docs, emails, and task descriptions with AI. Generate content, improve clarity, fix grammar, and adjust tone instantly.

Smart Summaries

AI automatically summarizes long threads, documents, and meeting notes into digestible highlights. Save hours of reading time.

Workflow Automation

Create sophisticated automations without code. AI suggests automations based on your team's patterns and workflow needs.

AI Templates

Pre-built AI templates for project planning, sprint planning, meeting agendas, and more. Customize with natural language.

ClickUp Brain

Ask questions about your workspace and get instant answers. Brain searches across all tasks, docs, and conversations.

Smart Insights

AI analyzes your team's productivity patterns and suggests optimizations. Identify bottlenecks and improve workflows.

Priority Intelligence

AI helps prioritize tasks based on deadlines, dependencies, and team capacity. Focus on what matters most.

ClickUp Brain Deep Dive

ClickUp Brain is the AI-powered knowledge manager that connects everything in your workspace. Ask questions in natural language and get instant answers pulled from tasks, docs, comments, and chat. It's like having a team member who remembers everything and can find any information in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay extra for ClickUp AI features?+
Yes, AI features like ClickUp Brain and the AI Writing Assistant are available as add-ons starting from the Business plan ($12/user/month) or can be purchased separately. The core project management features work without AI.
Is ClickUp suitable for small teams?+
Absolutely! ClickUp offers a generous free forever plan for small teams. While it has advanced features for large enterprises, small teams can start simple and add complexity as they grow. The AI features scale well for teams of any size.
Can ClickUp AI write entire project plans?+
Yes! Give ClickUp AI a project description and it can generate complete project plans with tasks, subtasks, timelines, and assignments. You can refine and customize the AI-generated plan to fit your specific needs.
How does ClickUp Brain work?+
ClickUp Brain indexes all content in your workspace - tasks, docs, comments, and chat. Ask it questions in natural language and it retrieves relevant information instantly. Think of it as a search engine that understands context and can synthesize answers.
Does ClickUp integrate with other tools?+
Yes, ClickUp integrates with 1000+ tools including Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Figma, Zoom, and more. The AI features work across integrations, so you can use AI on imported content from other platforms.
Is there a mobile app with AI features?+
Yes, ClickUp has iOS and Android apps with AI features including the writing assistant and ClickUp Brain. However, some advanced features work better on the web version. Mobile apps are great for task management on the go.
