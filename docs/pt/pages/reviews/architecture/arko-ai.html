AI-Powered Architectural Design & Floor Plan Generation

Visão Geral

Arko AI is a cutting-edge AI-powered platform that revolutionizes architectural design by automating floor plan generation and 3D modeling. Launched in 2022, it has quickly gained traction among architects and building designers who need to rapidly prototype residential and commercial spaces.

The platform leverages advanced machine learning algorithms to generate code-compliant floor plans based on site constraints, program requirements, and design preferences. With over 50,000 architects using the platform, Arko AI has proven itself as a valuable tool for early-stage design exploration and client presentations.

What sets Arko AI apart is its intelligent understanding of architectural principles. The AI doesn't just randomly arrange rooms—it considers circulation patterns, natural lighting, building codes, and spatial relationships to create functional, buildable designs. This makes it far more than a simple layout generator.

Key Innovation

Arko AI can generate multiple design variations in minutes, allowing architects to explore dozens of options in the time it would take to manually draft one floor plan.

Recursos Principais

AI Floor Plan Generation

Automatically generates optimized floor plans based on site dimensions, program requirements, and design constraints. Produces multiple variations instantly.

3D Visualization

Converts 2D floor plans into 3D models with realistic materials and lighting. Helps clients visualize spaces before construction begins.

Design Optimization

AI analyzes layouts for circulation efficiency, space utilization, and functional relationships. Suggests improvements based on best practices.

Code Compliance

Automatically checks designs against building codes and zoning regulations. Flags potential compliance issues before they become costly problems.

Material Estimation

Provides preliminary material quantities and cost estimates based on generated designs. Helps with early-stage budgeting and feasibility analysis.

Style Transfer

Apply different architectural styles to your designs instantly. From modern minimalist to traditional Victorian—explore aesthetic variations effortlessly.

📐 Parametric Design

Adjust key parameters like room sizes, ceiling heights, and window placement with real-time updates. Fine-tune AI-generated designs to your exact specifications.

BIM Integration

Export designs to popular BIM platforms like Revit and ArchiCAD. Seamlessly transition from AI-generated concepts to detailed construction documents.

Perguntas Frequentes

Is Arko AI suitable for large commercial projects?
Arko AI is primarily optimized for residential and small commercial projects. For large-scale commercial or institutional buildings, you may need more specialized tools like Hypar or traditional BIM workflows.
Can I edit the AI-generated floor plans?
Yes, Arko AI provides parametric controls that allow you to adjust room sizes, wall positions, and other elements. You can also export to BIM software for detailed refinement.
Does Arko AI replace traditional architectural software?
No, Arko AI is designed to accelerate the early design phase, not replace comprehensive BIM platforms. Most architects use it for concept generation, then refine designs in Revit or ArchiCAD.
How accurate is the code compliance checking?
Arko AI's code compliance features are improving but should be verified by a licensed professional. It's excellent for catching obvious issues early, but cannot replace proper code review.
What file formats can I export to?
Professional and Enterprise plans support DWG, DXF, IFC, Revit (RVT), and PDF exports. The free tier is limited to PDF with watermarks.
Is there a learning curve?
The basic interface is quite intuitive, and most architects can generate their first design within 15 minutes. Mastering the parametric controls and optimization features takes a few hours of practice.
