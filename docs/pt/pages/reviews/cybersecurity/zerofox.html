Visão Geral

ZeroFox is a leading digital risk protection platform that leverages AI and machine learning to safeguard organizations against external cyber threats across social media, mobile apps, cloud platforms, email, and the surface, deep, and dark web. Founded to address the growing attack surface beyond traditional perimeters, ZeroFox provides comprehensive brand protection, executive protection, and threat intelligence capabilities.

The platform continuously monitors billions of data points across 4,000+ platforms and thousands of underground forums to detect phishing, impersonation, data leakage, brand abuse, and other digital risks. With its AI-driven automation and expert human analysis, ZeroFox enables security teams to identify, analyze, and respond to threats that could damage reputation, compromise credentials, or enable fraud.

Trusted by enterprises, government agencies, and financial institutions worldwide, ZeroFox has become essential for organizations that need to protect their digital presence, brand integrity, and stakeholders from sophisticated social engineering and external threats that bypass conventional security controls.

Perguntas Frequentes

What types of threats does ZeroFox detect?

ZeroFox detects phishing campaigns, brand impersonation, account takeovers, executive threats, data leakage, fraudulent domains, fake mobile apps, counterfeit products, trademark infringement, and social engineering attacks across digital channels.

How quickly can ZeroFox take down malicious content?

Takedown times vary by platform and threat type. Social media accounts are typically removed within hours, while domain takedowns may take 24-72 hours depending on registrar cooperation. ZeroFox achieves a 98%+ takedown success rate.

Does ZeroFox integrate with existing security tools?

Yes, ZeroFox integrates with major SIEM platforms (Splunk, QRadar, ArcSight), SOAR solutions (Phantom, Demisto), ticketing systems (ServiceNow, Jira), and threat intelligence platforms via REST API and native connectors.

What platforms does ZeroFox monitor?

ZeroFox monitors 4,000+ platforms including major social networks (Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram), messaging apps, forums, app stores, domain registrations, paste sites, code repositories, dark web marketplaces, and underground forums.

Is ZeroFox suitable for small businesses?

While ZeroFox serves enterprises primarily, smaller organizations with significant digital presence or high-value brand protection needs can benefit. However, pricing and resource requirements may make it better suited for mid-size to large enterprises.

How does ZeroFox's AI work?

ZeroFox employs machine learning models trained on billions of data points to classify threats, predict risk levels, identify patterns, and automate responses. Models continuously improve through feedback loops and expert analyst validation.
