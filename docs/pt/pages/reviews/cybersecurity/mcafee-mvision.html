Visão Geral

McAfee MVISION is a leading endpoint security solution that combines advanced threat detection, prevention, and response capabilities. The platform leverages artificial intelligence and machine learning to protect organizations against sophisticated cyber threats including malware, ransomware, and zero-day attacks.

With cloud-native architecture and comprehensive threat intelligence, McAfee MVISION provides enterprise-grade security that scales from small businesses to global organizations. The solution integrates seamlessly with existing security infrastructure while offering centralized management and visibility across all endpoints.

Built for the modern threat landscape, McAfee MVISION delivers proactive protection through behavioral analysis, sandboxing, and automated response—stopping threats before they can cause damage.

Perguntas Frequentes

What makes McAfee MVISION different?

The platform combines advanced AI/ML detection with comprehensive threat intelligence and proven enterprise capabilities. Its unique approach to threat prevention and response sets it apart in the market.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is based on number of protected endpoints with tiered features. Contact sales for custom quotes based on your organization size and requirements.

Is cloud or on-premise deployment available?

Both deployment options are available. Cloud-native management is standard, with on-premise options for organizations with specific data residency requirements.

What platforms are supported?

Protection is available for Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile devices (iOS/Android), all managed from a single unified console.

How effective is the ransomware protection?

The solution uses behavioral detection and machine learning to identify ransomware encryption patterns, automatically isolating threats and providing rollback capabilities to recover encrypted files.
