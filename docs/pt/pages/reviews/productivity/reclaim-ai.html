Visão geral

Reclaim.ai represents the next evolution of project management, combining powerful task organization with intelligent AI assistance. As one of the most comprehensive productivity platforms available, Reclaim has integrated AI throughout its interface to help teams work smarter, not harder.

What sets Reclaim.ai apart is its seamless integration into every aspect of the platform. From automatically generating task descriptions and subtasks to writing meeting summaries and polishing documentation, the AI assistant is there whenever you need it. It's not a separate tool - it's woven into the fabric of Reclaim's workflow.

With over 10 million users worldwide, Reclaim has proven itself as a versatile solution for teams of all sizes. The addition of AI capabilities transforms it from a great project management tool into an intelligent workspace that anticipates your needs and accelerates your productivity.

Características Principais

AI Task Generation

Automatically create tasks, subtasks, and action items from meetings, documents, or simple prompts. AI understands context and generates structured work.

Auto-scheduling Assistant

Perguntas Mais Frequentes

Do I need to pay extra for Reclaim.ai features?+
Yes, AI features like Reclaim Brain and the Auto-scheduling Assistant are available as add-ons starting from the Business plan ($12/user/month) or can be purchased separately. The core project management features work without AI.
Is Reclaim suitable for small teams?+
Absolutely! Reclaim offers a generous free forever plan for small teams. While it has advanced features for large enterprises, small teams can start simple and add complexity as they grow. The AI features scale well for teams of any size.
Can Reclaim.ai write entire project plans?+
Yes! Give Reclaim.ai a project description and it can generate complete project plans with tasks, subtasks, timelines, and assignments. You can refine and customize the AI-generated plan to fit your specific needs.
How does Reclaim Brain work?+
Reclaim Brain indexes all content in your workspace - tasks, docs, comments, and chat. Ask it questions in natural language and it retrieves relevant information instantly. Think of it as a search engine that understands context and can synthesize answers.
Does Reclaim integrate with other tools?+
Yes, Reclaim integrates with 1000+ tools including Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Figma, Zoom, and more. The AI features work across integrations, so you can use AI on imported content from other platforms.
Is there a mobile app with AI features?+
Yes, Reclaim has iOS and Android apps with AI features including the writing assistant and Reclaim Brain. However, some advanced features work better on the web version. Mobile apps are great for task management on the go.
