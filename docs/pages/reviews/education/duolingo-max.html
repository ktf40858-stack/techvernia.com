Overview

Duolingo Max is a subscription tier above Super Duolingo, announced in March 2023 and built on GPT-4. It includes everything in Super, such as no ads, unlimited energy and personalised review in the Practice Hub, plus AI conversation features.

The two headline features are Video Call, where you talk with the character Lily in your target language, and Roleplay, scenario-based conversations such as ordering coffee in Paris, with AI feedback on accuracy and complexity. Explain My Answer, once exclusive to Max, is free for all users as of 2026, according to Wikipedia.

Feature coverage depends on the course. Duolingo's Max page says English speakers learning Spanish, French, German, Italian or Portuguese get both Video Call and Roleplay, while learners of Japanese, Korean or Chinese get Video Call only. Max is available on iOS and Android in 188 countries.

Key Features

Video Call with Lily

Spoken conversations with Lily, who remembers what you discussed last time; a transcript is available after each call.

Roleplay

Scenario-based conversations that earn XP as Side Quests, with feedback on accuracy and complexity.

Everything in Super

No ads, unlimited energy and personalised review through the Practice Hub.

GPT-4 Under the Hood

Duolingo introduced Max as a learning experience powered by GPT-4.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Speaking practice with an AI conversation partner
  • Feedback on the accuracy and complexity of your answers
  • Includes all Super Duolingo benefits
  • Available on iOS and Android in 188 countries

Disadvantages

  • Prices are not published on the website; they appear in the app
  • Roleplay is not available for Japanese, Korean or Chinese courses
  • Explain My Answer is no longer Max-only, which narrows the gap with Super
  • Duolingo only lists iOS and Android for Max

Pricing

Duolingo does not publish Max prices on its website; they are shown in the app and vary by country. Duolingo has three tiers:

Free

Most lessons, supported by ads.

Super Duolingo

No ads, unlimited energy and personalised review in the Practice Hub.

Duolingo Max

Everything in Super plus Video Call and Roleplay.

Best Use Cases

Duolingo Max Excels For:

  • Learners of Spanish, French, German, Italian or Portuguese who want speaking practice
  • Super subscribers who want conversation practice
  • Learners who prefer practicing on a phone

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Learners who only need the free core lessons
  • Japanese, Korean or Chinese learners who want Roleplay
  • Learners who want to practice on the web

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Duolingo Max include?

Everything in Super Duolingo (no ads, unlimited energy, Practice Hub review) plus Video Call with Lily and Roleplay.

Which courses support Video Call and Roleplay?

According to Duolingo, English speakers learning Spanish, French, German, Italian or Portuguese get both; Japanese, Korean and Chinese learners get Video Call only.

Is Explain My Answer still a Max feature?

No. According to Wikipedia, Explain My Answer was free for all users as of 2026.

How much does Duolingo Max cost?

Duolingo shows Max prices in the app; they vary by country and are not published on its website.

Which AI model does Max use?

Duolingo introduced Max in March 2023 as powered by GPT-4.
