Overview

CENTURY Tech is a London-based online learning platform for English, maths and science. It combines learning science, AI and neuroscience to tailor learning to each student, with thousands of learning videos and self-marking questions.

It serves primary and secondary schools (KS1-4), international schools following the English national curriculum or IB, tutoring businesses, FE colleges, apprenticeship providers and initial teacher training. A home version covers home learning, entrance exam preparation and home education for ages 6 to 16.

For teachers, the pitch is less time spent on marking, analysis and resource creation, plus data for targeted interventions. CENTURY says it is used by providers in more than 70 countries.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Published family pricing (from £8.33/month on an annual plan)
  • Aligned to the English national curriculum, with IB support for international schools
  • Covers primary, secondary, post-16 and teacher training
  • Self-marking questions reduce marking time
  • Entrance exam preparation with Bond and ISEB content

Disadvantages

  • School and college pricing is not published; you need to book a demo
  • Core subjects are limited to English, maths and science
  • Curriculum is UK-centric
  • No free trial is advertised on the homepage

Frequently Asked Questions

Which subjects does CENTURY cover?

English, maths and science. Secondary schools also get geography and PE content.

How much does CENTURY cost for families?

Home learning and home education start at £8.33/month and entrance exam preparation at £20.66/month, on annual plans.

Can schools outside the UK use CENTURY?

Yes. There is a version for international schools following the English national curriculum or IB, and CENTURY says it is used in over 70 countries.

How do schools get pricing?

School and college pricing is not published. CENTURY asks schools to book a demo.
