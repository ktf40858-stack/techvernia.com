Overview

MagicSchool AI is the leading AI platform for K-12 teachers, offering over 60 purpose-built AI tools that save educators time on planning, differentiation, communication, and assessment. Founded in 2023 by Adeel Khan, a former teacher, MagicSchool grew to 3 million teachers in its first year — making it one of the fastest-growing edtech platforms ever. The platform is designed specifically for teachers' real workflows: generating lesson plans, adapting content for different learning levels, writing parent communications, and creating assessments.

Unlike generic AI tools like ChatGPT, MagicSchool is purpose-built for education with pre-configured templates for teaching-specific tasks. Teachers don't need to learn prompt engineering — they select the task (differentiate this text for ELL students, generate a rubric, write a parent email) and MagicSchool handles the rest. The platform also has built-in safety guardrails appropriate for educational use.

In 2026, MagicSchool has expanded into student tools (with teacher oversight), school/district dashboards, and curriculum planning features. The platform is used in districts across the US, UK, and internationally, and has partnered with major educational publishers and curriculum providers.

Key Features

60+ Teacher AI Tools

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MagicSchool AI free for teachers?+
MagicSchool has a free tier that includes 20 AI tools with limited monthly generations — sufficient to evaluate the platform. The Teacher Plus plan ($9.99/month) unlocks all 60+ tools with unlimited use. Many schools and districts purchase subscriptions for all teachers.
Is MagicSchool AI safe for use with student data?+
MagicSchool is FERPA compliant and follows student data privacy best practices. Teacher tools don't require inputting student PII. Student tools include teacher oversight features. School districts should review the privacy policy for their specific jurisdiction requirements.
Does MagicSchool work for subjects other than English/Language Arts?+
Yes — MagicSchool has tools for all subjects including math, science, social studies, and physical education. Subject-specific tools include science lab report templates, math problem generators, and historical document analysis.
Can MagicSchool generate content in languages other than English?+
Yes — MagicSchool supports content generation in multiple languages, and its parent communication tools can draft emails in the family's home language. This is particularly valuable for schools with diverse ELL populations.
