Overview

SchoolAI is a comprehensive AI platform for K-12 schools that provides both student-facing AI tools and teacher management controls. Unlike MagicSchool (primarily teacher tools) or ChatGPT (not designed for education), SchoolAI is built for the complete classroom AI ecosystem — students get AI learning assistance while teachers maintain full visibility and control over how AI is used in their classroom.

SchoolAI's "Spaces" feature allows teachers to create custom AI-powered learning environments for their students: a tutor for a specific unit, a writing coach with defined guardrails, or a Socratic discussion partner. Students access these controlled AI experiences, and teachers see exactly what each student asked and received. This visibility is crucial for responsible AI integration in schools.

In 2026, SchoolAI has become one of the most widely adopted school AI platforms in the US, used by hundreds of thousands of students and their teachers. The platform's emphasis on teacher control and transparency has made it palatable to school administrators who might otherwise be concerned about students using unsupervised AI.

Key Features

Student AI Spaces

Teachers create custom AI learning environments (tutors, writing coaches, discussion partners) with defined topics, guardrails, and interaction styles. Students access teacher-configured AI, not open-ended chatbots.

Teacher Transparency Dashboard

See every student conversation with the AI in real time. Understand how each student is using AI and where they're struggling.

Safe AI Design

Purpose-built safety for K-12 use: content filtering, topic restrictions, age-appropriate language, and protection against misuse.

Differentiated Learning Paths

AI adapts to each student's level within the teacher-defined Space. Advanced students get challenge questions; struggling students get more scaffolding.

Class Insights

Aggregate analytics across the class: common misconceptions, engagement patterns, topics where students needed the most help. Inform future instruction.

Assignment Integration

Connect AI Spaces to specific assignments. Students use AI assistance within the context of the work they're doing, not as a standalone tool.

How It Compares

SchoolAI vs MagicSchool AI

MagicSchool focuses on teacher tools (planning, differentiation). SchoolAI focuses on student-facing AI with teacher oversight. Many schools use both together.

SchoolAI vs Khan Academy Khanmigo

Khanmigo is a conversational AI tutor from Khan Academy's content. SchoolAI is a platform that lets teachers build custom AI experiences tied to their own curriculum.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

SchoolAI solves the hardest problem in school AI adoption: how do you give students the benefits of AI assistance while keeping teachers informed and in control? The Spaces framework elegantly answers this — teachers configure the AI experience, students use it, and teachers see everything. For schools that want to thoughtfully integrate AI into learning rather than either banning it or letting students use uncontrolled tools, SchoolAI provides the right balance of access and oversight.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can teachers see what students type to the AI?+
Yes — this is a core feature of SchoolAI. Teachers have a transparency dashboard showing every student's conversation with AI Spaces, in real time. This oversight capability is what makes SchoolAI safe for K-12 deployment.
Do students access the open internet through SchoolAI?+
No — students only interact with the AI Spaces their teachers have created, not the open internet or unconstrained AI. Teachers define the topic boundaries, guardrails, and interaction style for each Space.
How are SchoolAI Spaces different from just using ChatGPT?+
ChatGPT is a general-purpose AI with no teacher oversight, content guardrails specific to K-12, or visibility into student interactions. SchoolAI Spaces are teacher-configured learning environments with topic restrictions, age-appropriate filtering, and full teacher visibility.
Is SchoolAI COPPA/FERPA compliant?+
Yes — SchoolAI is designed for K-12 compliance including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act). This compliance is essential for school adoption and is a key differentiator from general AI tools.
