What is Pitch?

Pitch is a Berlin-based presentation software that has positioned itself as the modern alternative to PowerPoint and Google Slides — with a strong emphasis on team collaboration, professional design, and AI-assisted slide creation. Launched in 2020, Pitch quickly gained traction among startups, venture-backed companies, and creative agencies who needed to produce investor-quality decks without a dedicated design team.

Unlike generic slide tools, Pitch is built around the idea that presentations are a team sport. Multiple collaborators can work on the same deck simultaneously, leave comments on specific slides, and track version history — all in real time. In 2026, Pitch has added a full AI assistant that can generate slide content, suggest layouts, rewrite copy, and auto-format slides based on your brand palette.

What makes Pitch stand out in an increasingly crowded market is the quality of its template library and the polish of its default design system — new users can look like professional designers from the first deck they create.

Key Features

AI Design & Content Assistant

Pitch's AI assistant can generate full slide decks from a text prompt, suggesting a logical structure, writing slide copy, and applying design layouts automatically. You can also highlight existing text on a slide and ask the AI to expand it, shorten it, or reframe it for a different audience. The AI is context-aware — if you've set a brand palette, it applies your colors and fonts consistently across generated slides.

Real-Time Collaboration

Pitch's collaboration model is closer to Figma than Google Slides: you see teammates' cursors in real time, can assign slides to specific team members, and leave threaded comments tied to individual slides or elements. For agencies managing client reviews, this is a significant workflow improvement over emailing decks back and forth. Version history is granular — you can restore any previous state with one click.

Premium Template Library

Pitch ships with 1,000+ professionally designed templates organized by use case: pitch decks, investor updates, sales decks, product roadmaps, quarterly reviews, and more. Templates are not just pretty — they're structurally sound, with logical section flow and placeholder content that guides you toward the right narrative. Many are contributed by top design studios, giving them a quality level above what most competitors offer.

Deck Analytics

When you share a Pitch deck via a public link, you get detailed viewer analytics: who viewed it, how long they spent on each slide, where they dropped off, and whether they forwarded it. For sales teams and fundraising founders, this data is invaluable — knowing a VC spent 45 seconds on your market size slide tells you where to focus the follow-up conversation.

Brand Kit & Custom Fonts

Teams can upload a brand kit — logo, color palette, typography — and Pitch applies it to any template automatically. Custom fonts from Google Fonts or uploaded font files are supported. This ensures every deck produced by the team stays on-brand without manual formatting work on each slide.

Final Verdict

Pitch is the best presentation tool for teams that prioritize collaboration, design quality, and analytics over raw AI generation power. The template library is genuinely the best in the category — if you need to produce a professional-looking investor deck or sales presentation quickly, the quality ceiling is higher than any other tool at this price point.

The AI assistant is capable but still secondary to the collaboration and design core. If your primary use case is generating a full deck from a prompt with minimal editing, Tome or Presentations.AI may be stronger. But for teams managing multiple presentations simultaneously, sharing decks externally with analytics, and enforcing brand consistency across departments, Pitch is the clear recommendation.

Best for: Startups, sales teams, agencies, and anyone who needs to produce professional decks collaboratively at scale.
