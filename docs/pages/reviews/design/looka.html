What is Looka?

Looka (formerly Logojoy) is an AI-powered brand identity platform that helps entrepreneurs, small business owners, and startups create professional logos and brand assets without any graphic design experience. Founded in 2016, Looka has generated over 2 million logos for businesses worldwide.

The platform uses machine learning to understand your preferences � industry, colors, style, and symbols � and generates dozens of logo concepts within seconds. Unlike hiring a designer or using complex tools like Illustrator, Looka democratizes professional branding at a fraction of the cost.

In 2026, Looka has expanded beyond logos to offer full brand kits including business cards, social media templates, email signatures, and a custom website builder powered by your brand identity.

Key Features

AI Logo Generator

The core feature: answer a short questionnaire about your business name, industry, style preferences, colors, and symbols. Looka's AI instantly generates 100+ logo variations tailored to your inputs. Each concept is fully customizable � fonts, colors, layouts, icons � through an intuitive editor with no design knowledge required.

Brand Kit

Once you have your logo, Looka extends it into a complete brand identity. The Brand Kit subscription ($96/year) includes business card designs, letterhead, email signatures, social media profile images and banners, presentation templates, and merchandise mockups. Everything automatically uses your brand colors and logo.

Brand Kit Website

Looka now offers an AI website builder that generates a complete site using your brand identity. It's basic compared to Webflow or Framer, but it's a compelling option for small businesses that need a simple online presence that's perfectly on-brand without hiring a developer.

Logo Files & Formats

Purchase provides high-resolution files in PNG (with transparent background), SVG (scalable vector), and PDF formats. The Premium package also includes EPS files, which are industry-standard for print production. All files come with full commercial usage rights.

Customization Editor

Looka's web-based editor is beginner-friendly. You can adjust every element: swap icons from a library of 3M+ symbols, change font pairings, modify colors with hex codes, reposition elements, and try different layout styles. Real-time previews show your logo on business cards, t-shirts, and storefront mockups.

Pricing Breakdown

Looka uses a one-time purchase model for logos plus optional subscriptions for brand assets:

  • Basic Logo ($20): 1 PNG file at low resolution � suitable for digital use only. Very limited.
  • Premium Logo ($65): Full PNG, SVG, PDF, and EPS files plus all color variations. Best value for logo-only needs.
  • Brand Kit ($96/year): Everything in Premium Logo plus 300+ branded templates (business cards, social media, etc.). Subscription renews annually.
  • Brand Kit + Website ($129/year): Adds the AI website builder with a custom domain connection.

The Basic tier at $20 is misleadingly cheap � most businesses will need the $65 Premium for actual usability. However, compared to hiring a freelance designer ($300�$1,500+), even the premium tier is exceptional value for startups and solopreneurs.

Real-World Performance

Logo Generation Speed

The generation process takes about 2�3 minutes from answering the questionnaire to seeing your first concepts. The AI is genuinely good at interpreting style preferences � choosing "minimalist" vs "bold" or "tech" vs "artisan" produces noticeably different results. You'll get 50�100 concepts to browse through.

Customization Quality

The editor is intuitive and works well for basic adjustments. However, advanced customizations (complex icon combinations, exact spacing control, multi-element layouts) are limited. Power users accustomed to Illustrator or Figma will find it constrictive. For the target audience � non-designers � it's perfect.

Logo Uniqueness Concern

This is Looka's most valid criticism. Since all users draw from the same icon and font library with AI-applied combinations, there's a real chance another business has a similar logo. For businesses where brand differentiation is critical, a human designer remains the better option. For local services, restaurants, and online stores, Looka's output is more than sufficient.

Who Should Use Looka?

Looka is ideal for: solopreneurs and freelancers launching a new brand, small businesses that need a professional logo quickly, startups validating a concept before investing in full branding, and side projects that need visual identity without a designer's budget.

It's less suitable for: enterprise companies where brand originality is paramount, businesses in highly competitive markets where visual differentiation is critical, or anyone wanting a truly custom, one-of-a-kind design.

Final Verdict

8.1 / 10

Looka delivers on its core promise: professional-quality logos in minutes for a fraction of designer prices. The AI understands style preferences well, the customization editor is beginner-friendly, and the Brand Kit subscription provides solid value for startups needing consistent visual identity across multiple assets.

The trade-off is uniqueness � logos feel like AI-generated combinations rather than truly bespoke creations. For the price and speed, this is an entirely acceptable compromise for most small businesses. If your brand is the cornerstone of your competitive advantage, invest in a human designer. For everyone else, Looka is an outstanding starting point.

Best for: Solopreneurs, startups, small businesses, side projects needing quick professional branding on a budget.
