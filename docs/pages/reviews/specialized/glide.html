Overview

Glide is a no-code platform that transforms Google Sheets, Excel files, Airtable bases, and other data sources into fully functional business applications — without writing a single line of code. Founded in 2018 in San Francisco, Glide has enabled over 100,000 apps to be built by operations teams, founders, and citizen developers who need custom software without engineering resources. The platform's proposition is elegantly simple: your data is already in a spreadsheet, so your app should come from it directly.

In 2025–2026, Glide expanded significantly beyond its original drag-and-drop app builder with the introduction of Glide AI — a suite of features that bring generative AI directly into app workflows. Users can now build apps with AI-powered columns that classify, extract, summarize, or generate data automatically; create AI assistants embedded inside their apps; and build automated workflows that trigger AI actions based on user interactions or data changes.

The result is a platform that bridges the gap between simple spreadsheets and fully custom software: complex enough to replace real business processes, but accessible enough that a non-developer can build and deploy a production-ready internal tool in an afternoon. For teams managing field operations, inventory, customer databases, or approval workflows, Glide offers a compelling alternative to expensive custom development.

Key Features

Spreadsheet-to-App Conversion

Connect Google Sheets, Excel, Airtable, or SQL databases and Glide automatically generates an app UI from your data structure. Customize layouts, screens, and components with a visual editor — no coding required.

Glide AI Columns

Add AI-powered computed columns that automatically classify data, extract information, generate summaries, translate text, or assign categories — running on every row without manual intervention.

Embedded AI Assistants

Add AI chat interfaces directly inside your Glide app, grounded in your data. Employees can ask questions about your inventory, customers, or processes and get instant AI answers from real business data.

Workflow Automations

Build multi-step automations triggered by form submissions, button clicks, data changes, or schedules. Send notifications, update records, call APIs, or run AI actions — all configured visually.

Role-Based Access & Permissions

Control what each user can see and do based on their role or identity. Build apps with multiple user types — field workers, managers, admins — each with tailored views and permissions.

Native Mobile Apps

Glide apps work natively on mobile browsers and can be added to home screens as PWAs. Users get a native app experience without publishing to the App Store — instantly available to any team.

How It Compares

Glide vs AppSheet (Google)

AppSheet (owned by Google) is Glide's closest competitor. AppSheet has more advanced formula logic and deeper Google Workspace integration; Glide has a better visual design, more polished UX, and more accessible AI features. For teams prioritizing speed and aesthetics, Glide often wins.

Glide vs Bubble

Bubble is a more powerful no-code platform supporting full web app development. It has a steeper learning curve and higher complexity. Glide is faster to start and better for data-centric internal tools; Bubble is better for complex product apps with custom logic.

Glide vs Retool

Retool targets developers building internal tools with code. Glide targets non-developers. If your team has developer time, Retool offers more power; if they don't, Glide's no-code approach delivers faster results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code to use Glide?+
No — Glide is designed specifically for non-developers. You connect your data source (Google Sheets, Excel, Airtable, etc.), and Glide generates an app structure automatically. You then customize the interface, add screens, configure permissions, and set up automations using a visual editor. No coding knowledge is required for the vast majority of use cases.
What data sources can Glide connect to?+
Glide connects natively to Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, Airtable, BigQuery, and Glide's own hosted database (Glide Tables). For other data sources, you can connect via API or use Zapier/Make integrations to sync data. Most teams start with Google Sheets as their backend, which works well for apps with up to ~10,000 rows.
How do Glide AI Columns work?+
Glide AI Columns are computed columns powered by AI models. You configure what you want the AI to do — classify customer feedback sentiment, extract key information from product descriptions, generate personalized email subject lines, translate text — and Glide runs that AI operation on every row in your data automatically. AI Column usage is measured in credits and is available on Pro plans and above.
Can Glide apps work offline?+
Glide apps have limited offline functionality. Basic browsing of cached data is possible offline, but actions that require writing to the database (form submissions, record updates) require an internet connection to sync. For field teams in areas with spotty connectivity, this is a consideration — Glide works best with reliable mobile data access.
