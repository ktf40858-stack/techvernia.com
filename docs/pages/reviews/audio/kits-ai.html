Overview

Kits AI is an AI vocal transformation and voice model platform designed for music producers and artists. Founded in 2022, Kits AI allows musicians to transform their vocals using AI voice models — converting a sung performance into the style of different vocal characters, or creating custom AI voice models from sample recordings. In January 2026, Kits AI was acquired by Splice, the leading music production sample marketplace, marking a significant consolidation in the AI music tools space.

Kits AI's core capability is real-time vocal conversion: sing a melody or record a vocal performance, and Kits AI transforms it using an AI voice model — either from their library of community and artist voices, or a custom model you train on your own voice samples. This enables producers without professional vocalist access to explore vocal ideas, and artists to experiment with vocal styles before committing to recording sessions.

Post-Splice acquisition, Kits AI benefits from Splice's distribution reach and sample library integration, potentially enabling tight integration between AI vocal tools and Splice's massive library of loops, samples, and presets.

How It Compares

Kits AI vs ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs focuses on speech synthesis and voice cloning for content/video. Kits AI focuses specifically on music vocal transformation. Different primary applications.

Kits AI vs Suno AI / Udio

Suno and Udio generate complete songs from text. Kits AI transforms existing vocal recordings using AI models. Different workflows — Kits AI is for producers who already have vocal performances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happened when Splice acquired Kits AI?+
Splice, the leading music sample and plugin marketplace, acquired Kits AI in January 2026. Kits AI continues to operate as a product with growing integration into Splice's ecosystem. The acquisition gives Kits AI access to Splice's millions of users and potentially enables deep integration with Splice's sample library and production tools.
Can I use my own voice to create an AI model?+
Yes — Kits AI allows you to train a custom AI voice model from recordings of your own voice. You upload a set of vocal samples, the AI trains a model, and you can then use that model for vocal transformation. This creates a personalized AI vocal twin that captures your voice characteristics.
Is using artist voice models on Kits AI legal?+
Artist voice models on Kits AI are provided under specific licensing terms. Community-created models have varying permissions. Using AI voice models for commercial releases requires reviewing the specific license terms for each model. The legal landscape for AI voice reproduction is evolving rapidly.
How does Kits AI compare to singing in a DAW?+
Kits AI provides AI vocal transformation that traditional DAW pitch correction tools don't offer. While DAWs can pitch-correct and tune vocals, Kits AI actually transforms the vocal timbre and style using AI voice models — a fundamentally different capability that enables experimentation with completely different vocal characters.
