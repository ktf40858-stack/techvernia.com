Overview

Descript is revolutionizing audio and video editing by treating media like text documents. Instead of working with traditional timeline-based editing, you edit transcripts and the media automatically updates. This approach makes editing accessible to non-professionals while remaining powerful enough for seasoned creators.

What truly sets Descript apart is its AI-powered Overdub feature, which can clone your voice and generate speech in your own voice. Combined with industry-leading transcription accuracy, collaborative editing, screen recording, and a suite of AI tools for removing filler words and background noise, Descript has become the go-to platform for podcasters, YouTubers, and content creators.

Key Features

Text-Based Editing

Edit audio and video by editing the transcript. Delete words, rearrange sentences, and the media updates automatically.

Overdub Voice Cloning

Create an AI clone of your voice. Type text and generate natural speech in your own voice for corrections and additions.

Studio Sound

AI-powered audio enhancement that removes background noise, improves clarity, and makes recordings sound professionally produced.

Final Verdict

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is Descript's transcription? +
Descript offers industry-leading transcription accuracy at around 99% for clear audio with good recording quality. It automatically detects speakers and includes timestamps. You can edit any errors directly in the transcript.
How does Overdub voice cloning work? +
Overdub creates an AI model of your voice by having you read a script for about 10 minutes. Once trained, you can type any text and it will generate speech in your voice. It's perfect for fixing mistakes or adding new content without re-recording.
Can I use Descript for professional video editing? +
Yes, but with limitations. Descript excels at basic to intermediate video editing, especially for content creators. For advanced effects, complex motion graphics, or color grading, professional tools like Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve are still superior.
Is there a limit to how much I can transcribe? +
Yes, all plans except Business have monthly transcription limits. Free includes 1 hour, Hobbyist gets 10 hours, and Creator includes 30 hours per month. You can purchase additional hours if needed.
Can multiple people edit the same project? +
Yes, Descript supports real-time collaboration. Multiple team members can work on the same project simultaneously with commenting, version history, and shared libraries. This feature is available on Creator and Business plans.
Does Descript work on mobile devices? +
Descript is primarily a desktop application (Windows and Mac). While you can access some features through a web browser, the full editing experience is best on desktop. There's no dedicated mobile app currently.
