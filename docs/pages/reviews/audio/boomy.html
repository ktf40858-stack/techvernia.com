Boomy Overview

Unique value proposition: Boomy is the only major AI music tool that combines song creation with direct streaming distribution and revenue sharing. You don't just create music — you become a published recording artist on Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music, earning real streaming royalties on every play. Over 20 million AI songs have been created on the platform.

Founded in 2019 by Alex Mitchell and team, Boomy was built on the radical premise that anyone should be able to create and monetize music, regardless of musical training. The platform generates complete songs — including beats, melodies, and instrumentation — from a simple genre selection, then handles all the technical aspects of streaming distribution.

Boomy made headlines in 2023 when Spotify temporarily removed 7% of all Boomy tracks from its platform due to suspected streaming manipulation. The incident raised important questions about AI music and streaming economics, but also highlighted just how significant Boomy's footprint had become — millions of AI tracks were being actively streamed.

2025–2026 Update: Boomy introduced "Vocal AI" — allowing users to add AI-generated singing vocals to their instrumental tracks by entering lyrics. This moved Boomy closer to Suno AI territory, enabling non-singers to create complete songs with sung vocal performances. Boomy also raised the Pro royalty share from 70% to 80%.

Key Features

Instant Song Generation

Select a genre (Global Bass, Trap & Swag, Ambient, Lo-Fi, etc.) and hit Generate. Boomy produces a full song in under 30 seconds — no musical knowledge required.

Vocal AI

Add AI-generated singing vocals to any track. Enter your lyrics and choose a vocal style. The AI performs your lyrics over the generated instrumental — no recording equipment needed.

📡

Direct Streaming Distribution

Publish your songs to 40+ platforms including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, TikTok, and YouTube Music directly from Boomy — no third-party distributor required.

Streaming Revenue

Earn real streaming royalties on every play. Free plan users keep 40% of royalties; Personal plan 70%; Pro plan 80%. Payments via direct bank transfer or PayPal.

️

Basic Customization

Adjust energy level, add or remove instruments, and regenerate sections you dislike. Less flexible than Soundraw but sufficient for quick song creation.

Song Library

All created songs are saved in your personal library. Manage your catalog, track streaming performance, and see royalty earnings across all platforms in one dashboard.

🌍

Global Release

Songs appear on all streaming platforms worldwide — not just in your home country. Reach listeners in every market where Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music operate.

Analytics Dashboard

Track stream counts, listener geography, playlist placements, and royalty earnings across all platforms in Boomy's unified analytics dashboard.

Final Verdict — Is Boomy Worth It?

Boomy occupies a unique position in the AI music landscape: it is the only tool that turns AI music creation into a potential (if modest) income stream. The combination of instant song generation, Vocal AI for complete songs with singing, and direct streaming distribution to 40+ platforms is unmatched.

Realistic expectations matter: streaming royalties are tiny at typical play counts. Unless your tracks generate millions of streams, Boomy won't replace your income — but it provides a fascinating, accessible entry point into music publishing for complete non-musicians.

Recommended for: Non-musicians who want to create and publish music, hobbyists curious about the streaming economy, social media creators who want custom music they can monetize, and anyone interested in experimenting with AI music publishing.

Not recommended for: Professional content creators who need high-quality background tracks (use Soundraw), film/game composers (use AIVA), or users who want maximum customization control over their music.

Boomy retains certain rights to the underlying compositions and sounds it generates. However, you own the master recording and collect the streaming royalties. Read Boomy's Terms of Service carefully — the ownership model is more complex than traditional music creation and has evolved since the 2023 Spotify controversy.
After submitting a song for distribution, it typically appears on Spotify within 3–5 business days. Apple Music and Amazon Music usually take 5–7 days. TikTok can take 1–2 weeks. Boomy's Pro plan prioritizes distribution, which may reduce wait times slightly.
In May 2023, Spotify removed approximately 7% of all Boomy tracks — millions of songs — after detecting suspected artificial streaming manipulation (bots generating fake plays to earn royalties). Boomy cooperated with the investigation. This incident highlighted the challenges of AI music at scale, but Boomy continues to operate and distribute to Spotify today.
Boomy's Vocal AI produces passable sung vocals for casual use but lacks the nuance and emotion of Suno AI's vocal generation. For hobby projects and social media content, it works well. For professional music with high vocal quality expectations, Suno AI is significantly better. Boomy's strength remains in its distribution ecosystem, not vocal quality.
