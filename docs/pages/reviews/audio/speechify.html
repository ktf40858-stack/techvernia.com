Overview

Speechify is the world's leading text-to-speech reading assistant, trusted by over 20 million users including students, professionals, and individuals with reading difficulties. What makes Speechify stand out is its focus on making reading accessible, efficient, and enjoyable through natural-sounding AI voices and intelligent features designed specifically for consuming written content.

Founded by Cliff Weitzman, who has dyslexia, Speechify was built to solve a real accessibility problem. Today, it's evolved into a comprehensive reading platform that helps anyone consume more content in less time. Whether you're a student tackling textbooks, a professional processing reports, or someone who simply prefers listening to reading, Speechify transforms any text into high-quality audio.

The platform works seamlessly across devices with apps for iOS, Android, Chrome, Safari, and Mac. You can scan physical documents with OCR, import PDFs and ebooks, or have web pages read aloud. With celebrity voice options, adjustable speeds up to 5x, and intelligent text highlighting, Speechify makes "reading with your ears" both productive and pleasant.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Speechify is the gold standard for text-to-speech reading assistance, and its massive 20+ million user base proves its value. The combination of excellent voice quality, powerful OCR scanning, cross-platform availability, and accessibility features makes it indispensable for students, professionals, and anyone with reading difficulties. While it's not designed for content creation or professional voice generation, it absolutely excels at its core purpose: making reading more accessible and efficient. The free tier is generous enough to evaluate properly, and the premium subscription is worth every penny for regular users. The mobile apps are particularly outstanding, making it easy to consume content on the go. For students with dyslexia, professionals drowning in documents, or anyone wanting to read more in less time, Speechify is an essential tool that delivers real productivity gains and accessibility improvements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Speechify free? +
Yes, Speechify offers a free tier with standard voices and basic features. However, premium features like high-quality voices, unlimited listening, OCR scanning, and faster speeds require a Premium subscription starting at $11.58/month. There's also a free trial period to test premium features.
Does Speechify work offline? +
Yes, with a Premium subscription, you can download documents and listen to them offline on mobile devices. This is perfect for flights, commutes through areas with poor connectivity, or conserving data usage. The offline mode works seamlessly once content is downloaded.
How accurate is the OCR scanning? +
Speechify's OCR is highly accurate for printed text, typically achieving 95%+ accuracy with clear documents. It handles books, magazines, and printed papers excellently. Handwriting recognition varies depending on legibility - neat handwriting works well, but messy or cursive writing may have lower accuracy.
Can Speechify help with dyslexia? +
Absolutely. Speechify was actually founded by someone with dyslexia and includes specific features for dyslexic readers: adjustable reading speeds, synchronized text highlighting to follow along, dyslexia-friendly fonts, and the ability to listen instead of read. Many users with dyslexia report it's transformative for their reading ability.
What are the celebrity voices? +
Speechify offers AI-generated celebrity voices including Snoop Dogg, Gwyneth Paltrow, and others through its Premium+ plan. These are AI recreations trained to sound like the celebrities, created with their permission. They add a fun, engaging element to listening, though they cost extra beyond the standard Premium subscription.
Which platforms does Speechify support? +
Speechify is available on iOS (iPhone/iPad), Android, Mac (desktop app), Chrome (browser extension), Safari (browser extension), and Microsoft Edge. All platforms sync seamlessly, so you can start listening on one device and continue on another. The mobile apps are particularly polished and feature-rich.
