Overview

Morpholio Board is a professional mood board and design visualization app built specifically for architects, interior designers, and creative professionals. Founded in New York in 2012 and designed primarily for iPad with Apple Pencil support, Morpholio Board has evolved from a pure mood board tool into an AI-enhanced design visualization platform that helps designers curate materials, products, and inspirations into compelling client-ready presentations.

Morpholio Board's AI features help designers discover and curate design elements: AI-powered product search, image recognition for identifying materials and furnishings from inspiration photos, and smart color palette extraction. The result is a design workflow that connects inspiration gathering, product curation, and client presentation in one tool optimized for the touch-and-pencil interface of iPad.

In 2026, Morpholio Board has added AI room visualization features, allowing designers to place curated products into room renders directly in the app — bridging mood board creation and space visualization for interior design pitches.

Key Features

Professional Mood Boards

Create stunning, professionally formatted mood boards and design presentations on iPad. Drag-and-drop layout with grid snapping and alignment tools for polished results.

AI Product Discovery

AI-powered search identifies products, materials, and furnishings similar to inspiration images. Find purchasable items matching your design vision in seconds.

AR Product Placement

Place furniture and design elements in actual spaces using iPad AR. Visualize how products look in real rooms before purchasing or committing to a design direction.

Shoppable Design Boards

Link products directly to purchase sources on mood boards. Clients can buy items directly from your presentation, creating a seamless path from design to purchase.

AI Color Extraction

Extract color palettes from inspiration images automatically. Build cohesive color stories from your design references with AI-powered palette generation.

Sketch Integration

Apple Pencil sketching directly on mood boards. Annotate, sketch over images, and add hand-drawn elements to digital boards for a personalized presentation feel.

How It Compares

Morpholio Board vs Canva for Mood Boards

Canva is a general-purpose design tool. Morpholio Board is purpose-built for architectural and interior design professionals, with AR, product sourcing, and design-specific templates that Canva lacks. For professional design presentations, Morpholio Board delivers far more relevant capability.

Morpholio Board vs Pinterest

Pinterest is inspiration gathering. Morpholio Board is professional presentation and product curation with AI assistance. They serve different purposes — Pinterest typically feeds into Morpholio Board as a source of inspiration to be refined into client-ready presentations.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Morpholio Board has earned its place as the essential iPad app for interior designers and architects who need to create beautiful, professional client presentations. The combination of drag-and-drop mood board creation, AI product discovery, AR placement, and shoppable boards creates a workflow that's genuinely better than assembling presentations in PowerPoint or Canva. For design professionals who work on iPad, Morpholio Board is a category-defining tool that makes the design pitch process faster and more impressive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Morpholio Board only for iPad?+
Morpholio Board is primarily designed for iPad with Apple Pencil and is at its best on that platform. Limited functionality is available on iPhone. A desktop/web version exists but with fewer features. iPad users get the full experience including AR placement and Apple Pencil sketch integration.
How does the AR product placement work?+
Using the iPad camera, Morpholio Board's AR feature lets you point at a room and digitally place furniture, art, and design elements into the live view. You can see how a specific sofa or lamp would look in an actual client space before purchasing or committing to a design direction.
Can clients shop directly from Morpholio Board?+
Yes — products added to mood boards can be linked to purchase sources (retailers, manufacturers). Boards can be shared with clients as interactive presentations where they can browse products and click through to purchase. This creates a seamless path from design selection to purchasing.
Is Morpholio Board suitable for architecture students?+
Yes — Morpholio Board is popular in architecture and interior design schools for portfolio development, design presentations, and concept communication. The free tier offers core functionality, and student pricing may be available directly through the app.
