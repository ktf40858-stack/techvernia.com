Overview

Snaptrude is a cloud-native collaborative BIM (Building Information Modeling) and architectural design platform that uses AI to accelerate the early stages of architectural design. Founded in Bangalore in 2019 with offices in New York, Snaptrude enables architects and designers to collaborate in real-time on 3D building models — similar to how Figma transformed UI design with cloud collaboration.

Traditional architectural design software like Revit and AutoCAD is powerful but desktop-bound, slow to collaborate on, and has steep learning curves. Snaptrude offers a browser-based alternative for conceptual and schematic design, with AI tools that automatically generate floor plans, suggest space arrangements, and optimize building layouts based on program requirements. Files export to Revit for detailed design work.

In 2026, Snaptrude has grown to serve thousands of architecture firms globally, offering a modern collaborative design environment that integrates AI-generated design suggestions with BIM data intelligence. The Revit integration means architects can start in Snaptrude and move to Revit for construction documentation without losing data.

Key Features

Real-Time Collaborative Design

Multiple architects work on the same 3D model simultaneously in a browser. Version control, comments, and live cursors — Figma-style collaboration for architecture.

AI Floor Plan Generation

AI generates floor plan options from program inputs (room types, areas, relationships). Rapidly explore multiple layout configurations without starting from scratch.

BIM-Intelligent Modeling

Every element has BIM data — walls know their materials, rooms know their areas, components have specs. Design and data stay connected throughout the workflow.

Revit Interoperability

Export full BIM models to Revit for detailed design and construction documentation. Round-trip workflow between Snaptrude and Revit maintains data continuity.

Site Context Import

Import site data, topography, and context from maps for site-responsive design. Ground your designs in real site conditions from the beginning.

Area & Program Analysis

Real-time floor area calculations, program compliance checks, and space efficiency analysis. Make data-driven design decisions throughout the conceptual phase.

How It Compares

Snaptrude vs Revit (Autodesk)

Revit is the industry standard for detailed BIM. Snaptrude is optimized for early design and collaboration. They're complementary — Snaptrude for concept, Revit for documentation. The Revit export feature makes this pairing seamless.

Snaptrude vs ArchiCAD

ArchiCAD is desktop BIM software. Snaptrude is cloud-native with real-time collaboration. Snaptrude is more modern in collaboration; ArchiCAD has deeper documentation tools. For teams that prioritize distributed collaboration, Snaptrude has a meaningful edge.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Snaptrude has correctly identified that the architectural design industry needs a Figma-moment — cloud-native collaboration that makes early-stage design faster and more collaborative. The AI floor plan generation and real-time multi-user design represent genuine productivity gains over traditional workflows. For architecture firms struggling with the collaboration limitations of desktop BIM software, Snaptrude offers a compelling modern alternative for the conceptual phases where design exploration matters most.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Snaptrude replace Revit?+
No — Snaptrude is designed to complement Revit, not replace it. Snaptrude excels at early-stage collaborative design; Revit handles detailed design, documentation, and construction administration. The Revit export feature allows seamless handoff between the two workflows.
Can non-architects use Snaptrude?+
Yes — Snaptrude's interface is more accessible than traditional BIM software, making it usable by developers, project managers, and clients for reviewing and understanding architectural designs. Architects use it for design; stakeholders use it for review and feedback.
How does the AI floor plan generation work?+
You input your program requirements — room types, target areas, adjacency preferences — and Snaptrude's AI generates multiple floor plan layout options for a given building footprint. You can then select, modify, and iterate on AI-generated layouts rather than starting from scratch.
Is Snaptrude suitable for large architectural firms?+
Yes — Snaptrude's collaboration features, version control, and project management capabilities scale to large team use. Enterprise pricing and dedicated support are available. Large firms often use Snaptrude for early project phases while maintaining Revit for production documentation.
