Overview

Veras AI, developed by Evolve Lab and launched in 2023, is an AI-powered rendering plugin that transforms basic 3D models into photorealistic visualizations. It integrates directly with SketchUp and Revit, bringing the power of generative AI to architects' existing workflows without requiring a complete software change.

The platform has quickly gained popularity among over 30,000 designers who appreciate the ability to create presentation-quality renders in minutes rather than hours. Unlike traditional rendering engines that require extensive material setup and lighting configuration, Veras AI uses machine learning to intelligently interpret your model and generate realistic images.

What makes Veras AI particularly valuable is its understanding of architectural context. The AI recognizes building elements, applies appropriate materials, adds realistic entourage, and creates atmospheric lighting—all based on your design intent and simple text prompts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Veras work with other 3D software besides SketchUp and Revit?
Currently, Veras AI is only available as plugins for SketchUp and Revit. Support for other platforms like Rhino and ArchiCAD is being considered based on user demand.
How does Veras AI compare to traditional render engines?
Veras is much faster and easier to use than traditional engines like V-Ray or Corona, but offers less precise control. It's ideal for concept renders and client presentations, while traditional engines are better for final marketing materials requiring exact specifications.
Can I use Veras renders for commercial projects?
Yes, all subscription plans include commercial usage rights. You can use Veras renders in client presentations, marketing materials, and portfolio work without additional licensing fees.
What happens if I exceed my monthly render limit?
On Starter and Professional plans, you'll need to upgrade or wait until the next billing cycle. The Studio plan offers unlimited renders. You can also purchase additional render credits if needed.
How detailed does my model need to be?
Veras works best with simple massing models that clearly define spaces and major elements. The AI fills in realistic details, so you don't need fully detailed models—basic geometry with clear intent is sufficient.
Can I control the level of photorealism?
Yes, Veras offers multiple rendering styles from sketch-like to highly photorealistic. You can use text prompts to guide the AI toward your desired aesthetic, and adjust materials and lighting as needed.
