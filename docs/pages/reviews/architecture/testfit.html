TestFit Review

Real estate feasibility and site planning platform

★★★★★4.8/5
Updated: October 2026
10 min read
Developers & Architects

Overview

TestFit, Inc. makes a real estate feasibility platform. Its pitch is to test any site, understand the constraints and communicate the deal starting from an AI-generated plan that you can edit “down to the last parking stall”.

The product line has two solvers. Site Solver uses generative design for site planning across multifamily, single-family, industrial, data center, retail, hotel and parking typologies. Parking Solver generates surface and structured parking layouts. Add-ons include Site Intelligence (zoning and environmental data, 3D terrain with cut and fill, power and utilities), Pro Forma (a live pro forma synced to the design, with your own Excel template) and MCP Connection, which lets an AI assistant edit site plans through MCP.

Plans can be pushed to Revit or exported to AutoCAD, SketchUp, Excel and PDF, and TestFit generates takeoffs for parking, infrastructure and earthwork. Pricing is published: Parking Solver is billed monthly, while Site Solver and Site Solver Portfolio are annual contracts. TestFit says more than 6,200 users work with the platform.

Generative design is Site Solver only

TestFit’s pricing FAQ states that generative design is only available with the Site Solver subscription; Parking Solver covers parking automation and 2D/3D massing.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Prices published on the website
  • Parking Solver includes unlimited users
  • Pro forma stays synced with the layout
  • Wide range of typologies, from multifamily to data centers
  • Revit and SketchUp integration in all plans
  • Automatic takeoffs for parking, infrastructure and earthwork

Disadvantages

  • No free plan or self-serve trial listed; the pricing page offers demos
  • Generative design requires Site Solver, from $15,000 per year
  • Add-ons raise the Parking Solver bill ($150, $170 and $100 per month)
  • Some data layers (parcels, flood zones, zoning) are not available in every country
  • Support and training are in English only, during U.S. business hours

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does TestFit cost?
Parking Solver is $195 per month billed monthly. Site Solver starts at $15,000 per year and Site Solver Portfolio at $20,000 per year, both billed annually.
Which plan includes generative design?
Only Site Solver (and Site Solver Portfolio, which includes it).
Is there a free trial?
The pricing page does not list one. It offers Buy Now for Parking Solver and demos for Site Solver.
Does TestFit work outside the United States?
Yes, with the same core features, but some data layers such as parcels, flood zones, wetlands or zoning may not be available. Support and training are in English during U.S. business hours (9:00 AM–5:00 PM CST).
Does it integrate with Revit and SketchUp?
Yes, in all plans. TestFit also exports to AutoCAD, Excel and PDF.
How do I upgrade my plan?
TestFit asks customers to email sales@testfit.io.
