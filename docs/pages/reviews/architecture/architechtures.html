Architechtures Review

Generative AI design for residential developments

★★★★★4.4/5
Updated: October 2026
10 min read
Architects & Developers

Overview

Architechtures is a cloud-based, generative AI building design platform from SmartScapes Studio S.L. It focuses on residential developments and is used by architects and real estate developers for feasibility studies, conceptual design and schematic design.

You enter design criteria, such as minimum and maximum net areas for each room and typology, minimum room dimensions, heights and vertical circulation, and model above- and below-ground volumes in 2D and 3D. The system then generates, in real time, the layout that best fits those parameters, which you can adjust manually. The result is a BIM model navigable online, with unit program, gross and net floor areas, urban parameter compliance and quantity takeoffs to which you can add unit costs.

Other tools include below-grade parking generation, import of context and topography from OpenStreetMap, and presets for design and cost parameters. Designs download as XLSX, DXF and IFC. Plans are priced in euros: Pro, Business and a custom Enterprise tier.

Still on the roadmap

Environmental analysis and fuller collaboration features (chat, multiple editors, notifications) are listed as “coming soon”. Today, designs can be shared with colleagues in view or edit mode; viewers only need a free account.

Key Features

Design Criteria Input

Areas per room and typology, room dimensions, heights and vertical circulation.

AI-Generated Layouts

Real-time generation of the solution that best fits the criteria, editable by hand.

Real-Time BIM Model

Navigable online, with floor areas, unit program and compliance data.

Below-Grade Parking

Instant underground parking layouts from your contour, ramps and lanes.

OpenStreetMap and Topography

Import buildings, roads, terrain and vegetation; buildings adapt to the terrain.

Takeoffs and Costs

Quantity takeoffs with unit costs; cost and financial analysis on Business.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • 7-day free trial on the Pro plan
  • Unlimited IFC, DXF and XLSX downloads
  • Live areas, unit program and compliance data
  • Underground parking and topography handled in the same model
  • Shared designs can be viewed with a free account

Disadvantages

  • Focused on residential developments
  • Pro is billed annually only
  • Environmental analysis and multi-editor collaboration are not available yet
  • Pro support is by email, with replies within 5 working days
  • Cost and financial analysis require the Business plan

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Architechtures do?
It generates residential building designs in real time from your design criteria and returns a BIM model with areas, unit program and takeoffs.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. The Pro plan has a 7-day free trial.
Which file formats can I download?
XLSX, DXF and IFC, with unlimited downloads on Pro.
How much does it cost?
Pro is €41 per month billed yearly (€492). Business is €246 per month billed yearly (€2,952). Enterprise is custom.
Which plan includes cost analysis?
Business adds design cost analysis, project financial analysis and a design comparison dashboard.
Do people I share a design with need a plan?
No. They only need to register for a free account to view a shared design.
