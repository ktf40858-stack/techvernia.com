Overview

Obsidian Smart Connections is one of the most highly-rated plugins in the Obsidian community — a free, open-source plugin that transforms Obsidian's knowledge graph from a manual link system into an AI-powered semantic search and connection engine. Built by independent developer Brian Petro, the plugin uses vector embeddings to compute the semantic similarity between all notes in your vault and surfaces the most conceptually related notes in a persistent sidebar panel — without requiring you to manually tag, link, or organize anything.

The core innovation is shifting from keyword matching to semantic understanding. Traditional Obsidian search finds notes that contain the exact words you type. Smart Connections finds notes that are about the same concepts, ideas, or topics — even if they use completely different vocabulary. This is particularly valuable for writers, researchers, academics, and thinkers with large vaults (thousands of notes) where related ideas are spread across time and context, and manual connections would take hours to maintain.

In 2026, Smart Connections has evolved beyond passive connection surfacing to include an AI chat interface ("Smart Chat") that lets you query your entire vault in natural language, asking questions like "What did I write about attention mechanisms?" or "Find all my notes related to stoicism and decision-making." The plugin supports multiple embedding providers — including local models (via Ollama) for users who need complete data privacy — making it one of the most privacy-conscious AI tools in the personal knowledge management space.

Key Features

Semantic Similarity Panel

A persistent sidebar panel that automatically updates as you switch notes, showing the most semantically similar notes in your vault ranked by relevance score — surfacing connections you'd never discover through manual linking or tag browsing.

Smart Chat (Vault Q&A)

Chat with your vault using natural language. Smart Chat retrieves relevant note excerpts using vector search, then uses an LLM (OpenAI or local) to synthesize an answer from your own content — like ChatGPT trained on only your knowledge base.

Local AI Support (Ollama)

Run embeddings and chat entirely on-device using Ollama and open-source models like Llama 3 or Mistral — no data ever leaves your machine. Essential for users handling sensitive professional, medical, legal, or personal notes.

Block-Level Embeddings

Embeds at the paragraph/block level rather than the entire note — enabling precise, contextual matching when a single note contains multiple different ideas. A relevant paragraph can surface a connection even when the overall note is only tangentially related.

Automatic Link Suggestions

When writing a new note, Smart Connections suggests existing notes and blocks that are semantically related to what you're currently writing — prompting you to create explicit Obsidian links to related content you might not have thought to cross-reference.

Multi-Provider Embedding

Supports multiple embedding providers: OpenAI (cloud, highest quality), Anthropic Claude (cloud), Ollama with local models (private), and Google Gemini. Switch providers without losing your vault's connection data — embeddings are cached locally.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Free and open-source — no subscription fees for the core plugin
  • Local AI option (Ollama) provides complete data privacy
  • Automatically discovers connections without manual tagging or linking
  • Smart Chat makes querying large vaults conversational and fast
  • Block-level embeddings are more precise than whole-note approaches
  • Works offline once embeddings are generated locally

Disadvantages

  • Requires Obsidian — not useful without an existing note-taking practice on the platform
  • Initial indexing of large vaults (1,000+ notes) can be time-consuming and incur API costs
  • OpenAI API key required for cloud embeddings — ongoing cost for active users
  • Local model quality (Ollama) noticeably lower than OpenAI embeddings for semantic precision
  • Plugin is community-maintained — not backed by a company with support guarantees

Best Use Cases

Smart Connections Excels At:

  • Researchers and academics with hundreds of notes on overlapping topics
  • Writers building interconnected knowledge bases for non-fiction work
  • Developers using Obsidian for technical documentation and personal wikis
  • Anyone practicing zettelkasten or atomic notes methodology
  • Knowledge workers wanting to "chat with their notes" without copy-pasting into ChatGPT

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • New Obsidian users with fewer than 100 notes — the value scales with vault size
  • Teams needing collaborative knowledge management (Obsidian is single-user by default)
  • Users unwilling to set up API keys or local models — requires some technical comfort
  • Users who prefer Notion, Roam, or Logseq (Smart Connections is Obsidian-only)

How It Compares

Smart Connections vs Notion AI

Notion AI adds AI writing and summarization to Notion's collaborative workspace but does not offer semantic note discovery — it won't surface connections between related pages automatically. Smart Connections is purely a connection and retrieval tool for Obsidian, not a writing assistant. If your primary need is surfacing related knowledge across a large note vault, Smart Connections does something Notion AI doesn't. If you need team collaboration and document management, Notion is the better ecosystem.

Smart Connections vs Mem.ai

Mem.ai is a standalone note-taking app with built-in AI organization that surfaces related notes automatically — similar to what Smart Connections does, but as a complete app rather than a plugin. Smart Connections wins if you're already invested in Obsidian's ecosystem and want local data control; Mem.ai wins if you want a frictionless out-of-the-box experience without managing plugins or API keys.

Smart Connections vs Manual Obsidian Linking

Obsidian's native backlinks, tags, and graph view are powerful tools for intentionally structured knowledge. Smart Connections complements rather than replaces them — it discovers connections you didn't know existed, while manual links capture explicit relationships you intend to record. Power Obsidian users typically use both: Smart Connections for discovery, manual links for structure.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Obsidian Smart Connections is the best free AI enhancement available for Obsidian users, and it's not close. If you have a vault with hundreds or thousands of notes, the semantic similarity panel alone will surface connections you'd never find manually — and the Smart Chat feature turns your accumulated notes into a searchable, conversational knowledge base. The cost is essentially zero (especially with the Ollama local option), and the quality of connections with OpenAI embeddings is genuinely impressive on well-written vaults. The only real barrier is adoption: this tool rewards users who already have a mature Obsidian practice. If that's you, installing Smart Connections is one of the highest-leverage things you can do to get more value from the knowledge you've already captured.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my note data private when using Smart Connections?+
It depends on which embedding provider you choose. If you use OpenAI or Google Gemini for embeddings, your note content is sent to those APIs for processing — subject to their data policies. If you use Ollama with a local model, all processing happens on your own machine and no data ever leaves it. For users with sensitive personal, medical, legal, or professional notes, the local Ollama option provides complete privacy at the cost of somewhat lower semantic precision. The plugin is transparent about what data it sends where.
How much does it cost to embed a large vault?+
Using OpenAI's text-embedding-3-small model (the most common choice), embedding typically costs $0.02 per 1,000 tokens. A typical note with ~200 words is about 300 tokens. For a vault of 1,000 notes, initial embedding costs around $0.06–0.30 — essentially free for most users. For a very large vault of 10,000 notes, expect $0.60–3.00 for initial indexing. Ongoing costs are minimal because only new and modified notes need re-embedding. The plugin shows you estimated costs before embedding so there are no surprises.
Does Smart Connections work on mobile (iOS/Android)?+
Smart Connections works on Obsidian Mobile (iOS and Android) in limited form. The connection panel functions normally for viewing related notes. Smart Chat also works on mobile. However, initial indexing of a large vault is best done on desktop due to processing time and battery considerations. Once the vault is indexed, the embedding data syncs with your vault files (if you use iCloud or Obsidian Sync), so mobile access works with the existing embeddings without re-processing on the phone.
How is Smart Connections different from Obsidian's built-in graph view?+
Obsidian's graph view shows explicit links — connections you manually created with [[wikilinks]] or tags. It only knows about relationships you intentionally built. Smart Connections uses AI embeddings to find semantic relationships regardless of whether you linked the notes — it discovers the connections that exist in the content even when you never thought to link them. The two are complementary: graph view shows your intentional structure; Smart Connections reveals the latent conceptual structure across your entire vault.
