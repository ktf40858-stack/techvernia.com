What is Read AI?

Read AI is an AI meeting intelligence platform that differentiates itself from competitors through its emphasis on meeting analytics and behavioral insights. Beyond transcription and summaries, Read AI generates an engagement score for every meeting — measuring participant attention, talk-time distribution, sentiment, and meeting quality — helping teams understand not just what was said, but how effectively the meeting ran.

Founded in 2021, Read AI works across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Its target audience spans customer success teams who want to measure customer engagement in calls, sales teams tracking prospect sentiment, and organizational leaders wanting aggregate meeting health data across their companies.

In 2026, Read AI has added AI-powered meeting preparation (generates suggested questions and talking points before each meeting based on context), a team analytics dashboard, and an Ask Read chatbot that lets you query any past meeting with natural language questions.

Key Features

Engagement Scores

Read AI's signature feature: every meeting receives a score (0-100) measuring overall engagement, individual participant engagement, and talk-time balance. The score is generated from voice activity patterns, filler word frequency, participation distribution, and meeting length vs. productive content ratio. While not a perfect measure of meeting quality, the engagement score surfaces patterns — consistently low-scoring meetings are often genuinely problematic in structure or relevance.

AI Meeting Summaries & Transcription

Real-time transcription with speaker identification in 20+ languages. Summaries include key topics, decisions, and action items — structured similarly to tl;dv and Fellow but with additional context from the engagement analysis. Read AI's summaries include which participants were most engaged during which topics, adding a dimension competitors lack.

Sentiment Analysis

Read AI analyzes the emotional tone of conversation segments throughout a meeting, flagging moments of high enthusiasm, confusion, or concern. For sales teams, this provides signals about prospect interest levels. For customer success, it helps identify moments where customers expressed frustration or uncertainty that might need follow-up attention.

Ask Read (AI Chat Interface)

Query your entire meeting history with natural language: "What questions has this prospect asked across all our calls?" or "What were the main action items from last week's product review?" The AI searches and synthesizes across your recording library, making your meeting history into a searchable knowledge base.

Team Analytics Dashboard

Aggregate meeting analytics for managers and leaders: average engagement scores by team, meeting frequency per person, talk-time distribution trends, and meeting cost estimates (based on attendee salaries and meeting duration). This organizational view helps leaders identify teams with poor meeting culture and quantify the cost of inefficient meetings.

Pricing

  • Free: 5 meetings/month, basic summaries and transcription, engagement scores. Very limited for regular use.
  • Pro ($19.75/mo): Unlimited meetings, full analytics, sentiment analysis, Ask Read, CRM integration, all export options.
  • Enterprise ($29.75/user/mo): Team analytics dashboard, SSO, custom data retention, dedicated support, advanced security.

At $19.75/month, Read AI sits in a middle price point — more expensive than tl;dv's free tier or Fellow's $6/user, but less than enterprise tools. The value justification depends on how heavily you weight the analytics features vs. basic transcription.

Final Verdict

Read AI occupies a distinctive niche in the meeting AI space: it's the best choice for teams and leaders who want to understand their meeting culture and participant behavior, not just capture what was said. The engagement scoring system, sentiment analysis, and team analytics dashboard provide genuinely actionable organizational insights that competitors don't offer at this price point.

For pure transcription quality or action item tracking, tl;dv and Fellow are stronger choices respectively. But for customer success teams measuring client engagement, sales teams analyzing prospect sentiment, and organizational leaders wanting to improve meeting culture with data — Read AI delivers a unique and valuable perspective on how your team meets.

Best for: Customer success teams, sales organizations, and leaders wanting data-driven insight into meeting quality and participant engagement.
