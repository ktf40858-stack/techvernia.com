What is tl;dv?

tl;dv (too long; didn't view) is an AI meeting intelligence platform that automatically joins, records, transcribes, and summarizes video meetings on Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Founded in 2020 in Cologne, Germany, tl;dv has become one of the most popular meeting AI tools for sales teams, product managers, and remote-first companies who need to capture and act on meeting insights at scale.

What sets tl;dv apart from simple transcription tools is its focus on extractability: you can clip specific moments from recordings, create highlight reels, push summaries to Salesforce or HubSpot, and even query your entire library of past meetings with an AI chat interface. It's built for teams that treat meeting recordings as a searchable knowledge base, not just archive footage.

In 2026, tl;dv has added multi-meeting AI reports (synthesize insights across multiple meetings), coaching mode for sales reps, and deeper integrations with project management tools like Notion, Jira, and Linear.

Key Features

Automatic Recording & Transcription

tl;dv's AI bot joins meetings automatically (or on demand) and produces real-time transcriptions in 30+ languages with speaker identification. Transcription accuracy is among the best in the category — consistently above 95% for clear English audio, and better than most competitors for accented speech and technical terminology.

AI Meeting Summaries

After each meeting, tl;dv generates a structured summary: key topics discussed, decisions made, action items with owner attribution, and follow-up questions. Summaries are formatted for immediate sharing in Slack, email, or project management tools. The AI correctly identifies and separates multiple agenda topics even in unstructured conversations.

Moment Clipping & Highlight Reels

Mark any moment during or after a meeting and tl;dv creates shareable video clips. Sales teams use this to share customer objections or product feedback clips with their team. PMs use it to capture feature requests directly from customer voice. Clips include the transcript context and are shareable with a single link.

CRM Integration

Push meeting summaries, action items, and key insights directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive. For sales teams, this eliminates manual CRM data entry after customer calls — summaries auto-populate the contact record with call notes, next steps, and sentiment signals. This is tl;dv's strongest differentiator for B2B sales workflows.

AI Meeting Reports (Multi-Meeting)

A 2025 feature: query your entire meeting library with natural language. "What feature requests came up most frequently in Q1 customer calls?" or "What are the main objections prospects raise in the first discovery call?" The AI synthesizes patterns across dozens or hundreds of meetings — turning your recording archive into a strategic research tool.
