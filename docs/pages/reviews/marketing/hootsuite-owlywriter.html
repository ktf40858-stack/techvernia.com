What is Hootsuite OwlyWriter AI?

OwlyWriter AI is Hootsuite's built-in AI content generation feature, available to all paid Hootsuite subscribers. Rather than a standalone tool, it's deeply integrated into Hootsuite's social media management platform — you can generate content and schedule it to publish across 10+ networks without leaving the Hootsuite dashboard.

Hootsuite itself is one of the oldest and most established social media management platforms, serving 18 million users and thousands of enterprise clients. OwlyWriter leverages this legacy: it's trained on social media best practices and understands platform-specific content requirements across Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, and more.

OwlyWriter can generate posts from scratch, repurpose existing content (turn a blog URL into social posts), generate caption ideas from hashtags, and suggest posts based on trending topics in your industry.

Key Features

Post Generation from URL or Topic

Input a URL (blog post, product page, news article) or topic, and OwlyWriter generates platform-optimized posts for all your connected social accounts. It automatically adjusts tone, length, and format per platform — LinkedIn posts are longer and professional, Twitter/X concise, Instagram caption-focused with hashtag suggestions.

Content Repurposing

Paste a long-form blog post or article and OwlyWriter extracts key points to create a series of social posts. This workflow — long-form to multi-platform social — is one of the most time-consuming tasks for content teams, and OwlyWriter handles it well.

Caption Ideas & Hashtag Suggestions

For image-heavy workflows, OwlyWriter generates caption options from a brief description of the image and suggests relevant hashtags with reach data. The hashtag suggestions tend to be solid for mid-tier tags (better than generic top-100 hashtags), though niche topics produce less precise results.

Best Time to Post (AI-Powered)

Hootsuite's AI analyzes your account's historical engagement data to recommend optimal posting times per platform. Combined with OwlyWriter's content generation, this creates a near-automated content workflow: generate → optimize timing → schedule → publish.

✅ Pros

  • Fully integrated with Hootsuite's scheduling and analytics
  • No separate tool to manage — everything in one dashboard
  • Repurposes URLs/blogs into social posts automatically
  • Supports 10+ social networks
  • 30-day free trial (generous for testing)
  • Enterprise-grade team collaboration and approvals
  • Best time to post powered by your real data

❌ Cons

  • Very expensive — $99/mo minimum for Professional
  • AI content quality not superior to standalone tools
  • Overkill for solo creators or small teams
  • Hootsuite's overall UX has grown complex over time
  • OwlyWriter limited without a paid Hootsuite plan
  • No image generation (text content only)

Pricing

  • Professional ($99/mo): 1 user, 10 social accounts, full OwlyWriter access, scheduling, analytics.
  • Team ($249/mo): 3 users, 20 accounts, team collaboration, content approval workflows.
  • Business ($739/mo): 5 users, 35 accounts, advanced analytics, custom roles.
  • Enterprise (custom): Unlimited users/accounts, SSO, API, dedicated support.

The pricing reflects Hootsuite's enterprise positioning. For most small businesses and solo creators, the cost is difficult to justify for AI writing alone — the value proposition is the full Hootsuite ecosystem. If you're already paying for Hootsuite, OwlyWriter is a strong free add-on.

Final Verdict

Hootsuite OwlyWriter AI is a solid AI content generation feature that benefits enormously from being integrated into Hootsuite's comprehensive social media management platform. If your team is already in the Hootsuite ecosystem, OwlyWriter adds meaningful productivity without adding another tool to your stack.

As a standalone reason to subscribe to Hootsuite, however, the value proposition is weaker — dedicated tools like Predis.ai or Buffer AI offer comparable AI writing at a fraction of the cost. The pricing makes OwlyWriter a recommendation primarily for existing Hootsuite users and enterprise teams that need the full suite of management, analytics, and collaboration features.

Best for: Existing Hootsuite customers, enterprise social media teams, agencies managing multiple large brand accounts.
