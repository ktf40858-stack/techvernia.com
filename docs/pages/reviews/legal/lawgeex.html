Overview

LawGeex automates contract review. It uses patented AI to review and redline legal documents against policies you define in a digital playbook, then returns ready-to-sign redlined contracts in Microsoft Word.

It is built for legal and business teams that handle a steady flow of routine contracts. Contracts can be submitted by email or through integrations with CLM and CRM systems.

LawGeex now also promotes Superlegal, which it calls its new generative AI contract review platform, on a separate website.

Key Features

Digital Playbook

Define your contract review guidelines once; the platform applies them to every contract.

Automated Redlining

AI reviews and redlines documents against your predefined policies.

Microsoft Word Delivery

Reviewed contracts come back redlined and ready to sign in Word.

Analytics

Data on contract review to inform legal policies and negotiation.

Email and Integrations

Submit contracts by email or connect LawGeex to CLM and CRM systems.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Consistent, policy-driven review
  • Output arrives in familiar Microsoft Word
  • Email submission keeps adoption simple
  • Analytics help refine legal policies
  • Patented review technology

Disadvantages

  • No published pricing
  • No free trial; only a free demo
  • Homepage names no specific security certifications
  • Focus now split with the newer Superlegal product
  • Contract review only, no research or litigation tools

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based; contact sales. LawGeex offers a free, tailored demo and lists no prices or trial.

Savings figures on the LawGeex site (such as 80% time saved, or 209% ROI from a Forrester Total Economic Impact study) are vendor-reported.

Best Use Cases

LawGeex Excels For:

  • In-house teams processing NDAs and routine agreements
  • Procurement and sales contract queues
  • Organizations that already have clear review policies

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Bespoke deal diligence across large data rooms
  • Litigation or e-discovery
  • Teams without a defined contract playbook

Comparison

LawGeex vs Competitors
