Overview

Clio Duo is the AI assistant embedded natively inside Clio, the world's leading legal practice management platform used by over 150,000 law firms globally. Launched in 2024, Clio Duo integrates directly into the workflows that lawyers already use every day — matter management, billing, client communications, document drafting — bringing AI assistance without requiring lawyers to switch between tools or learn new systems.

What sets Clio Duo apart from standalone legal AI products is its deep context: because Duo lives inside Clio, it has access to a firm's actual matter history, time entries, documents, and client records. This means that when a lawyer asks Duo to summarize a case or draft a client update, the AI can ground its response in real case data rather than generic knowledge. The result is AI assistance that is immediately practical and firmly connected to the firm's actual work.

In 2026, Clio Duo has expanded its capabilities significantly, adding document drafting from templates, intelligent billing narrative suggestions, and proactive matter health alerts. For the 150,000+ firms already on Clio, Duo represents a frictionless path to AI-augmented legal practice — no new vendor, no data migration, no new login.

Key Features

AI Case Summaries

Automatically summarizes matter history, key dates, outstanding tasks, and recent activity. Lawyers can onboard to any file instantly without reading through hundreds of notes and emails.

Client Communication Drafts

Drafts client update emails, status letters, and engagement correspondence grounded in actual matter data. Lawyers review and send — reducing hours of writing to minutes.

Billing Narrative Suggestions

Generates clear, detailed billing narratives from time entry descriptions. Reduces write-offs caused by vague billing and improves client invoice acceptance.

Matter Health Alerts

Proactively surfaces matters with approaching deadlines, stalled activity, or overdue invoices. Helps firms avoid missed deadlines and revenue leakage.

Document Drafting from Templates

Uses firm templates and matter context to draft first versions of common legal documents: retainer agreements, demand letters, court forms, and correspondence.

Natural Language Matter Search

Lawyers ask questions in plain English — "What matters are we handling for Acme Corp?" or "Which files have no activity in 30 days?" — and get instant structured answers.

How It Compares

Clio Duo vs Harvey AI

Harvey is a deep legal AI platform focused on legal reasoning, research, and drafting at the highest complexity level. Clio Duo is focused on practice management efficiency — billing, summaries, communications. They target different problems; many firms use both.

Clio Duo vs Lexis+ AI

Lexis+ AI is a legal research tool. Clio Duo is a practice management AI. Research and case management are complementary; Clio Duo wins on workflow integration while Lexis+ AI wins on research depth.

Clio Duo vs Standalone AI Tools

Clio Duo's advantage is context — it knows your matters, your clients, your billing. Generic AI tools lack this firm-specific context and require lawyers to manually provide it each time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a separate subscription for Clio Duo?+
Clio Duo is included with certain Clio subscription tiers (Boutique and above). It is not a fully separate product — you access it as part of your Clio plan. Some advanced Duo features may require higher tier plans. Check Clio's current pricing page for the exact feature breakdown by tier.
Is Clio Duo secure and compliant for legal data?+
Yes — Clio Duo operates within Clio's existing security infrastructure, which includes SOC 2 Type II compliance, data encryption at rest and in transit, and regular third-party security audits. Clio has served law firms for over 15 years and has strong trust credentials in the legal technology space.
Can Clio Duo do legal research?+
Clio Duo is not a legal research tool — it does not search case law, statutes, or legal databases. Its AI is focused on practice management tasks: summarizing your matters, drafting communications, and improving billing. For legal research, tools like Lexis+ AI, Westlaw Precision, or Harvey AI are more appropriate.
Which Clio plan includes the full Duo AI features?+
As of 2026, full Clio Duo functionality is available on Clio's Boutique and Elite tiers. The Starter tier includes limited AI features. Clio updates its plans regularly, so check clio.com/duo for the current breakdown. Existing Clio customers should contact their account manager to understand their upgrade path to full Duo access.
