Overview

Darrow AI is a legal intelligence platform that discovers litigation opportunities by analyzing public data, regulatory filings, news, and social media to identify patterns of harm that may form the basis of class action or mass tort litigation. Founded in 2020 in Tel Aviv, Darrow is uniquely focused on the plaintiff side of litigation — helping law firms identify cases before potential clients know to look for legal representation.

The platform's AI continuously analyzes millions of data points to find patterns that indicate mass harm: product defects, data breaches, environmental contamination, workplace violations, and consumer fraud. When the AI identifies a pattern, it surfaces it to plaintiff law firms with the supporting evidence, potential class size estimates, and harm analysis. This transforms case origination from a reactive (waiting for clients to call) to proactive (identifying harm before clients know to seek lawyers) business model.

In 2026, Darrow has partnered with hundreds of plaintiff law firms across the US and is expanding internationally. The platform's ability to identify class action opportunities has created significant business value for the contingency fee law firm model, where case selection is critical to economic viability.

Key Features

Harm Pattern Detection

AI monitors public data sources for patterns indicating mass harm: product defects in complaints data, data breaches in regulatory filings, environmental violations in EPA records.

Class Action Intelligence

Estimates potential class sizes, harm severity, and litigation viability for identified opportunities. Helps firms decide which cases to pursue.

Evidence Aggregation

Automatically gathers and organizes supporting evidence from public sources: court filings, regulatory documents, news, social media, and consumer complaint databases.

Competitive Intelligence

Tracks which law firms are filing similar cases, helps identify underserved litigation opportunities, and monitors case outcomes.

Client Matching

Identifies potential plaintiffs from public information and helps firms connect with class members at scale.

Case Portfolio Analytics

Analytics dashboard for tracking the firm's case pipeline, litigation trends, and portfolio performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Darrow AI useful for defense law firms?+
No — Darrow's focus is specifically on identifying plaintiff litigation opportunities and harm patterns. Defense firms would not benefit from its case discovery features. Darrow is exclusively useful for plaintiff-side and contingency law practices.
How does Darrow find class action opportunities?+
Darrow's AI monitors millions of public data sources including FDA complaint databases, SEC filings, EPA violations, court records, news, and social media. When it identifies patterns suggesting mass harm (e.g., a product defect pattern across consumer complaints), it surfaces the opportunity to plaintiff firms with supporting evidence and case viability analysis.
Is it ethically permitted to proactively contact potential plaintiffs?+
Legal ethics rules around client solicitation vary significantly by jurisdiction. Darrow provides the intelligence; law firms must conduct outreach in compliance with their jurisdiction's Rules of Professional Conduct. Most states permit informational outreach to potential class members under certain conditions.
How accurate are Darrow's class size estimates?+
Darrow uses available public data to estimate potential class sizes, which are inherently approximations. Firms use Darrow's estimates as an initial filter for case viability, not as definitive figures — actual class certification and damages calculations require subsequent legal work.
