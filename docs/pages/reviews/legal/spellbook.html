Overview

Spellbook is an AI contract drafting and review tool that works natively inside Microsoft Word — where lawyers already spend the majority of their time. Founded in 2022 in Toronto, Spellbook was built on GPT-4 and fine-tuned on a large corpus of contracts to provide AI assistance that feels like having a highly skilled contract attorney reviewing your document in real time. The Microsoft Word integration is a key differentiator: lawyers don't need to leave their primary working environment, upload documents to a separate platform, or change their workflow.

Spellbook's AI understands standard commercial contract provisions, identifies missing clauses, flags unfavorable terms, suggests market-standard language, and can draft entire sections from a brief description. The tool is trained to understand contract context — it knows that a limitation of liability clause in a software licensing agreement has different market norms than one in a construction contract.

In 2026, Spellbook has expanded to cover more contract types, added a negotiation assistance feature that suggests counter-proposals for flagged terms, and improved its understanding of jurisdiction-specific standards. The platform is widely used by in-house legal teams and law firms handling high volumes of commercial contracts.

Key Features

Microsoft Word Native

AI drafting and review sidebar directly inside Word. No copy-pasting, no browser switching — AI assistance where lawyers actually work.

Contract Drafting

Draft entire contracts or specific clauses from a brief description. Generates standard commercial language appropriate for the contract type and jurisdiction.

Risk Flagging

Automatically identifies missing provisions, unfavorable terms, and non-market-standard language. Highlights risks with suggested improvements.

Negotiation Assistance

Suggests counter-proposals for flagged terms based on market norms. Helps lawyers understand what's reasonable to push back on.

Clause Library

Access to standard market clauses for common provisions. Insert proven language without drafting from scratch.

GPT-4 Underlying Model

Powered by GPT-4 with legal fine-tuning. Combines frontier language model capability with domain-specific legal knowledge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Spellbook work in Google Docs or other platforms?+
Spellbook is designed specifically for Microsoft Word. There is no native Google Docs integration — Word users get the best experience. Lawyers who primarily use Google Docs may find other platforms more suitable.
What types of contracts does Spellbook handle best?+
Spellbook excels at commercial contracts: NDAs, MSAs, SaaS agreements, employment contracts, vendor agreements, M&A documents, and term sheets. It has strong coverage of standard commercial contract types used in business transactions.
Can Spellbook draft an entire contract from scratch?+
Yes — given a brief description of the deal terms and contract type, Spellbook can generate a full contract draft with standard provisions. Lawyers typically review and customize the output, but the drafting time savings are substantial.
Is Spellbook's AI advice as reliable as a lawyer's review?+
Spellbook provides AI-assisted review that catches common issues and suggests improvements, but it is not a substitute for qualified legal advice. All output should be reviewed by a licensed attorney, particularly for high-stakes transactions or jurisdiction-specific requirements.
