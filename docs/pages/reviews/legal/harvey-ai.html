Overview

Harvey is AI software for legal and professional services. It was founded in 2022 in San Francisco by Winston Weinberg, a former litigator, and Gabriel Pereyra, a former research scientist at Google DeepMind and Meta.

It is sold to law firms and in-house legal departments. Harvey says more than 3,500 legal organizations use it, including more than 80 AmLaw 100 firms, across 85+ countries.

The platform combines agents that run legal work end to end, Vault for bulk document analysis, Knowledge for legal, regulatory and tax research, and Spaces for work shared with clients and co-counsel. In September 2026 Harvey announced a $550 million raise at a $15.5 billion valuation.

Key Features

Agents

Purpose-built agents handle complex legal tasks end to end, with each task routed to the right agent.

Vault

Store, organize and bulk-analyze legal documents.

Knowledge

Research legal, regulatory and tax questions.

Spaces

Shared workspaces for firms, clients and co-counsel, with ethical walls.

Contract Intelligence

Contract review, negotiation support and portfolio insights.

Command Center

Usage analytics and peer benchmarking for firm leadership.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Broad platform covering research, documents and contracts
  • Widely adopted by large law firms
  • SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 27701 and ISO 42001
  • Does not train on customer data by default
  • SAML SSO, audit logs and IP allow-listing
  • Regional hosting in the US, EU/Switzerland and Australia

Disadvantages

  • No published pricing
  • Sold by demo only, with no self-serve sign-up
  • Aimed at large firms and enterprise legal teams
  • No free trial mentioned on the website
  • Fast-changing product line means features shift often

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based; contact sales. Harvey's website lists no prices or trial; the only call to action is a demo request.

Best Use Cases

Harvey Excels For:

  • Large law firms rolling out AI across practice groups
  • In-house legal departments at large companies
  • Firms that collaborate with clients and co-counsel in shared workspaces
  • Teams tracking regulatory change (Horizon Scanning)

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Solo and small firms that want self-serve pricing
  • Tax-only research teams
  • Large-scale e-discovery review

Comparison

Harvey vs Competitors

  • vs CoCounsel: CoCounsel is grounded in Thomson Reuters' Westlaw and Practical Law content and shows pricing to small firms. Harvey is sold by demo and focuses on its own agents and workspaces.
  • vs Luminance: Luminance specializes in contract intelligence for enterprises. Harvey covers contracts as one part of a broader legal platform.
  • vs Everlaw: Everlaw is an e-discovery platform and lists Harvey among the AI tools that can connect to it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Harvey?

AI software for legal and professional services, with agents, document analysis (Vault), research (Knowledge) and shared workspaces (Spaces).

Who uses Harvey?

Law firms and in-house legal teams. Harvey says 3,500+ legal organizations use it, including 80+ AmLaw 100 firms.

How much does Harvey cost?

Harvey does not publish pricing. Request a demo for a quote.

Does Harvey train on my data?

Harvey says it never trains on customer data by default, and requires zero data retention from its model providers.

What security certifications does Harvey have?

Harvey lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 27701 and ISO 42001, with annual SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audits.
