Overview

Lexis+ AI is LexisNexis' generative AI legal research platform, built on top of the world's largest legal content database with over 12 billion documents. Unlike general-purpose AI that frequently hallucinates legal citations, Lexis+ AI is designed with hallucination prevention as a core requirement — every cited case, statute, or regulation is verified against the actual LexisNexis database. This makes it fundamentally more trustworthy for legal practice than general AI tools.

LexisNexis has over 50 years of legal data and relationships with courts, publishers, and legal information providers worldwide. This unmatched data foundation means Lexis+ AI has access to primary legal sources — actual case law, statutes, regulatory materials — that other AI systems either don't have or have only in incomplete form. The AI can research complex legal questions, draft legal documents, analyze contracts, and extract information from large document sets, all anchored to verified legal sources.

In 2026, Lexis+ AI has become the AI product of choice for established law firms and legal departments that need the reliability and citation accuracy that LexisNexis' brand promises. The platform integrates into existing LexisNexis subscriptions, making adoption straightforward for the millions of lawyers already using LexisNexis for research.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Lexis+ AI make up legal citations (hallucinate)?+
Lexis+ AI is specifically designed to prevent hallucination of legal citations. Every case, statute, and regulation cited by the AI is verified against the actual LexisNexis database. This is a fundamental safety requirement for legal AI that most general-purpose AI tools fail.
Is Lexis+ AI only for US law?+
Lexis+ AI has comprehensive coverage of US federal and state law, with strong coverage of UK, Canadian, Australian, and EU law as well. International coverage is broader than many competitors, though US law remains the most comprehensive.
Do I need a new subscription to access Lexis+ AI?+
Lexis+ AI is being integrated into existing Lexis+ subscriptions, so many LexisNexis customers can access AI features without a separate subscription. Access levels depend on your current subscription tier. New customers need a Lexis+ subscription.
How does Lexis+ AI verify that cited cases are still good law?+
Lexis+ AI integrates with Shepard's Citations, LexisNexis' citation service that tracks whether cases have been overturned, criticized, or distinguished by subsequent decisions. Any case cited as authority is automatically checked against Shepard's.
