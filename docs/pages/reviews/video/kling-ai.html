Overview

Kling AI is the video and image generation model and creative studio developed by Kuaishou Technology, the Chinese short-video company. It turns text prompts, images and references into video clips and images, and is available at kling.ai on the web, iOS, Android and desktop.

The Kling Video 3.0 series, launched in February 2026, generates up to 15 seconds per clip with multi-shot control, native audio with character-level lip-sync for several speakers and languages, readable on-screen text and consistent characters across shots. Kling Image 3.0 produces native 2K and 4K images. In October 2026 kling.ai began promoting Kling 4.0 as its newest model.

Beyond generation, Kling offers Image to Video, Motion Control, an agent-based Canvas for scripting and assembling projects, MCP and CLI access for batch generation, and a separate API platform for developers. Usage is paid in credits through monthly memberships.

Key Features

Text and Image to Video

Generate clips from prompts, images or references with the Video 3.0 series.

Multi-Shot Generation

Up to 15 seconds per generation with several shots and dynamic camera movement.

Native Audio and Lip-Sync

Audio generated with the video, with lip-sync for multiple characters, languages and accents.

4K Output

Native 4K video (30 credits per second on Video 3.0) and 2K/4K images with Image 3.0.

Motion Control

Transfer motion to characters for controlled animation.

Canvas, MCP and API

Agent-based Canvas, MCP/CLI batch generation and a developer API platform.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • High-end video generation with native audio and lip-sync
  • Multi-shot clips with consistent characters
  • Native 4K video and image output
  • Free Basic plan to try the interface
  • Available on web, mobile and desktop, plus an API

Disadvantages

  • Free Basic plan has no monthly credits and is not for commercial use
  • 1080p, watermark removal and video extension require a paid membership
  • Membership credits expire after one month
  • Intro prices apply only to the first month; renewal costs more
  • 4K generation consumes credits quickly

Best Use Cases

Kling AI Excels At:

  • Short cinematic clips for ads, music videos and social media
  • Scenes with dialogue that need lip-synced audio
  • Creators who want 4K output
  • Developers integrating video generation through an API

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Long videos in a single generation
  • Editing existing footage on a timeline
  • Commercial use on the free plan

How It Compares

Kling AI vs Runway

Both develop their own generation models. Kling’s Video 3.0 emphasizes native audio, lip-sync and multi-shot clips up to 15 seconds.

Kling AI vs Google Veo

Veo is Google’s video model, available through Google products; Kling is sold directly by Kuaishou through kling.ai memberships and an API.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Kling AI is one of the strongest AI video generators available, with multi-shot clips, native audio and 4K output, and a fast release pace. The free tier is mainly for trying it out, and heavy 4K use requires the larger memberships.
