Overview

Lumen5 is a browser-based video creation tool aimed at marketers, communications teams and L&D teams. It turns written material, such as a blog URL, an uploaded PDF, pasted text or a short idea, into a finished video with scenes, captions, stock media and optional AI narration. It assembles videos from media and text rather than generating footage.

Its AI Script Composer reads the source content and proposes several script options with different angles and tones. Once you pick one, Lumen5 pairs each scene with media, timing and motion to produce a rough cut, which you can then edit: text, visuals, colors, layouts, aspect ratio and pacing. A Talking Head feature adds presenter clips with callouts and cutaways.

Paid plans add AI voiceover minutes, voice cloning, AI avatars and, from the Starter plan, 1080p export and a library of 50M+ stock photos and videos. Lumen5 sells Free, Basic, Starter and Pro plans billed monthly or yearly, plus an annual Team plan with collaboration, approvals and single sign-on.

Key Features

Blog, PDF and Idea to Video

Paste a URL, upload a PDF, type text or describe an idea; Lumen5 extracts the key points and builds the video.

AI Script Composer

Generates several script variations and lets you adjust tone, length and structure before the video is built.

AI Voiceover

1000+ AI voices on paid plans; voice minutes are only counted when you publish.

Brand Kits and Watermarks

Custom colors and fonts from Basic, a customizable brand kit from Starter, custom watermarks on Pro.

Stock Media Library

50M+ stock photos and videos from Starter; Shutterstock editorial content on Pro.

Team Workflow

Team plan adds collaboration, a video approval process, SSO and a customer success manager.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Free plan, no payment details to sign up
  • Repurposes existing articles and PDFs quickly
  • Script options generated before any editing
  • Annual billing cuts prices by about a quarter (Basic $19 vs $29)
  • AI voice minutes are clearly stated per plan

Disadvantages

  • Free and Basic plans export in 720p only
  • Free videos are limited to 2 minutes and end with a Lumen5 outro
  • Large stock library and brand kit start at Starter ($59/month billed yearly)
  • Collaboration, approvals and SSO only on the Team plan
  • Builds videos from stock media and text; it does not generate footage

Best Use Cases

Lumen5 Works Well For:

  • Turning blog posts and articles into social videos
  • Repurposing PDFs and whitepapers into short explainers
  • Internal communications and announcements
  • Training modules built by L&D teams without editors

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Cinematic or generated footage (generative tools such as Runway fit better)
  • Editing long camera recordings
  • Budgets that need 1080p export on the cheapest plan

How It Compares

Lumen5 vs Pictory

Both turn scripts and URLs into stock-media videos. Pictory adds text-based editing of existing recordings and ElevenLabs voices; Lumen5 has a permanent free plan, while Pictory offers a 14-day trial.

Lumen5 vs Synthesia

Synthesia builds videos around AI avatar presenters with 140+ languages and voices. Lumen5 builds text-and-media videos from existing content.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Lumen5 is a practical way for marketing and communications teams to turn written content into on-brand videos without an editor. The free plan is enough to test the workflow; most teams will want Starter for 1080p, the stock library and a brand kit. It is not a generative video tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. The Free plan allows 5 videos a month, up to 2 minutes each, with a Lumen5 branded outro and 720p export.

No. Lumen5 says a credit card is only required when you upgrade to a paid plan.

Can Lumen5 turn a blog post into a video?+
Yes. Paste the URL, and the AI Script Composer generates script options; Lumen5 then pairs scenes with media, timing and motion.
Does Lumen5 include AI voiceover?+
Yes. Paid plans include 1000+ AI voices with monthly voice minutes (20 on Basic, 60 on Starter, 180 on Pro), counted only when you publish.
