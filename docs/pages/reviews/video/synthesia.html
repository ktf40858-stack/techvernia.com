Overview

Synthesia is a London-based company founded in 2017 by Victor Riparbelli, Steffen Tjerrild, Matthias Niessner and Lourdes Agapito. Its platform turns a script into a video presented by an AI avatar, without cameras, studios or actors.

Users choose stock avatars or create personal avatars, pick from 140+ languages and voices, and edit scenes in the browser. AI Dubbing translates existing videos (70+ output languages on self-serve plans, 140+ on Enterprise). Interactive Avatars, Roleplay Sessions and Survey Sessions are aimed at corporate training.

Pricing is credit-based: credits are spent on video creation, dubbing and survey responses. Plans are Basic (free), Starter, Pro and Enterprise, and learner seats for roleplay training cost $29 per learner per month ($25 billed yearly). Synthesia states it is SOC 2 compliant and labels AI-generated content under Article 50 of the EU AI Act.

AI Dubbing

Roleplay and Surveys

Interactive roleplay and survey sessions for training, with paid learner seats.

Enterprise Controls

SAML/SSO, live collaboration, brand kits, SCORM export and a dedicated CSM on Enterprise.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Free plan to test the editor and avatars
  • Very wide language coverage (140+)
  • Built for training: roleplay, surveys, SCORM export
  • SOC 2 compliance stated
  • Annual billing lowers Starter from $29 to $18/month

Disadvantages

  • Free videos carry a Synthesia watermark
  • Brand kits, SCORM export and SSO are Enterprise only
  • Starter has one editor seat (plus 3 guests)
  • Credits make minutes per plan approximate
  • Avatar-presented format; it does not generate scenes or footage

Best Use Cases

Synthesia Works Well For:

  • Corporate training and onboarding videos
  • Internal communications in several languages
  • Localizing existing videos with AI dubbing
  • L&D teams that need SCORM export (Enterprise)

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Cinematic or generated footage (Runway fits better)
  • Teams needing several editors on a small budget
  • Brand-kit or SSO requirements below Enterprise

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Synthesia is one of the most complete avatar video platforms for business, with 140+ languages, dubbing and training features such as roleplay and SCORM export. The free plan is useful for testing; Starter suits individuals, while companies needing brand kits, SSO or SCORM will end up on Enterprise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Synthesia free?+
Yes. The Basic plan is free and includes 500 credits a month (about 10 minutes of video or dubbing), 9 stock avatars and a watermark.
How many languages does Synthesia support?+
140+ languages and voices for narration. Dubbing supports 70+ output languages on self-serve plans and 140+ on Enterprise.
Can I create an avatar of myself?+
Yes, with personal avatars. Enterprise includes unlimited personal avatars.
Is Synthesia secure?+
Synthesia states it is SOC 2 compliant; Enterprise adds SAML/SSO.
Who founded Synthesia?+
Victor Riparbelli, Steffen Tjerrild, Matthias Niessner and Lourdes Agapito, in 2017. The company is based in London.
