Overview

Runway is an AI research company and creative platform that builds generative models for video, images and audio. It describes its mission as "building AI to simulate the world through merging art and science" and lists offices in New York, San Francisco, Seattle, London, Paris, Tel Aviv and Tokyo. It is not an AI avatar or spokesperson tool.

Its flagship video model, Gen-4.5, was released on December 1, 2025 and generates video from text prompts. Aleph, released on July 25, 2025, edits existing footage: adding or removing elements, changing how objects look, generating new camera angles, and adjusting style or lighting. Gen-4 Image handles still images, and the app also generates music, voice and sound effects.

Everything is paid in credits. Gen-4.5 costs 12 credits per second of video (60 credits for 5 seconds), and the same credit balance can be spent on third-party models listed on Runway's pricing page, including Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0, Nano Banana Pro and Lyria 3. Plans run from Free to Standard, Pro, Max and Enterprise, and developers can reach the models through the Runway Dev platform.

Key Features

Gen-4.5 Video

Runway's state-of-the-art video model for motion quality and prompt adherence, at 12 credits per second.

Aleph Video Editing

Edit real footage: add or remove objects, change appearance, new camera angles, restyle or relight a scene.

Gen-4 Image

Image generation at 1080p (8 credits) or with Gen-4 Image Turbo (2 credits).

Audio Generation

Music, voice and sound effects, including text to speech and Google's Lyria 3 for songs.

Third-Party Models

One credit balance also covers models such as Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0 and Nano Banana Pro.

Developer Access

Runway Dev platform (models, model router, workflows) and Runway MCP on Pro and Max.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • One credit balance covers Runway's own and third-party models
  • Aleph edits existing footage, not only generates new clips
  • Free credits do not expire
  • No watermarks and 4K upscaling from the Standard plan
  • Max plan exports HDR, ProRes and image sequences
  • Annual billing lowers every paid plan by about 20%

Disadvantages

  • Credits go fast: Standard's 625 monthly credits are about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5
  • Monthly credits do not roll over, except one month on Max
  • Free credits are a one-time allowance
  • Project limits on self-serve plans (3, 5 or 10)
  • Voice clones and brand kits start at Pro

Best Use Cases

Runway Works Well For:

  • Concept shots, ads and short creative sequences generated from text
  • Editing or restyling existing footage with Aleph
  • Creators who want several video models under one subscription
  • Developers building on Runway models through the Dev platform

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Presenter or talking-head training videos (an avatar tool such as Synthesia fits better)
  • Long videos on a small budget, given the per-second credit cost
  • Teams that need unused credits to roll over on lower plans

How It Compares

Runway vs Kling AI

Kling 3.0 is listed among the models available inside Runway, so a Runway subscription can cover it. A direct Kling subscription makes sense if Kling is the only model you use.

Runway vs Veo 3

Veo is Google's video model. Runway's draw is a full creative app around several models, with Aleph editing and audio generation on the same credit balance.

Runway vs Synthesia

Different jobs: Synthesia makes avatar-presented business videos from a script, while Runway generates and edits footage.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Runway is a strong choice for generative video work: Gen-4.5 for new shots, Aleph for editing real footage, and one credit balance that also covers third-party models. Watch the credit math, though: Gen-4.5 costs 12 credits per second, so the Standard plan suits experimentation more than production. Pro or Max fit regular use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Runway an AI avatar tool?+
No. Runway builds generative video, image and audio models (Gen-4.5, Aleph, Gen-4 Image). For avatar presenter videos, look at Synthesia or HeyGen.
Is Runway free?+
The Free plan gives 125 one-time credits, which do not expire, and access to a selection of models.
How do Runway credits work?+
Each generation costs credits: Gen-4.5 uses 12 credits per second of video, Aleph 2.0 140 credits per 5 seconds, Gen-4 Image 8 credits. Monthly plan credits reset each cycle; purchased credits never expire.
What is Runway Aleph?+
A video model released on July 25, 2025 that edits an existing video: adding or removing elements, changing objects, generating new camera angles, and adjusting style or lighting.
Does Runway have an API?+
Yes. The Runway Dev platform gives developers access to models, a model router and workflows, and Pro and Max plans include Runway MCP.
