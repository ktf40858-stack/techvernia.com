Overview

InVideo AI, now branded simply as invideo, describes itself as an agentic video editor. It combines an AI editing agent with a multitrack timeline editor, so you can generate a video from a prompt and then refine it by hand.

The AI agent, Agent Two, comes in Lite, Pro and Ultra tiers. Inside the editor, invideo gives access to third-party generative models, including Google Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Kling, Seedance, Hailuo, Wan and Pixverse for video, and Nano Banana Pro, Ideogram, Seedream and Flux Kontext for images, with ElevenLabs and Minimax for audio. invideo also offers AI avatars, voice cloning and text-to-speech tools, and lets users connect external agents such as Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini.

The editor itself includes multiplayer editing with live cursors, plus color, audio and 3D tools on every plan. invideo runs in the browser and has iOS and Android apps. Plans are priced per seat and include a monthly credit allowance used by the AI models.

Key Features

Agent Two

AI editing agent in Lite, Pro and Ultra tiers that drafts and edits videos from prompts.

Timeline Editor

Multitrack editor with color, audio and 3D tools, available on all plans.

Multiplayer Editing

Live cursors and real-time collaboration for up to 5, 10 or 20 seats depending on plan.

Generative Models

Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Kling, Seedance, Nano Banana Pro and other video, image and audio models in one place.

Avatars and Voice

AI avatar generator, voice cloning and text-to-speech tools.

Stock Media

Premium stock footage included in paid plans (45, 150 or 180 items per month by plan).

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Many leading video and image models behind one subscription
  • Real timeline editor, not only prompt-to-video
  • Multiplayer editing with free guest seats on Pro and Ultra
  • Annual plans cost half the monthly price
  • Web plus iOS and Android apps

Disadvantages

  • No free plan is listed on the pricing page
  • Entry plans exclude the premium video models (no Seedance 2.5 on Starter; Basic limited to Agent Two Lite)
  • Unused monthly credits do not roll over
  • Credit cost depends on the model and quality chosen, so output per plan varies
  • Prices are per seat, so team costs scale with headcount

Best Use Cases

InVideo AI Excels At:

  • Creators who want several generative models without separate subscriptions
  • Social and marketing videos drafted by an AI agent, then edited
  • Small teams editing together in real time
  • Mobile editing on iOS and Android

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Users who need a permanent free plan
  • Heavy generators on the entry plan, which lacks the premium models
  • Teams needing predictable cost per finished video

How It Compares

InVideo AI vs Runway

Runway develops its own generation models; invideo aggregates third-party models (Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance) inside a timeline editor with an editing agent.

InVideo AI vs HeyGen

HeyGen specializes in presenter videos with AI avatars and video translation; invideo is a general-purpose editor that also offers avatars among many generative tools.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

invideo has evolved from a template-based maker into an agentic video editor that bundles many of the best generative models with a real timeline and multiplayer editing. It is best value on annual billing, while the entry plan is restrictive and the lack of a free plan makes testing harder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is InVideo AI free?+
invideo’s pricing page lists only paid, per-seat plans; no free plan is shown.
How much does invideo cost?+
It depends on your region. In October 2026 the USD pricing page listed Starter at $20, Plus at $60 and Max at $150 per seat per month, or half that billed annually; the EUR page listed Basic at €9, Pro at €30 and Ultra at €80.
Which AI models can I use in invideo?+
Video models include Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Kling, Seedance, Hailuo, Wan and Pixverse; image models include Nano Banana Pro, Ideogram, Seedream and Flux Kontext. Entry plans are limited to selected models.
Do unused credits roll over?+
No. Credits reset every month on the day you subscribed, including on annual plans.
Can several people edit the same video?+
Yes. Multiplayer editing with live cursors is included on every plan; the EUR plans allow up to 5, 10 or 20 seats, with free guest seats on the higher tiers.
