Overview

Sora is OpenAI's groundbreaking text-to-video AI model, publicly launched in December 2024 and significantly upgraded with Sora 2 in late 2025. It represents one of the most significant advances in generative AI video, capable of producing cinematic-quality clips up to 60 seconds long from simple text prompts or images.

What sets Sora apart from competitors is its exceptional understanding of physics, motion continuity, and cinematic language. The model can simulate complex camera movements, maintain character consistency across scenes, and generate footage that would be difficult to distinguish from real video at first glance. With the Sora 2 update, OpenAI dramatically expanded clip duration from 6 to up to 60 seconds, introduced the "Cameos" feature for inserting real people into scenes, and launched a partnership with Disney for licensed character use.

Sora is accessible through the dedicated sora.com platform, the ChatGPT interface, and via API for developers. It's deeply integrated into the ChatGPT ecosystem, making it a natural choice for the millions of existing ChatGPT users.

Key Features

60-Second Videos

Generate cinematic clips up to 60 seconds — a massive leap from the 6-second limit of v1. Perfect for commercials, short films, and social media content.

Cameos Feature

Insert real people into AI-generated scenes with the Cameos feature. Create personalized video content with realistic human subjects without a camera crew.

Disney Partnership

OpenAI's $1B Disney partnership enables licensed use of Disney, Pixar, Marvel, and Star Wars characters for commercial video creation — a first for AI video.

Text & Image to Video

Generate videos from detailed text prompts or animate any static image into a realistic video clip with natural motion and camera movements.

Physics Simulation

Industry-leading understanding of real-world physics: liquids, fabrics, gravity, collisions, and atmospheric effects are rendered with exceptional realism.

iOS Social App

Dedicated iOS application for sharing and remixing Sora-generated content. Build a creative community around your AI video projects.

Sora Turbo Model

The Turbo variant delivers faster generation speeds for rapid iteration and prototyping, while the standard model maximizes visual quality.

API Access

Full API access for developers at $0.10–$0.50 per second of generated video. Build video generation into your applications and pipelines.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Sora 2 is the most technically impressive AI video generator in 2026. Its 60-second clips, exceptional physics simulation, and the Disney partnership make it uniquely powerful for high-end creative content. If you're already a ChatGPT Plus subscriber, Sora is an obvious addition to your toolkit at no extra cost — making it exceptional value for existing users.

However, the removal of the free tier, EU/UK unavailability, and the absence of audio generation (a gap Veo 3.1 fills) are real limitations. For creators needing audio-synced video, Veo 3.1 is the better choice. For those on tight budgets, Pika Labs offers a free tier with capable results. But for pure cinematic quality and clip length, nothing matches Sora 2 in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sora free to use in 2026?+
No. OpenAI removed Sora's free tier on January 10, 2026. You now need at minimum a ChatGPT Plus subscription ($20/month) to access Sora. ChatGPT Pro ($200/month) unlocks Sora 2 Pro with the highest quality and unlimited generations.
Why is Sora not available in Europe?+
Sora is currently unavailable in the EU and UK due to ongoing regulatory reviews related to the EU AI Act and UK AI regulations. OpenAI has not confirmed a timeline for European availability as of February 2026.
What is the Cameos feature in Sora?+
Cameos is a Sora feature that allows you to insert real people into AI-generated video scenes. You provide a reference image or description of the person, and Sora integrates them realistically into your generated video content. This is powerful for personalized marketing and creative projects.
Can Sora generate videos with audio?+
No — as of February 2026, Sora does not natively generate audio alongside video. You will need to add audio separately using tools like ElevenLabs or Descript. If synchronized audio is critical to your project, consider Google's Veo 3.1, which generates native synchronized audio.
How long does it take to generate a Sora video?+
Generation times vary by prompt complexity and server load. Simple clips typically take 2-5 minutes. Complex 60-second scenes can take up to 10-15 minutes. Pro subscribers receive priority access which reduces wait times significantly during peak hours.
Can I use Sora videos commercially?+
Yes, ChatGPT Plus and Pro plans include commercial usage rights for Sora-generated videos. However, Disney-licensed character content is subject to Disney's specific terms and may have restrictions on commercial use depending on your project type.
