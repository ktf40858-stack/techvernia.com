Overview

Artlist is a music licensing and stock footage platform that has evolved into a comprehensive creative asset marketplace for video creators and filmmakers. Founded in 2016 in Tel Aviv, Artlist pioneered the "annual subscription = unlimited downloads + lifetime commercial license" model that disrupted the per-track music licensing industry. Today over 26 million creators use Artlist for royalty-free music, sound effects, stock footage, and video templates.

What makes Artlist exceptional is its licensing model: one subscription covers unlimited use across all projects forever — no per-video fees, no content ID claims on YouTube, no need to re-license if you renew. This is in stark contrast to platforms like Epidemic Sound where licenses expire. The 450,000+ track library spans every genre, curated by music editors for quality and sync-ability.

In 2026, Artlist has integrated AI-powered tools including smart music search (describe the mood, get curated matches), automatic music-to-video sync, and AI-generated sound effects. The platform has also expanded its AI footage collection, becoming a one-stop shop for video production assets. The acquisition of Motion Array in 2021 added Premiere Pro templates, further cementing Artlist's position as the go-to creative platform for video professionals.

How It Compares

Artlist vs Epidemic Sound

Epidemic Sound has a comparable library but licenses expire when your subscription ends — a major disadvantage for evergreen content. Artlist's lifetime license is superior for long-term creators who want to know their catalog is always protected.

Artlist vs Musicbed

Musicbed offers higher-end curated music (often from indie artists) but at higher per-track pricing. Artlist wins on unlimited use and integrated footage library for creators producing content at volume.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Artlist is the gold standard for video creators who need a reliable, legally safe source of music, footage, and sound effects. The lifetime license model is a genuine competitive advantage — once you license a track, it's yours forever regardless of subscription status. For YouTubers, filmmakers, and social media creators producing regular content, the annual subscription pays for itself after just a few projects. In 2026, the addition of AI music search and AI footage tools makes it even more indispensable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Artlist license really last forever?+
Yes — Artlist's lifetime license means any music you download while subscribed remains licensed for those specific projects even after your subscription ends. This is a key advantage over platforms where licenses expire with your subscription.
Will Artlist music trigger content ID claims on YouTube?+
No — Artlist has agreements with YouTube and major platforms to whitelist their music. Your videos will not receive content ID claims for Artlist-licensed tracks.
What's the difference between Creator and Pro plans?+
Creator covers music and sound effects. Pro adds stock footage (4K video clips). Both include unlimited downloads with lifetime commercial licenses.
How many tracks does Artlist have?+
Over 450,000 curated royalty-free tracks across every genre, plus 100,000+ sound effects. New tracks are added weekly by the editorial team.
