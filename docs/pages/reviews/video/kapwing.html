Overview

Kapwing is an online video editor that runs in the browser with no download. It offers a drag-and-drop timeline for trimming and combining clips and adding overlays, plus an audio editor, a resizer for different social formats and templates, all in a shared workspace where teammates can comment and edit.

Its AI tools handle the repetitive parts of editing: an auto subtitle generator, subtitle translation into 60+ languages, AI dubbing into 40+ languages, text to speech, Clean Audio to remove background noise, Smart Cut to remove silences, and Clip Maker and Repurpose Studio to cut long videos into short social clips. A Video Generator builds a draft video from a text prompt with script, voiceover, visuals and subtitles.

Kapwing has a free plan and paid plans billed per member, monthly or annually, with a monthly allowance of AI credits.

Key Features

Browser Timeline Editor

Trim, combine, overlay and resize videos in the browser without installing anything.

Auto Subtitles and Translation

Generate subtitles automatically and translate them into 60+ languages.

AI Dubbing and Text to Speech

Dub dialogue into 40+ languages and create voiceovers from text; Business adds custom voice clones and lip sync.

Repurposing Tools

Clip Maker and Repurpose Studio turn long videos into short, social-ready clips.

Smart Cut and Clean Audio

Remove silences automatically and clean background noise from audio.

Team Workspace

Shared workspace, Brand Kit and custom fonts for consistent team videos.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Works entirely in the browser
  • Free plan with unlimited watermarked exports
  • Strong subtitle, translation and dubbing tools
  • Collaborative workspace with Brand Kit on Pro
  • Clear per-member pricing with an annual discount

Disadvantages

  • Free exports carry a watermark, are limited to 720p and 4 minutes, and files are stored for 3 days
  • Free includes only 10 one-time AI credits
  • Voice clones and lip sync require Business
  • SAML single sign-on is Enterprise only
  • Exports are capped at 120 minutes on every paid plan

Best Use Cases

Kapwing Excels At:

  • Social media teams repurposing long videos into clips
  • Adding and translating subtitles at scale
  • Marketing teams editing together in the browser
  • Creators on locked-down computers where installing software is not possible

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Long-form productions over 120 minutes
  • Generating fully synthetic AI presenter videos
  • Users who need watermark-free exports for free

How It Compares

Kapwing vs InVideo AI

invideo centers on an AI agent and third-party generative models; Kapwing centers on editing real footage, with strong subtitle, translation and dubbing tools.

Kapwing vs HeyGen

HeyGen generates presenter videos with AI avatars; Kapwing edits the videos you already have.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Kapwing is a practical browser editor for teams that publish a lot of social and marketing video, with some of the most useful AI helpers for subtitles, translation and repurposing. The free plan is fine for testing but watermarked, and voice cloning and lip sync sit on the Business tier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kapwing free?+
Yes. The Free plan allows unlimited exports with a watermark, at 720p and up to 4 minutes each, with 10 one-time AI credits.
How much does Kapwing Pro cost?+
$16 per member per month billed annually ($192/year), or $24 billed monthly. Business is $50 per member per month billed annually or $64 monthly.
Does Kapwing need to be installed?+
No. Kapwing runs in the web browser.
How many languages can Kapwing translate subtitles into?+
Kapwing translates subtitles into 60+ languages and dubs dialogue into 40+ languages.
Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?+
Yes, at any time. Moving from annual to monthly, or from Business to Pro, requires cancelling and re-upgrading at the end of the billing cycle.
