Overview

Topaz Video AI is the professional standard for AI-powered video enhancement. Unlike generative AI video tools that create footage from scratch, Topaz Video AI specializes in making existing video dramatically better — sharper, smoother, cleaner, and higher resolution. It is the go-to software for filmmakers, post-production studios, video archivists, and content creators who need to restore or upscale footage to modern standards.

Developed by Topaz Labs, the same Texas-based company behind the widely respected Topaz Photo AI and DeNoise AI for photography, this application applies similar deep-learning principles to video. The result is a toolkit that can transform 480p archival footage into convincing 4K, remove decades of grain and noise from old VHS tapes, double or quadruple a video's frame rate with AI-generated intermediate frames, and stabilize shaky handheld shots — all in one application running entirely on your local hardware.

What sets Topaz Video AI apart from the competition is the depth and maturity of its AI models. Topaz Labs has been developing and refining these models since 2019, and each major update brings measurable improvements verified by a large community of professional users. The software is trusted by restoration studios working on Hollywood archive material, documentary filmmakers rescuing decades-old footage, indie creators converting old drone shots to modern resolutions, and YouTube channels that rely on consistently sharp output. If you work seriously with video, this tool pays for itself quickly.

Key Features

AI Upscaling

Enhance video resolution from 480p all the way up to 4K or 8K with genuine detail recovery. Topaz's proprietary upscaling models — including Proteus, Artemis, and Gaia — analyze motion and texture to reconstruct fine detail that simpler interpolation methods cannot reproduce. The difference compared to traditional bicubic upscaling is immediately visible.

Frame Interpolation

Increase your video's frame rate from 24fps to 60fps, or from 30fps to 120fps, using AI-generated in-between frames. The APOD and Apollo models analyze motion vectors across multiple frames to synthesize natural-looking intermediate frames, producing buttery smooth slow motion or simply bringing old footage up to modern broadcast standards without judder or ghosting artifacts.

Denoising & Deblur

Remove digital noise, film grain, compression artifacts, and motion blur from video shot in low light or with older equipment. Topaz Video AI's denoising models are trained on massive datasets of clean and degraded footage pairs, giving them the ability to distinguish between genuine image detail and unwanted noise — a distinction that matters enormously when working with precious archival material.

AI Stabilization

Smooth out shaky handheld or drone footage with AI-powered stabilization that goes beyond traditional warp stabilizers. Rather than simply cropping and warping the frame, Topaz analyzes camera motion across the entire clip and applies intelligent compensation that preserves the original field of view as much as possible while eliminating the micro-jitter that makes amateur footage look unprofessional.

Face Recovery

Restore facial detail in old, low-resolution, or compressed footage with a dedicated face enhancement model. This feature is particularly valuable for archival projects where close-up shots of people have degraded over decades of format conversions. The AI sharpens eyes, skin texture, and hair detail without introducing the uncanny artificial smoothing typical of older upscaling approaches.

Batch Processing

Queue entire folders of video files and process them automatically overnight. Each file can have its own set of enhancement settings, and Topaz Video AI will work through the queue using your GPU for maximum throughput. This makes it practical to process entire TV series seasons, documentary archives, or large social media backlogs without babysitting each job manually.

How It Compares

Topaz Video AI vs Topaz Photo AI

Topaz Photo AI is the still-image equivalent of Topaz Video AI, sharing several of the same underlying AI models but optimized for single frames rather than temporal sequences. They are complementary tools rather than competitors. Video AI is better for footage processing and includes frame interpolation and stabilization; Photo AI is the choice for batch-processing RAW or JPEG photographs. Many Topaz Labs users own both, especially photographers who also shoot video. The bundle pricing reflects this overlap.

Topaz Video AI vs DaVinci Resolve AI Upscaling

DaVinci Resolve 19's built-in AI upscaling is free and integrated directly into one of the industry's best color grading NLEs, which is a compelling package. However, in head-to-head quality comparisons, Topaz Video AI consistently produces sharper results with better edge preservation, less haloing, and more natural texture. Resolve's upscaler is a solid "good enough" option for quick jobs; Topaz is the choice when output quality is the primary concern. The two are often used together — edit and grade in Resolve, export for enhancement through Topaz, bring back into Resolve for final output.

Topaz Video AI vs Adobe Premiere AI Upscale

Adobe Premiere Pro's AI upscaling, part of the Enhance Speech and video tools suite, is convenient for Premiere users but falls noticeably short of Topaz's quality level when processing heavily degraded or very low-resolution material. Adobe's solution benefits from deep NLE integration and requires no separate application, making it the right choice for quick, good-enough results within an existing Premiere workflow. For dedicated enhancement work — especially archival restoration or any project where quality is paramount — Topaz Video AI is the stronger tool by a significant margin.

Topaz Video AI vs Runway Video-to-Video

These tools solve fundamentally different problems. Runway's video-to-video features apply generative AI transformations to footage — changing style, adding effects, or re-imagining the scene entirely. Topaz Video AI enhances the original footage without altering its fundamental character. If you need to upscale a wedding video from 2005, you want Topaz. If you need to stylize drone footage to look like a painting, you want Runway. There is no meaningful overlap in their core use cases, and many professionals use both tools in different parts of their pipeline.

Frequently Asked Questions

What GPU do I need to run Topaz Video AI effectively?+
Topaz Video AI runs on any system, but processing speed scales dramatically with GPU capability. For practical professional use, NVIDIA GPUs from the RTX 20-series onwards are recommended, with the RTX 3080 and above providing comfortable render speeds for 4K output. AMD Radeon RX 6000-series and newer cards also provide good performance. Apple Silicon Macs (M1 Pro and later) are fully supported and deliver strong performance through Metal acceleration. Integrated graphics and older discrete GPUs will work but render times will be many times the clip duration, making large projects impractical. Topaz's website publishes benchmark render times for common GPU configurations to help users gauge performance expectations before purchase.
What video file formats does Topaz Video AI support?+
Topaz Video AI supports a broad range of input formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, ProRes (all variants), HEVC/H.265, H.264, AV1, DNxHD, and many legacy formats going back to DV and MPEG-2. The application uses FFmpeg internally for decoding, which provides excellent format coverage. For output, you can export to ProRes 4444, ProRes 422, H.265, H.264, and several other formats. RAW camera formats like BRAW or ARRIRAW are not directly supported and should first be transcoded to a mezzanine format like ProRes before processing. Topaz regularly expands format support in updates.
Does Topaz Video AI work in the cloud, or is it desktop-only?+
Topaz Video AI is a desktop application only and processes all footage locally on your machine. There is no cloud rendering option and no subscription-based cloud service. This is intentional — many of Topaz's professional users work with confidential client footage and need the assurance that their files never leave their own hardware. The desktop-only model also means there are no per-minute processing fees, no upload/download wait times for large files, and no dependency on internet connectivity. For studios with multiple workstations, the license allows installation on up to two machines under a single account.
Can Topaz Video AI upscale footage to 8K, and is it actually useful?+
Yes, Topaz Video AI can upscale footage up to 8K resolution, and the Gaia model in particular is optimized for very high-resolution output. Whether 8K output is practically useful depends on your delivery requirements. Most streaming platforms, broadcast standards, and client deliverables currently require 4K at most, making 4K the most common target resolution. However, 8K upscaling is used in certain archival contexts, large-format digital cinema, and future-proofing workflows where 8K displays are anticipated. The processing time for 8K output is significantly longer than for 4K, and the storage requirements are substantial — a single minute of 8K ProRes 4444 footage can exceed 20GB.
