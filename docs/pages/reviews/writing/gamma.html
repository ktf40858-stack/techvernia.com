Overview

Gamma is the fastest-growing AI presentation and document platform, reaching 100 million users in just a few years and achieving a $2.1 billion valuation with $100M ARR. It fundamentally reimagines how presentations, documents, and webpages are created — using AI to generate complete, beautifully designed content from a simple prompt.

Unlike traditional tools like PowerPoint or Google Slides, Gamma creates fully-formatted, visually stunning presentations in seconds. Its AI understands not just the text, but the layout, visual hierarchy, and design principles needed to make content compelling. This has made it incredibly popular with startups, consultants, educators, and marketing teams.

In 2026, Gamma has evolved well beyond presentations. The launch of Gamma Imagine positions it as a direct competitor to Canva, offering an AI-powered image and design suite. With $100M ARR and explosive growth, Gamma is one of the most significant AI productivity tools of the decade.

How It Compares

Gamma vs PowerPoint

Gamma creates presentations 10x faster with better default design. PowerPoint wins for complex formatting, macros, and enterprise integration. Gamma wins for speed, design quality, and modern web publishing. For most business presentations, Gamma's output is equal or better with a fraction of the effort.

Gamma vs Canva

Canva has a much larger template library and stronger graphic design tools. Gamma is better for presentations and documents specifically. Gamma Imagine is closing the gap on Canva's image creation capabilities. For slide-based content, Gamma's AI generation is a clear advantage over Canva's manual approach.

How good is Gamma's AI for creating presentations?+
