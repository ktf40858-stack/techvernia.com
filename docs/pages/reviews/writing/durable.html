Overview

Durable is a Canadian AI-powered all-in-one business platform that lets you build a professional website in 30 seconds — no coding required. With over 3 million businesses created and a 4.8 Trustpilot rating, Durable is the fastest-growing AI website builder targeting small businesses, freelancers, and local service providers.

Beyond website creation, Durable bundles a complete business suite including CRM, invoicing, and AI-generated content tools. This makes it uniquely positioned as a single platform for micro-businesses to manage their entire digital presence without needing multiple subscriptions.

In 2026, Durable continues to refine its AI capabilities with smarter website customization, better SEO tools, and an expanding marketplace of business integrations. Its strength is making professional digital presence accessible to non-technical business owners within minutes.

Key Features

AI Website Builder

Generate a complete, professional website in 30 seconds from just your business type and location. AI writes all copy, selects images, and structures pages.

Built-in CRM

Manage customer contacts, track interactions, and follow up with leads — all within the same platform as your website.

AI Invoicing

Create and send professional invoices directly from Durable. Track payments and manage billing without a separate accounting tool.

SEO Tools

Custom Domain

How It Compares

Durable vs Wix

Wix offers much more design flexibility and a larger app market, but lacks Durable's AI-first speed and built-in CRM/invoicing. Durable wins for simplicity and small business all-in-one needs. If you need pixel-perfect design control or an extensive plugin ecosystem, Wix is the better choice.

Durable vs Squarespace

Squarespace has superior design templates and e-commerce capabilities. Durable wins on speed and bundled business tools for service businesses. For photographers, creatives, or online stores, Squarespace is stronger. For local service businesses, Durable delivers more value faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I really build a website with Durable?+
Genuinely fast — 30 seconds is accurate for the initial generation. You input your business type and location, and Durable's AI generates a complete site with copy, images, and structure. Customization takes additional time, but the starting point is remarkably complete.
Does Durable handle e-commerce?+
Durable offers basic e-commerce features for simple product selling, but it's not optimized for large catalogs. For serious e-commerce, platforms like Shopify or WooCommerce are better suited.
Can I use my own domain name?+
Yes — all paid plans allow you to connect a custom domain. The free plan uses a Durable subdomain.
