Overview

Copy.ai started as an AI copywriting tool and now presents itself as "The First AI-Native GTM Platform" for sales and marketing teams. It is model-agnostic and gives access to models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google Gemini.

The product has three parts: Chat, a prompting interface for one-off tasks; Workflows, which codify processes and plays (research, content generation, tool integrations); and Agents, which automate targeted tasks with guardrails. Copy.ai highlights a Prospecting Cockpit for account research and outreach drafts, content creation (SEO, thought leadership, social) and inbound lead processing. It lists 2,000+ integrations, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Zapier, Outreach and Salesloft.

Fullcast acquired Copy.ai in October 2025. Pricing starts with the Chat plan for small teams; workflow plans (Growth, Expansion, Scale) are priced per month for large seat counts, and Enterprise is custom.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Chat plan is inexpensive and includes 5 seats
  • Several model providers in one tool
  • Workflows connect AI to CRM and sales tools
  • Unlimited chat words on every plan
  • Enterprise includes guided implementation

Disadvantages

  • Steep jump from $29/month Chat to $1,000/month Growth
  • Workflow credits are capped per tier
  • API access and bulk runs are Enterprise only
  • Free plan terms are not published on the pricing page
  • Roadmap now tied to Fullcast's RevOps platform
