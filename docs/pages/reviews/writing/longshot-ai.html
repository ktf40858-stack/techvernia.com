Longshot AI Overview

Unique positioning: Longshot AI is one of the few AI writing tools that tackles the hallucination problem head-on. Its FactGPT feature checks facts against real-time web searches, attaching citations to every claim. This makes it uniquely valuable for research-heavy industries: journalism, health, finance, and B2B SaaS content.

Founded in 2021 by Raj Shamani and team, Longshot AI was purpose-built for content marketers who need factually reliable long-form content at scale. The team observed that generic AI tools produced confident-sounding but frequently wrong content — a major liability for professional publishers.

Longshot's solution: integrate web search and fact-checking directly into the content generation pipeline. FactGPT (launched in early 2023 and continuously improved through 2025) fetches current data from the web, incorporates it into generated content, and provides citations for every factual claim.

The platform integrates with SurferSEO and Semrush for keyword optimization, making it a compelling choice for content agencies and SEO teams who need both accuracy and search performance.

2025–2026 Update: Longshot AI added a "Custom Fact Library" feature, allowing teams to upload proprietary research, whitepapers, and product documentation. The AI then prioritizes those sources when generating content, ensuring brand-accurate, internally consistent output.

FactGPT — Longshot's Killer Feature

FactGPT is what sets Longshot apart from every other AI writing tool. Here's how it works step by step:

1. Real-Time Web Search

FactGPT searches the live web for information related to your topic before generating content — similar to how a journalist would research an article first.

2. Source Selection

You can manually select which sources to include (e.g., only .gov, .edu, or top-tier publications) or let Longshot choose the best sources automatically.

3. Cited Generation

The AI generates content with inline citations for every factual claim, making it trivial to verify accuracy or remove citations for a cleaner published version.

4. Claim Checker

After generation, you can run any section through the Claim Checker to flag potentially incorrect statements before publishing.

Final Verdict — Is Longshot AI Worth It?

Longshot AI earns its place by solving the most critical problem with AI-generated content: factual accuracy. If you operate in a regulated industry (healthcare, finance, legal) or simply cannot afford to publish wrong information, Longshot's FactGPT feature is not just nice-to-have — it is essential.

The Custom Fact Library addition makes it particularly valuable for enterprises with proprietary research, product documentation, or compliance requirements that need to be reflected in AI-generated content.

Recommended for: Content agencies, B2B SaaS companies, health and finance publishers, research-driven blogs, SEO teams who need accuracy alongside ranking performance.

Not recommended for: Writers who primarily create short-form or creative content. For general marketing copy and social media, Jasper AI or Copy.ai offer a better feature-to-price ratio.
