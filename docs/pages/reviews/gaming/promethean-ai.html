Overview

Promethean AI is an assistant for digital art teams. It indexes a team's creative assets (images, videos, 3D models, animations, PDFs and presentations), builds a searchable metadata library around them and helps artists recombine them into new content through natural-language requests.

It targets professional teams in games, film and animation, outsourcing and architectural visualisation. Assets stay where they are: the company says it never sees, uploads, stores or trains on customers' files, and the shared library can live in a network folder, Dropbox, a MySQL server or S3.

Unreal Engine is the primary integration, with open-source plugins for Unity, 3ds Max, Maya and Blender. A free version for individuals covers asset management, palettes and some AI features; enterprise licensing is handled through sales.

Key Features

Asset management

Syncs asset folders and keeps a searchable library of references and source files, with natural-language search.

AI world building

Helps build scenes from your own assets; the company claims up to 10x faster production.

Palettes

Curated asset sets used for art direction and outsourcing hand-offs.

Editor plugins

Primary Unreal Engine integration, plus open-source plugins for Unity, 3ds Max, Maya and Blender.

Python customisation and API

Tech teams can customise asset types, attributes, data sources and menus in Python, and use an API published on GitHub.

Enterprise controls

SSO with your own access management and MFA, deployment automation and custom model training for enterprise partners.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Assets stay on your own infrastructure; the vendor says it does not upload or train on them
  • Works inside existing tools (Unreal, Unity, Maya, 3ds Max, Blender)
  • Free version for individuals
  • Extensible with Python and an open API
  • Claims over 10,000 users, including PlayStation Studios

Disadvantages

  • Paid and enterprise pricing is not published
  • Unreal is the primary integration; other editor versions may not be tested
  • Aimed at professional art teams, not hobbyists generating images
  • Getting full value requires setting up a shared library and plugins

Pricing

Promethean AI offers a free version for individuals that includes its AI asset management platform, palettes and some AI functionality. Enterprise pricing is quote-based; contact sales.

Best Use Cases

Promethean AI Works Well For:

  • Game and film art teams managing large libraries of assets
  • Outsourcing studios that hand off curated asset palettes
  • Unreal Engine teams building environments from existing kits
  • Studios that cannot upload assets to a third-party cloud

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Solo creators who want text-to-image generation
  • Teams that need published, self-serve paid pricing
  • Pipelines built around engines or editors without a plugin

Comparison

Promethean AI vs Alternatives

Promethean AI focuses on organising and reusing your own assets, unlike generators that create new ones.

  • Scenario: Scenario generates new images, video, 3D and audio from hundreds of cloud models. Promethean keeps your existing assets local and helps you find and recombine them.
  • Ludo.ai: Ludo generates game-ready sprites, models and audio. Promethean is an asset library and world-building assistant for teams with large existing catalogues.
