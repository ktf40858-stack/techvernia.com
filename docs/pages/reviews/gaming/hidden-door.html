Overview

Hidden Door is an interactive storytelling game. You pick a world, add modifier cards to change the tone or plot, create a character, and a Narrator builds a story around your choices. You can play solo or with friends, collect cards and share story moments.

Creators can build their own playable worlds with the Atlas studio, from existing writing or a new idea, publish them and earn a share of revenue when others play. Rights holders can license their worlds; the FAQ mentions worlds such as Pride & Prejudice, The Crow and Call of Cthulhu.

The company was founded in 2020 in New York by Hilary Mason (CEO) and Matt Brandwein. It says human writers create the story beats, characters and hand-drawn art, that language models combine them with player choices, and that it never trains models on partners' works.

Key Features

Narrator-driven stories

A Narrator builds each story around your character and choices, so every playthrough can go differently.

Modifier cards

Cards shift a story's tone or plot, and players unlock and collect them over time.

Atlas world builder

Creators build, refine, publish and analyse playable worlds; the site says a world can take as little as 30 minutes.

Creator revenue share

Creators keep the rights to their IP and earn a share of revenue based on player engagement.

Multiplayer and sharing

Play with friends and share favourite story moments on social media.

API and MCP for partners

Partners can build their own experiences through an API, and creators can work from other AI tools through an MCP.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Free plan with unlimited stories and turns per day
  • Stories built on human-written beats and hand-drawn art
  • Creators keep their IP and share in revenue
  • Clear content policy: designed for players 13+, with parental controls
  • States that it does not train models on partners' works

Disadvantages

  • Subscription price is not shown on the public pricing page
  • Free plan limits you to 5 catalog worlds and one character conversation per day
  • Story-game format, not a general chatbot or game engine
  • Mature worlds contain R-rated content and need parental controls for younger players

Pricing

Hidden Door has a free plan and a paid subscription (monthly or annual):

Free

Unlimited stories and turns per day, up to 5 worlds from the catalog, one character conversation per day, create characters, items, locations and worlds, unlock modifier cards and earn achievements.

Subscription (monthly or annual)

Unlimited character chat sessions and no limits on worlds. The price is not listed on the public pricing page; switching from monthly to annual is prorated.

Accepted partnership applicants get free access to the Atlas creator tool.

Best Use Cases

Hidden Door Works Well For:

  • Players who enjoy fan-fiction style role-play in established story worlds
  • Writers, Dungeon Masters and world-builders who want to turn a setting into a playable world
  • Authors and rights holders who want fans to play in their worlds
  • Groups of friends looking for a shared story game

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Developers who need a game engine or SDK for their own game
  • Players who want an unrestricted, open-ended chatbot
  • Children under 13

Comparison

Hidden Door vs Alternatives

Hidden Door competes with other AI story games:

  • AI Dungeon: AI Dungeon is an open-ended text adventure with a free tier and paid plans from $14.99 to $99.99 per month. Hidden Door is more structured, with curated worlds, cards and illustrated content.
  • Rosebud AI: Rosebud is a tool for creating and hosting games from prompts. Hidden Door is a story game first, with Atlas for building worlds inside its own platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hidden Door?

An interactive storytelling game where you play through stories in different worlds, guided by an AI-assisted Narrator.

Is Hidden Door free?

Yes. The free plan includes unlimited stories and turns per day and up to 5 catalog worlds. A paid subscription removes world limits and adds unlimited character chats.

What age is Hidden Door for?

The platform is designed for players 13+. Worlds marked Mature contain R-rated content, and parental controls are available.

Can I create my own world?

Yes. The Atlas studio lets you build and publish worlds, keep your IP and earn a share of revenue when others play.

How does Hidden Door use AI?

Human writers create story beats, characters and art. Machine learning combines them with world rules and player choices. The company says it does not train models on authors' books or scripts.
