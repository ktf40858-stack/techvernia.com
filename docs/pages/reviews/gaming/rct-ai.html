Overview

rct AI describes itself as "providing AI solutions to the game industry". Its core technology, the Chaos Box algorithm, uses deep reinforcement learning to analyse player input in real time and generate NPC responses and new storylines without hand-written scripts.

The company targets game studios. It offers customised technical support to train Chaos Box on a game's theme, world and NPC characteristics, combined with Game Cloud services, and its site describes plans for an SDK with NPC templates for smaller developers.

rct AI lists Skysaga Capital, Y Combinator and Makers Fund among its backers and announced a Series A-3 round of over $10 million. Its news section also covers web3 projects (an intelligent NFT, a play-to-earn game and the Soularis virtual-being brand). Most of the site's news dates from several years ago, and no pricing is published.

Key Features

Chaos Box engine

Deep reinforcement learning that generates NPC dialogue, interactions and storylines from real-time player input.

Generated story branches

Story branches are generated automatically from a main storyline and custom parameters.

AI players and NPCs

Self-evolving AI players and adaptive NPCs, creatures and environments that react to a broad range of game states.

Studio integration support

rct AI works with studios to set up theme, world and NPC characteristics during training.

Game Cloud services

Cloud services combined with Chaos Box deployment, covering the full life cycle of a game.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Focused on game-specific AI (NPC behaviour and story generation)
  • Reinforcement learning approach rather than scripted dialogue trees
  • Hands-on technical support for studio projects
  • Backed by Y Combinator and Makers Fund

Disadvantages

  • No public pricing, self-serve plan or free trial
  • Website content appears dated; current product status is unclear
  • No public documentation or downloadable SDK found
  • Part of the company's recent work centres on web3 and NFT projects

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based; contact sales. rct AI does not publish plans or prices on its website.

Best Use Cases

rct AI Works Well For:

  • Studios exploring reinforcement learning for NPC behaviour
  • Games that need large numbers of AI players with varied behaviour
  • Projects with budget for a custom, supported integration

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Indie developers who need a self-serve tool today
  • Teams that need published pricing and documentation
  • Projects looking for LLM-based dialogue tools with SDKs available now

Comparison

rct AI vs Alternatives

rct AI takes a reinforcement learning approach to NPCs, while most current tools rely on language models.

  • Charisma.ai: Charisma offers a graph story editor, pay-as-you-go pricing ($5 per 50,000 credits) and Unity and Unreal SDKs you can use today.
  • Inworld AI: Inworld provides voice and LLM inference APIs with published usage pricing, starting with a $0 pay-as-you-go plan.
