Overview

Qure.ai is an Indian AI radiology company specializing in automated analysis of chest X-rays, CT scans, and other medical imaging for disease detection. Founded in Mumbai in 2016, Qure.ai has become one of the world's leading medical imaging AI companies, with its products deployed in over 50 countries. The company is particularly recognized for its work in tuberculosis detection and global health — its AI has screened millions of patients in resource-limited settings where radiologist access is scarce.

Qure.ai's qXR (chest X-ray AI) can detect 28+ abnormalities including tuberculosis, pneumonia, COVID-19, lung cancer, and pleural effusion from a standard chest X-ray in seconds. This capability is transformative in low-resource settings where a single radiologist may be responsible for thousands of patients — AI triage enables limited radiologist time to be focused on highest-priority cases.

In 2026, Qure.ai has expanded to include brain CT analysis (for stroke, hemorrhage), cardiac CT, and mammography AI. The company has received FDA clearance and CE marking for multiple products and partners with WHO, USAID, and major global health organizations on TB elimination programs.

Key Features

Chest X-ray AI (qXR)

Analyzes chest X-rays to detect 28+ findings including TB, pneumonia, lung cancer, COVID-19, cardiomegaly, and pleural effusion. Results in seconds.

TB Detection Specialist

World-class tuberculosis detection AI, validated across diverse populations. Key tool in WHO's TB elimination program across high-burden countries.

Brain CT Analysis

Detects stroke, hemorrhage, and other neurological emergencies from CT scans. Prioritizes urgent cases for immediate radiologist review.

AI Triage System

Automatically prioritizes scan queue by finding severity, enabling radiologists to see the most urgent cases first. Reduces time to treatment for critical patients.

Low-Resource Settings Design

Optimized for deployment in facilities with limited infrastructure. Works with standard X-ray machines and internet-connected tablets.

Population Health Screening

Mass screening programs for TB, lung cancer, and cardiac disease. Efficient analysis of large screening cohorts at population scale.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is Qure.ai's chest X-ray analysis?+
Qure.ai's qXR has been validated in multiple peer-reviewed studies with sensitivity and specificity comparable to experienced radiologists for most findings. For TB detection specifically, the AI has demonstrated sensitivity exceeding 90% in large-scale validation studies across multiple geographies.
Is Qure.ai FDA cleared?+
Yes — Qure.ai has received FDA 510(k) clearance for multiple products including chest X-ray analysis and head CT analysis. It also holds CE marking for European markets and regulatory approvals in India, Brazil, and other countries.
How does Qure.ai work in low-resource settings?+
Qure.ai is designed to work with standard X-ray machines and basic internet connectivity. Images can be analyzed via a cloud-connected tablet or mobile device. The system is specifically optimized for the imaging equipment common in low- and middle-income country health facilities.
Does Qure.ai replace radiologists?+
No — Qure.ai is designed to assist and augment radiologists, not replace them. In settings with limited radiologist access, the AI provides preliminary analysis and triage to ensure critical findings are prioritized. Final diagnostic reporting requires qualified radiologist review.
