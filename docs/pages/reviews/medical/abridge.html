Overview

Abridge is an AI clinical documentation platform that automatically generates structured clinical notes from doctor-patient conversations. Founded in Pittsburgh in 2018 by Dr. Shiv Rao (a cardiologist) and colleagues, Abridge addresses one of healthcare's most significant burnout drivers: documentation burden. Physicians spend an average of 2+ hours per day on EHR documentation, often after hours. Abridge's ambient listening AI converts conversations during appointments into draft clinical notes, reducing this burden by ~40%.

Abridge's Epic integration is a key differentiator — deep integration with Epic, the dominant US EHR system used by most large health systems, means notes flow directly into the EHR workflow without copy-pasting or re-entry. The AI understands medical terminology, clinical context, and documentation standards (SOAP notes, discharge summaries, progress notes) to produce structured, accurate draft notes.

In 2026, Abridge has become one of the most widely adopted ambient AI scribing tools in US healthcare, deployed at UCSF, University of Pittsburgh Medical Center, Kaiser Permanente, and hundreds of other health systems. The company has raised significant funding and is considered one of the most promising healthcare AI companies.

Key Features

Ambient AI Scribing

Records and transcribes doctor-patient conversations, automatically generating structured clinical notes. Physician reviews and approves, dramatically reducing documentation time.

Epic EHR Integration

Deep integration with Epic, the most widely used hospital EHR. Notes populate directly into Epic workflow fields — SOAP notes, discharge summaries, and other note types.

Multi-Language Support

Transcribes and documents conversations in multiple languages. Important for diverse patient populations.

Specialty-Specific Models

AI models trained on specialty-specific documentation standards for cardiology, oncology, emergency medicine, primary care, and other specialties.

Real-time Summary

During the visit, Abridge generates real-time summaries that physicians can reference. Improves visit efficiency and accuracy of documentation.

Patient-Friendly Summary

Generates plain language patient summaries alongside clinical notes. Helps patients understand their visit and follow-up instructions.

How It Compares

Abridge vs Nuance DAX (Microsoft)

Nuance DAX is Abridge's main competitor, backed by Microsoft. Both are leading ambient AI scribing platforms. Abridge is considered by many physicians to produce better note quality; Nuance has stronger Microsoft/Azure integration.

Abridge vs Suki AI

Suki AI is voice-based documentation with AI assistance. Abridge is fully ambient (no push-to-talk). Different interaction models with trade-offs in privacy and physician workflow.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Abridge addresses one of healthcare's most consequential problems — physician documentation burden that contributes directly to burnout and reduces time for patient care. The 40% documentation time reduction is clinically significant, and the Epic integration ensures it works within existing hospital workflows. The physician-led founding team and academic health system partnerships give Abridge credibility that technology-company competitors sometimes lack. For Epic-using health systems committed to reducing documentation burden, Abridge is the gold standard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Abridge require physicians to press a button to start recording?+
Abridge uses ambient listening — it activates during the clinical encounter and captures the full conversation without requiring the physician to toggle recording. This hands-free approach reduces friction compared to push-to-talk systems.
How does Abridge handle patient privacy?+
Abridge requires patient consent before each encounter recording. The system is HIPAA compliant with enterprise-grade security. Audio is processed to generate notes and then handled per the health system's data governance policies.
Does Abridge work with EHR systems other than Epic?+
Abridge's deepest integration is with Epic. It can work with other EHR systems but with less native integration depth. Epic users get the best experience with notes flowing directly into structured EHR fields.
How accurate are Abridge's auto-generated notes?+
Abridge produces draft notes that physicians review and approve before signing. Accuracy is high — physicians typically make minor edits rather than substantive rewrites. The AI understands medical terminology and clinical context well, though physician oversight remains essential for safety.
