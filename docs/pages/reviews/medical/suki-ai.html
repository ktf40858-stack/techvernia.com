Overview

Suki AI is a voice-enabled AI clinical assistant that allows physicians to complete documentation and EHR tasks using natural voice commands. Founded in 2017 by Punit Soni (former product chief at Motorola and Flipkart) and backed by Google, Suki provides a voice-first approach to clinical documentation that integrates with multiple EHR systems. Unlike ambient scribing tools like Abridge, Suki is voice-commanded — physicians speak commands and dictate notes actively rather than passively recording conversations.

Suki's approach gives physicians more control: they dictate when they want to, add context, and command EHR actions by voice (ordering labs, reviewing results, updating problem lists). The AI understands medical terminology and clinical workflows, enabling hands-free EHR navigation that's particularly valuable during procedures or when physicians are away from their keyboard.

In 2026, Suki has expanded its capabilities to include ambient scribing alongside traditional voice dictation, offering flexibility for physicians who want different documentation modes for different clinical scenarios. The platform serves physicians across multiple specialties and EHR platforms, with particular strength in outpatient and clinic settings.

Key Features

Voice-Commanded Documentation

Dictate clinical notes, add to patient charts, and complete documentation by voice. Natural language commands processed with medical AI understanding.

Multi-EHR Integration

Integrates with Epic, Cerner, Athena, and other major EHR systems. Broader EHR compatibility than some competitors.

Ambient Scribing Mode

Passive ambient listening option for full encounter documentation. Complements voice dictation for different clinical scenarios.

EHR Navigation by Voice

Command EHR actions by voice: order labs, review results, update medication lists, add diagnoses. Hands-free EHR interaction.

Specialty-Specific AI

AI trained for multiple specialties including primary care, internal medicine, orthopedics, and mental health. Understands specialty-specific terminology and documentation.

Mobile App

iOS and Android apps allow documentation from mobile devices. Useful for rounding and away-from-desk clinical scenarios.

How It Compares

Suki AI vs Abridge

Abridge is the ambient scribing leader with deeper Epic integration. Suki offers more voice control, multi-EHR support, and flexibility for voice-preference physicians.

Suki AI vs Nuance DAX

Nuance DAX is Microsoft-backed with strong Azure/Microsoft 365 integration. Suki has broader EHR support and a mobile-first approach. Both are capable clinical documentation AI platforms.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Suki AI occupies an important niche in clinical documentation — the voice-first physician who wants control and EHR breadth. Its combination of voice dictation, ambient scribing, EHR commands, and multi-EHR support makes it more versatile than Epic-only tools. The Google backing provides strong AI foundation, and the reported 72% documentation time savings is compelling. For physicians who want active voice engagement rather than passive ambient recording, Suki is the leading choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Suki different from Nuance Dragon Medical?+
Nuance Dragon Medical is primarily a dictation and transcription tool. Suki is an AI clinical assistant — it understands medical context, can navigate EHR systems by voice command, and generates structured notes rather than just transcribing speech. Suki is a more intelligent, integrated system.
Does Suki work with my EHR?+
Suki integrates with Epic, Cerner/Oracle Health, Athenahealth, and several other major EHR systems. The depth of integration varies by EHR — Epic integration is deepest for most ambient scribing tools. Contact Suki to confirm integration depth with your specific EHR.
Can I use Suki on my phone?+
Yes — Suki has iOS and Android mobile apps, allowing physicians to complete documentation on a mobile device. This is useful for rounding, telemedicine, and scenarios where physicians are away from a computer workstation.
Is Suki HIPAA compliant?+
Yes — Suki is HIPAA compliant with Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) available for health system deployments. The platform uses enterprise-grade security and data governance practices appropriate for protected health information.
