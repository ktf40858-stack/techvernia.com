Overview

Paige AI is the first FDA-approved artificial intelligence platform for pathology, revolutionizing cancer detection and diagnosis through advanced deep learning. Developed by Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center and launched commercially in 2018, Paige analyzes whole slide images to assist pathologists in identifying cancerous tissue with unprecedented accuracy. The platform is deployed in over 50 major hospitals and diagnostic laboratories worldwide, processing thousands of cases daily.

What sets Paige apart is its comprehensive training on the world's largest pathology archive, with millions of annotated slides representing diverse patient populations and cancer types. The AI uses convolutional neural networks to analyze tissue at cellular resolution, identifying subtle patterns that may escape human observation. Paige's algorithms have been clinically validated to detect prostate cancer with sensitivity exceeding 99%, reducing false negatives and improving diagnostic consistency.

Paige integrates seamlessly into existing digital pathology workflows, providing real-time analysis as pathologists review cases. The platform supports quality control by flagging areas requiring additional scrutiny, accelerates diagnosis by prioritizing urgent cases, and improves diagnostic accuracy through AI-assisted second opinions. With regulatory approvals in the US and Europe, Paige is setting the standard for AI-powered precision medicine in oncology.

How It Compares

Paige AI vs PathAI

Both are leading AI pathology platforms, but with different focuses. Paige AI has FDA approval for clinical diagnostics and emphasizes cancer detection for routine pathology workflows. PathAI focuses more on research applications, biomarker quantification, and pharma partnerships for clinical trials. Paige's prostate cancer detection is clinically validated and widely deployed, while PathAI offers broader algorithm development tools. Paige is better for diagnostic laboratories seeking FDA-approved cancer detection, while PathAI appeals to research organizations and pharmaceutical companies developing new biomarkers.

Paige AI vs Traditional Pathology

Paige AI augments rather than replaces pathologist expertise, providing AI-assisted analysis that reduces diagnostic errors and improves efficiency. Studies show Paige reduces false negatives by up to 70% compared to unassisted pathologist review. Traditional pathology relies solely on human observation, which can miss small foci of cancer or suffer from fatigue and inter-observer variability. Paige provides consistent, tireless analysis of every cell in every slide. However, pathologist judgment remains essential for integrating AI findings with clinical context, histological patterns, and patient history. The combination of AI precision and human expertise delivers superior patient outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Paige AI FDA approved for clinical diagnosis?+
Yes, Paige AI received FDA approval in 2021 as the first AI-based pathology platform for cancer detection. It is cleared for use as an adjunct to pathologist review for identifying prostate cancer in digitized biopsy slides. The FDA clearance was based on extensive clinical validation showing over 99% sensitivity for cancer detection. Paige is also CE marked for use in Europe and continues to expand regulatory approvals for additional cancer types and applications.
How accurate is Paige AI in detecting cancer?+
Paige AI demonstrates over 99% sensitivity in detecting prostate cancer, meaning it identifies malignant tissue in virtually all positive cases. In clinical validation studies, Paige reduced false negatives by approximately 70% compared to unassisted pathologist review. The platform was trained on millions of whole slide images from diverse patient populations, ensuring robust performance across different tissue types and patient demographics. Accuracy continues to improve through ongoing machine learning updates.
What equipment is needed to use Paige AI?+
Paige AI requires digital pathology infrastructure including whole slide scanners to digitize glass slides and workstations for pathologist review. The Paige software integrates with major scanner vendors including Leica, Philips, and Hamamatsu. Cloud-based deployment requires internet connectivity for AI analysis, though on-premise options are available. Most academic medical centers and large diagnostic laboratories already have the necessary digital pathology infrastructure. Paige provides technical support for integration with existing systems.
Can Paige AI replace pathologists?+
No, Paige AI is designed to assist pathologists, not replace them. It functions as an AI-powered second opinion that highlights suspicious areas and provides diagnostic support. Final diagnosis remains the responsibility of board-certified pathologists who interpret AI findings in the context of patient history, clinical presentation, and other diagnostic information. Paige augments pathologist capabilities by reducing oversight errors, improving consistency, and enabling more efficient review of high-volume cases. The optimal outcome comes from combining AI precision with human expertise.
What types of cancer can Paige detect?+
Paige's FDA-approved algorithm currently focuses on prostate cancer detection in needle biopsy specimens. The company has algorithms in development and clinical validation for breast cancer, colorectal cancer, and other solid tumors. Paige's research pipeline includes extensions to immunohistochemistry analysis, biomarker quantification, and prognostic prediction across multiple cancer types. The platform is continuously expanding its capabilities through partnerships with leading cancer centers and ongoing clinical trials.
How much does Paige AI cost for a hospital?+
Paige AI pricing is customized based on institution size, case volume, deployment model, and specific features required. Typical enterprise implementations involve annual licensing fees plus per-case charges or unlimited usage tiers. Pricing generally starts in the hundreds of thousands per year for hospital pathology departments. The investment includes software licensing, integration support, pathologist training, and ongoing technical support. Many institutions find that efficiency gains, reduced error costs, and improved patient outcomes provide strong ROI. Contact Paige directly for detailed pricing based on your organization's needs.
