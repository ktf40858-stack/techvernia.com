Overview

ClickUp is a work management platform that combines tasks, docs, chat, whiteboards, dashboards and automations in one workspace. ClickUp AI, branded ClickUp Brain², is the AI layer across that workspace: it answers questions from your tasks and docs, writes and edits content, and runs AI agents.

Brain² lets users switch between Claude, GPT and Gemini models, each with access to workspace context. The AI offering also includes Super Agents (named agents such as Strategist, Developer and Visual Designer), AI Fields, an AI Notetaker for calls, Enterprise Search across the workspace, connected apps and the web, and Talk-to-Text voice input. Brain MAX is a desktop app for Windows and Mac.

ClickUp sells five plans: Free Forever, Core, Business, Enterprise and Max. Each paid plan includes a pooled monthly allowance of AI credits, and extra AI Super Credits cost $10 per 10,000. ClickUp's AI page also lists Brain AI at $9 and Everything AI at $28 per user per month.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Free Forever plan with unlimited tasks and members
  • AI answers from your own tasks, docs and chat
  • Choice of Claude, GPT and Gemini models
  • Core plan from $9 per user per month, billed yearly
  • Web, Windows, Mac, iOS and Android apps

Disadvantages

  • AI use is metered by credits; top-ups cost $10 per 10,000
  • Free plan is limited to 60 MB of storage and 5 Spaces
  • The most generous AI limits are on Max, at $100 per user per month
  • SAML SSO, SCIM, audit logs and HIPAA require Enterprise
  • ClickUp's site currently shows more than one set of plans and AI tiers, which makes pricing hard to compare

Best Use Cases

ClickUp AI Excels At:

  • Teams that want tasks, docs and chat in one tool with AI on top
  • Teams already running projects in ClickUp
  • Organizations that want a choice of AI models
  • Teams that rely on automations and dashboards

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Individuals who only need a simple to-do list
  • Teams that need predictable AI costs under heavy use
  • Organizations that need SAML SSO on a small budget

How It Compares

ClickUp AI vs. Alternatives

  • vs. Notion AI: Notion centers on docs, wikis and databases, and full Notion AI comes with the Business plan ($20/member/month, billed yearly).
  • vs. Motion: Motion is built around an AI calendar that auto-schedules tasks; it has no free plan and starts at $19 per seat per month, billed annually.
  • vs. Zapier: Zapier connects more than 9,000 apps; ClickUp's own automations work inside the ClickUp workspace.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

ClickUp AI makes the most sense for teams that already run their work in ClickUp: Brain² answers from your tasks and docs, writes, and runs agents with a choice of models. The Free Forever plan is a real starting point, but AI use is metered by credits and ClickUp currently shows more than one set of plans, so check the AI allowance of the plan you buy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ClickUp AI included in ClickUp plans?+
Every paid plan includes a pooled monthly allowance of AI credits (1K per user on Core, 1.5K on Business). The Free Forever plan includes a Brain² trial of 500 credits per workspace. Extra credits cost $10 per 10,000.
Which AI models does ClickUp Brain use?+
ClickUp lets you switch between Claude, GPT and Gemini models, each with access to your workspace context.
Does ClickUp have a free plan?+
Yes. Free Forever includes unlimited tasks and members, with limits such as 60 MB of storage, 5 Spaces and 100 automations per month.
What are Super Agents?+
Named AI agents, such as Strategist, Developer and Visual Designer, that work with shared team context. They use AI Super Credits.
Which platforms does ClickUp support?+
Web, desktop apps for Windows and Mac (including Brain MAX), and iOS and Android apps.
