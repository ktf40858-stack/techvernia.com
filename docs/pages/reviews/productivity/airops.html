AirOps Overview

Strategic pivot to GEO: AirOps has repositioned from an AI content operations platform to the leading Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform. This is a genuinely new discipline — optimizing content to appear in AI-generated search answers rather than traditional Google blue links. It's an emerging bet, but one of the most important strategic moves in marketing technology for 2026.

AirOps has undergone a significant strategic pivot in 2025–2026 — repositioning from an AI content operations platform to the leading Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform. As AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews increasingly replace traditional search results, AirOps helps brands optimize their content to appear in these AI-generated answers.

This pivot reflects a massive market shift. When someone asks ChatGPT a product question or searches Perplexity for recommendations, the results come from AI models that have their own preference signals — different from Google PageRank. AirOps analyzes what content gets cited by these AI search engines and helps brands create content optimized for AI citation.

Founded in 2020 and originally known for AI-powered content workflows for SEO, AirOps now focuses on this emerging GEO opportunity. It's a genuinely novel category — and AirOps is one of the earliest dedicated platforms for it.

Key Features

GEO Content Analysis

Analyze which of your existing content gets cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Identify gaps and optimization opportunities across AI search surfaces.

AI Citation Optimization

Generate and optimize content specifically structured to be cited by AI search engines based on their preference patterns — a fundamentally new content discipline.

Competitive Intelligence

Track which competitors get cited for key topics and questions in AI search results. Understand your share of voice in AI-generated answers vs the competition.

Content Brief Generation

Create AI-optimized content briefs that guide writers to produce content more likely to be referenced by AI search engines. Data-driven briefs from GEO signal analysis.

Performance Monitoring

Track your AI search citation performance over time across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Dashboards for AI citation rate, share of voice, and traffic.

Workflow Automation

Legacy content operations features remain — AI writing workflows, content scaling, and team collaboration tools now reframed around AI search optimization goals.

How AirOps Compares

AirOps vs Traditional SEO Tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush)

Traditional SEO tools are optimized for Google search signals. AirOps specifically targets AI search engines — a complementary but different capability. These tools are not direct competitors: forward-thinking SEO teams should use both traditional SEO tools for Google optimization and AirOps for GEO. As AI search grows its share of the search market, the GEO category will become increasingly important.

AirOps vs AthenaHQ

AthenaHQ focuses specifically on ChatGPT search optimization. AirOps covers a broader range of AI search engines including Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, making it the more comprehensive platform for brands that need multi-surface AI search coverage. For teams that only need ChatGPT optimization, AthenaHQ may be more focused; for multi-surface GEO strategy, AirOps is the stronger choice.

Final Verdict — Is AirOps Worth It for GEO?

AirOps is making a smart strategic bet on the shift from traditional search to AI-generated answers. As ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews increasingly intercept search traffic, brands that invest in GEO now will have a significant advantage over those who don't.

AirOps is one of the most sophisticated platforms for this emerging discipline. The challenge is that GEO best practices are still evolving, making ROI harder to predict. For marketing teams and agencies that are forward-thinking about AI search, AirOps is worth exploring seriously.

Recommended for: Marketing teams and agencies that are forward-thinking about AI search, content-heavy brands losing organic search traffic, and organizations that want to be early movers in the GEO space.

Not recommended for: Small businesses with limited content budgets, teams primarily focused on traditional SEO without AI search concerns, or organizations where search traffic is not a core acquisition channel.
