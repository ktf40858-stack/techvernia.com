Overview

Reclaim.ai is an AI calendar assistant that works on top of Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar. It schedules tasks, recurring habits, focus time, breaks and meetings into your existing calendar, and moves them as priorities and meetings change.

Its features include Focus Time (a weekly focus goal that Reclaim defends), Habits (recurring events that flex to find time), Tasks scheduled by priority, Smart Meetings that find times across attendees, Scheduling Links as an alternative to Calendly, Calendar Sync to block time across calendars, Buffer Time, and pre-built AI agents, for example to protect deep-work time or detect meeting overload. Time tracking and workforce analytics show where time goes.

Dropbox acquired Reclaim in 2024, and Reclaim continues as its own product. It is sold per seat in four plans: Lite (free), Starter, Business and Enterprise.

Key Features

Focus Time

Set a weekly focus goal and Reclaim reserves and defends the time on your calendar.

Habits

Recurring routines that move to the best available slot instead of staying fixed.

Tasks

Schedules tasks onto your calendar by priority, with task-app integrations.

Smart Meetings

Finds meeting times across attendees' schedules.

Scheduling Links

Booking links to share your availability, positioned as a Calendly alternative.

Calendar Sync

Blocks availability across work and personal calendars to prevent double-booking.

Buffer Time

Adds breaks, prep time and follow-up time around meetings.

AI Agents and Analytics

Pre-built agents (deep-work protection, meeting overload detection) plus time tracking and workforce analytics.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Works on your existing Google or Outlook calendar
  • Free Lite plan
  • Habits and focus time move automatically around meetings
  • Scheduling links included on every plan
  • Calendar sync across work and personal calendars

Disadvantages

  • Lite is limited to 1 user, a 1-week scheduling range, 1 calendar sync and 1 scheduling link
  • Supports Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar only
  • Starter is capped at 10 seats and Business at 100
  • SSO and SCIM only on Enterprise ($22 per seat per month, yearly only)
  • Webhooks start on the Business plan

Best Use Cases

Reclaim.ai Excels At:

  • Professionals protecting focus time on a busy calendar
  • Teams on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 calendars
  • Executive assistants managing other people's calendars (delegated access on Business)
  • Teams scheduling recurring meetings across many attendees

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • People who use a calendar other than Google or Outlook
  • Teams looking for a full project management tool
  • Organizations that need SSO without moving to Enterprise

How It Compares

Reclaim.ai vs. Alternatives

  • vs. Motion: Motion is a full task and project app built around its own AI calendar, with no free plan (from $19 per seat per month, billed annually). Reclaim layers onto Google or Outlook and has a free plan.
  • vs. Calendly: Reclaim's Scheduling Links cover booking pages, bundled with task, habit and focus-time scheduling.
  • vs. ClickUp AI: ClickUp is a work platform with projects, docs and chat; Reclaim focuses on the calendar.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Reclaim.ai is a low-risk way to add AI scheduling to the calendar you already use: the free Lite plan is enough to see whether automatic habits and focus time suit you, and paid seats are inexpensive. It stays a calendar tool rather than a project manager, and it supports only Google and Outlook calendars.
