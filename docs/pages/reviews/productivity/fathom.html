Overview

Fathom is the #1 rated free AI meeting notetaker, trusted by hundreds of thousands of professionals who rely on it to record, transcribe, and summarize their Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet calls. In a market full of paid competitors, Fathom's generous free tier has made it the default choice for individuals and small teams who want meeting AI without the cost.

Beyond basic transcription, Fathom generates intelligent summaries organized by topic, automatically identifies action items and follow-ups, and syncs key insights directly to your CRM. Its AI understands meeting context well enough to create summaries that capture the essence of a conversation — not just a verbatim transcript.

In 2026, Fathom continues to lead the free tier market with regular improvements to summary quality, new CRM integrations, and better speaker identification. Its combination of exceptional free features, ease of setup, and privacy controls make it the go-to choice for professionals wanting to focus on their meetings instead of taking notes.

Key Features

Free AI Transcription

Automatic real-time transcription for Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet meetings. Supports 25+ languages. Available completely free — no credit card required.

Intelligent Summaries

AI-generated meeting summaries organized by topic and speaker. Captures key decisions, action items, and important context without you lifting a finger.

Instant Clips

Mark any moment during a meeting with a single click. Fathom creates a shareable video clip with transcript for that segment — perfect for sharing highlights.

CRM Integration

Sync meeting summaries, action items, and key insights directly to HubSpot, Salesforce, and other CRMs — eliminating manual data entry after every call.

Action Item Tracking

AI automatically identifies and tracks action items assigned during meetings. Never lose a commitment or follow-up buried in a long transcript again.

Team Collaboration

Share meeting recordings, transcripts, and summaries with team members. Search across all your past meetings to find any decision or discussion instantly.

How It Compares

Fathom vs. Otter.ai

Fathom's free plan is significantly more generous than Otter.ai. Otter has better real-time features and in-person meeting transcription support. Fathom wins on summary quality and CRM integration. For video meeting-centric workflows, Fathom is the clear winner on value.

Fathom vs. Grain

Grain focuses more on sales coaching and revenue intelligence. Fathom is better as a general-purpose free notetaker for any team or individual. Grain wins for sales teams needing manager coaching features and deep revenue analytics, but costs significantly more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fathom really free?+
Yes — Fathom's free plan includes unlimited meeting recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries with no credit card required. Some advanced features like full CRM sync and team analytics require the paid Team Edition.
Which meeting platforms does Fathom support?+
Fathom supports Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet. It works via a browser extension or bot that joins your meetings automatically when enabled.
Is Fathom private and secure?+
Yes — Fathom takes privacy seriously. You control who can see your recordings and transcripts. Meetings are only recorded when Fathom is actively enabled, and participants are notified that recording is in progress.
How accurate are Fathom's transcriptions?+
Very accurate — Fathom consistently ranks among the top AI notetakers for transcription accuracy, particularly for English. Quality in other languages varies but has improved significantly with recent updates.
