Overview

Grain is an AI meeting intelligence platform specifically optimized for sales teams and revenue-generating functions. Where general notetakers focus on transcription and summaries, Grain adds a layer of sales coaching intelligence — analyzing calls for best practices, identifying coaching opportunities, and helping managers improve team performance.

Founded in 2019, Grain has carved out a strong niche in the revenue intelligence space with its combination of meeting recording, AI summaries, video clip sharing, and deep CRM integration. Its HubSpot and Salesforce sync is particularly powerful for sales operations, automatically logging call details, action items, and deal insights.

In 2026, Grain continues to develop its AI coaching features, with improved conversation analysis, better deal risk identification, and enhanced manager coaching workflows. At $15/month starting price, it's accessible to individual sales reps while scaling to full enterprise revenue intelligence.

Key Features

AI Meeting Recording

Record and transcribe Zoom, Teams, and Meet calls with speaker identification and real-time searchable transcripts. Automatically captures every detail so reps can stay focused on selling.

Sales Coaching Intelligence

AI analyzes calls for talk-to-listen ratio, question frequency, competitor mentions, and coaching opportunities. Surface actionable coaching insights for managers automatically.

Video Clip Sharing

Create shareable video clips of specific meeting moments with a single click. Share highlights with prospects, internal stakeholders, or managers for rapid review and coaching.

CRM Sync (HubSpot + Salesforce)

Automatically sync meeting summaries, action items, and deal intelligence to HubSpot and Salesforce. Eliminate post-call manual data entry and keep your CRM always up to date.

Deal Intelligence

Track deal progress through call analysis, identify at-risk deals from conversation signals, and surface upsell opportunities. Keep revenue teams ahead of deal risks before they become losses.

Manager Coaching Workflows

Managers can review calls, leave timestamped coaching notes, and track improvement over time. Build a culture of continuous sales improvement without time-consuming 1:1 call reviews.

How It Compares

Grain vs. Fathom

Fathom has a better free plan and is better for general use by any team. Grain wins for sales teams with its coaching features and CRM depth. If you're in sales and need revenue intelligence alongside meeting notes, Grain is the stronger choice — though it comes at a cost where Fathom is free.

Grain vs. Gong

Gong is the enterprise standard for revenue intelligence but costs significantly more and requires larger teams to justify. Grain offers similar core features at a fraction of the price, making it the better choice for SMBs and growing sales teams that need conversation intelligence without enterprise pricing.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Grain is an excellent AI meeting intelligence platform for sales teams that want more than basic transcription. Its coaching features, CRM integration, and video clip capabilities deliver genuine value for revenue teams. At $15/month entry pricing, it's accessible to individual reps wanting to improve their sales performance. The Business plan at $39/user/month is where Grain's full value becomes clear — with complete CRM sync, deal intelligence, and manager coaching tools. For non-sales use cases, Fathom's free plan is likely sufficient. But for sales organizations serious about conversation intelligence and coaching, Grain is a compelling choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Grain's sales coaching work?+
Grain's AI analyzes recorded sales calls for performance metrics including talk-to-listen ratio, question frequency, competitor mentions, and buyer engagement signals. It surfaces coaching opportunities and allows managers to leave timestamped feedback directly on call recordings.
What CRMs does Grain integrate with?+
Grain has deep integrations with HubSpot and Salesforce, automatically syncing meeting summaries, action items, and call data. Additional CRM integrations are available via Zapier for other platforms.
Can I use Grain for free?+
Grain has a limited free plan for individual use. For full features including CRM sync and coaching tools, paid plans start at $15/month for the Starter plan.
How does Grain compare to Gong?+
Gong is the enterprise-grade standard for revenue intelligence with more advanced analytics and enterprise features. Grain offers similar core functionality at significantly lower cost, making it the better choice for SMBs and teams without enterprise-level budgets.
