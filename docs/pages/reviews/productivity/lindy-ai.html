Lindy AI Overview

Generous free tier: Lindy's 400 free monthly credits make it genuinely accessible for individuals wanting to test AI automation without any cost commitment. The Computer Use feature — which lets AI agents interact with any web application — is one of the most powerful capabilities available in a no-code tool at this price point.

Lindy AI is a no-code platform for building and deploying personal AI agents that automate your repetitive work tasks — email management, CRM updates, meeting scheduling, lead follow-up, and more. With 400 free credits per month and a Computer Use feature (AI that can control your browser), Lindy is one of the most accessible agent-building platforms of 2026.

Founded in 2023, Lindy targets knowledge workers who want the power of AI automation without writing code. Its visual workflow builder lets you create sophisticated agents by combining triggers, conditions, and AI actions in an intuitive interface. Pre-built "Lindy" templates let you deploy common automations in minutes.

Lindy's standout feature is Computer Use — borrowed from Anthropic's Claude technology — which allows Lindy agents to interact with any web application visually, enabling automation of tasks that don't have APIs. Combined with 400 free monthly credits, it makes powerful AI automation accessible to individuals and small teams.

Key Features

Visual Agent Builder

Create AI agents with a no-code visual workflow editor. Connect triggers, conditions, and AI actions without writing code — drag, drop, and deploy.

Computer Use

AI that can see and interact with your screen and browser, enabling automation of any web-based task regardless of API availability. Powered by Anthropic technology.

Email Automation

AI agents that read, categorize, respond to, and forward emails based on your defined rules and context. Handles your inbox while you focus on high-value work.

CRM Integration

Automatically update CRM records, log calls, add notes, and follow up with leads based on triggers and AI analysis. Works with HubSpot, Salesforce, and more.

Meeting Scheduling

AI agent that handles scheduling coordination — reading availability, proposing times, and confirming appointments so you never play calendar tag again.

Pre-built Templates (50+)

Ready-to-use agent templates for common use cases — email triage, lead qualification, meeting summaries, and more. Deploy powerful automations in minutes.

How Lindy AI Compares

Lindy AI vs Zapier with AI

Zapier has more integrations and is more mature as a platform. Lindy's Computer Use feature and AI-first design make it better for tasks requiring intelligence and screen interaction. If you need sheer breadth of integrations, Zapier wins. If you need an agent that can navigate web pages and reason about tasks, Lindy is the better choice.

Lindy AI vs Make.com

Make.com is more powerful for complex multi-step workflows with technical teams. Lindy is better for non-technical users who want agent behavior rather than pure workflow automation. Make.com's visual flowchart approach is more flexible technically; Lindy's agent templates make common tasks accessible in minutes.

Final Verdict — Is Lindy AI Worth It?

Lindy AI is one of the best no-code agent builders available in 2026, particularly for professionals who want to automate their personal workflows without engineering resources. The 400 free monthly credits make it genuinely accessible, and Computer Use opens up automation possibilities that traditional tools can't match.

It's not yet enterprise-grade for complex organizational deployments, but for individual professionals and small teams, it delivers impressive value. If you've ever wished you had a personal AI assistant to handle your inbox, CRM, and scheduling, Lindy is worth exploring.

Recommended for: Knowledge workers, sales professionals, small business owners, and anyone who wants to automate repetitive digital tasks without writing code.

Not recommended for: Developers comfortable with code-based automation, enterprises with strict security requirements, or heavy automation users who'll burn through credits quickly.
