Overview

Otter.ai is an AI meeting assistant. Its notetaker joins Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet calls (it can auto-join from Google Calendar), transcribes them live with speaker identification, and produces summaries and action items. The desktop app can also record without a bot, and you can import audio and video files.

Otter AI Chat answers questions across your meetings and connected apps, and the Otter MCP Server gives ChatGPT, Claude and other AI tools access to your meeting knowledge. Otter also offers an AI Meeting Agent, pre-call briefings, CRM sync and agents aimed at sales, education, media and recruiting teams.

Otter sells four plans: Basic (free), Pro, Business and Enterprise. They differ mainly in monthly transcription minutes, maximum conversation length, AI Chat queries and admin features. Apps are available for Mac, Windows, iOS and Android, plus a Chrome extension.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Free plan with 300 minutes a month
  • Works with Zoom, Teams and Google Meet, plus bot-free desktop recording
  • AI Chat across all your meetings
  • MCP server for ChatGPT and Claude, even on the free plan
  • Low annual price on Pro ($8.33 per user per month)

Disadvantages

  • Free plan caps conversations at 30 minutes and keeps only the 25 most recent
  • Pro is limited to 1,200 minutes a month and 90 minutes per conversation
  • Monthly billing on Pro ($16.99) costs about twice the annual rate
  • API, webhooks, SSO and HIPAA (add-on) only on Enterprise
  • Only 3 lifetime file imports on the free plan

Best Use Cases

Otter.ai Excels At:

  • Teams that meet on Zoom, Teams or Google Meet and want searchable notes
  • Sales teams that sync meeting notes to Salesforce or HubSpot
  • Students and educators (student pricing on Pro)
  • People who want their meeting notes available inside ChatGPT or Claude

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Regular meetings longer than 30 minutes on the free plan
  • Teams that need API access without an Enterprise contract
  • Teams that want meeting notes stored inside their docs tool

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Otter.ai is a mature choice for recording and searching meetings on Zoom, Teams and Google Meet. The free plan is fine for trying it, but its 30-minute limit pushes regular users to Pro, which is inexpensive on annual billing. Admins who need SSO, an API or HIPAA should plan for Enterprise.
