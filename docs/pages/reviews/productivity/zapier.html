Overview

Zapier is a no-code automation platform. A Zap links a trigger in one app to actions in others, and Zapier connects to more than 9,000 apps. Zapier says it has more than 3 million business customers.

Beyond Zaps, Zapier includes Tables (a built-in database), Forms, Agents (AI agents that work across your apps), Chatbots, Canvas for mapping and documenting workflows, Copilot for building with AI help, and Zapier MCP, which lets AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT run Zapier actions. Zapier now presents itself as a control plane for AI workflows and agents, with governance features such as AI guardrails and audit history.

Pricing is based on tasks: Zapier counts any successful action as a task, while triggers, filters, paths, Formatter steps and Tables or Forms steps are free. Plans are Free, Professional, Team and Enterprise. Zapier states SOC 2 Type II and SOC 3 compliance, and GDPR and CCPA compliance.

Key Features

Zaps

Trigger-and-action workflows; multi-step Zaps, filters, paths and webhooks from the Professional plan.

9,000+ App Integrations

Connectors for more than 9,000 apps, with premium apps unlocked on paid plans.

Tables

A built-in database; Table triggers and actions do not count toward tasks, and AI fields start on Pro.

Agents

AI agents that act across your apps, with a separate activity allowance (400/month on Free).

Chatbots

Two chatbots on the Free plan, with paid add-ons for more.

Zapier MCP

Lets Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants call Zapier actions; each MCP tool call uses 2 tasks.

Canvas

Diagram and document workflows; included on all plans.

Copilot

Build Zaps with AI help in plain language (daily message limit on Free).

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Connects to more than 9,000 apps
  • Free plan with unlimited two-step Zaps
  • Triggers, filters and Tables steps do not use tasks
  • MCP access on every plan for Claude and ChatGPT
  • 14-day Professional trial without a credit card

Disadvantages

  • Task-based pricing rises quickly with volume
  • Free plan is limited to two-step Zaps, 15-minute polling and 1 seat
  • Each MCP tool call uses 2 tasks
  • Shared workspaces and app connections need the Team plan (from $69 per month, billed annually)
  • Monthly billing costs 50% more than annual billing

Best Use Cases

Zapier Excels At:

  • Small businesses connecting SaaS tools without developers
  • Operations and marketing teams moving leads between forms, CRMs and email
  • Teams that want AI assistants to act in business apps through MCP
  • Quick internal tools built with Tables and Forms

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • High-volume data pipelines, where per-task pricing adds up
  • Developers who prefer code-first automation
  • Organizations that need to self-host their automation platform

How It Compares

Zapier vs. Alternatives

  • vs. n8n: n8n can be self-hosted; Zapier is a cloud service with a larger catalog of ready-made app integrations.
  • vs. built-in automations in ClickUp or Notion: these handle workflows inside one tool; Zapier connects across thousands of apps.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Zapier remains the simplest way to connect business apps without code, and its AI features (Agents, Chatbots, MCP) build on the same 9,000+ integrations. The free plan is enough for simple two-step Zaps; real workflows need Professional, and costs grow with task volume, so estimate your monthly tasks before choosing a tier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Zapier free?+
Yes. The Free plan includes 100 tasks per month, unlimited two-step Zaps with 15-minute polling, and one seat.
What counts as a task?+
Any successful action. Triggers, filters, paths, Formatter and Delay steps, and Zapier Tables and Forms steps do not count. Each MCP tool call uses 2 tasks.
Is there a free trial?+
New accounts get a 14-day trial of the Professional plan with no credit card required.
What is Zapier MCP?+
A way for AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT to run Zapier actions in your connected apps. Basic access is included on all plans.
Is Zapier secure?+
Zapier states SOC 2 Type II and SOC 3 compliance, GDPR and CCPA compliance, and offers SSO and SCIM.
