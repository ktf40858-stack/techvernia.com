Overview

6sense is a B2B go-to-market platform built around buyer intent data. The company says its "Signalverse" data captures about one trillion signals a day, which it uses to spot accounts researching a purchase and to predict where they are in the buying cycle.

The product has two main parts. Revenue Marketing covers Audience Builder, Intelligent Workflows, Smart Form Fill, Advertising 6AI and reporting. Sales Intelligence covers Sales Copilot 6AI, Account Prioritization 6AI, List Builder, company and people search, and sales alerts. Predictive modeling and RevvyAI, a conversational assistant for go-to-market questions, sit on top.

6sense is aimed at marketing and sales teams at B2B companies that run account-based programs. Customers named on its site include Okta, SAP and Shell.

Key Features

Intent Data & Web Deanonymization

Shows which accounts are researching relevant topics and which companies are behind anonymous website visits.

Predictive Modeling

Scores accounts on their likely buying stage so teams can focus on in-market accounts.

Advertising 6AI

Runs account-based advertising aimed at the audiences you build in 6sense.

Sales Copilot 6AI & Account Prioritization 6AI

Ranks accounts for reps and helps them decide whom to contact and how.

Email Agents 6AI

AI agents that run email outreach to target accounts.

MCP Server

Makes 6sense data available to other AI tools through the Model Context Protocol.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Intent data, predictive scoring and ABM advertising in one platform
  • Web deanonymization shows which companies visit your site
  • Separate Sales Intelligence product for reps (list building, people search, alerts)
  • The homepage advertises a free way to start with Sales Intelligence
  • MCP server for connecting AI assistants to 6sense data

Disadvantages

  • Paid plans are quote-based; no public prices
  • Built for B2B account-based programs, not B2C or transactional sales
  • A broad platform that needs setup across both marketing and sales
  • Limits of the free Sales Intelligence option are not spelled out on the homepage

Pricing

6sense does not publish prices for its Revenue Marketing platform. Pricing is quote-based; contact sales.

Best Use Cases

6sense Excels For:

  • B2B marketing teams running account-based marketing (ABM) programs
  • Sales teams that want to prioritize accounts showing buying intent
  • Companies that want to identify anonymous website visitors at the account level
  • RevOps teams combining intent, advertising and contact data

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • B2C businesses or high-volume transactional sales
  • Small teams that need transparent, self-serve pricing for the full platform
  • Teams that only need a contact database

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 6sense do?

It identifies B2B accounts that show buying intent, predicts their buying stage, and gives marketing and sales teams tools to act on that: audiences, ads, workflows, account prioritization and contact search.

How much does 6sense cost?

6sense does not publish prices for its main platform; pricing is quote-based. Its homepage advertises a free way to get started with Sales Intelligence.

What is the difference between Revenue Marketing and Sales Intelligence?

Revenue Marketing is for marketers: audience building, workflows, Smart Form Fill, advertising and reporting. Sales Intelligence is for reps: Sales Copilot, account prioritization, list building, company and people search, and alerts.

Does 6sense use AI agents?

Yes. The site lists Email Agents 6AI, Advertising 6AI, Sales Copilot 6AI, Account Prioritization 6AI and RevvyAI, a conversational assistant for go-to-market questions.

Who uses 6sense?

B2B marketing and sales teams in industries such as software, business services, financial services, manufacturing, and transportation and logistics. Named customers include Okta, SAP and Shell.

Final Verdict

It is broad and enterprise-oriented, with quote-based pricing, so it suits organizations with a real ABM program more than small teams looking for a contact list.
