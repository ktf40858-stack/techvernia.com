Overview

Attention builds AI agents that learn from a team's sales conversations. It records and transcribes calls, then turns them into CRM updates, follow-ups, coaching scorecards and reports. Its old attention.tech address now redirects to attention.com.

Its Super Agent layer sits on top of the existing stack, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Snowflake, Slack, Notion, Google Sheets and email. Managers can ask questions in plain language, get proactive alerts and let agents write back to the CRM. Each integration can be set to read-only or write-back.

Attention targets sales reps, sales leaders, revenue teams and customer success. The build is configured with the customer before use, and every account includes a dedicated Customer Success Manager.

Key Features

Call Recordings and Transcription

Records and transcribes calls; integrations include Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and RingCentral.

CRM Auto-Update

Writes call notes that match your CRM fields and updates the related opportunity after every call. Supports custom fields and objects.

Super Agent

No-code AI layer over your GTM data with natural-language answers, proactive alerts and a visual workflow builder.

AI Coaching Scorecards

Grades reps against frameworks such as BANT and MEDDIC.

Follow-up Automation

Helps keep deals from stalling after a call.

Reporting and Forecasting

Pipeline reporting and forecasting built from call and CRM data.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Detailed CRM write-back with field mapping and append or overwrite options
  • Super Agent needs no code or SQL; the vendor says most OAuth connections take under 30 minutes
  • Integrations include Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Slack, Gmail and Outlook
  • SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, with a BAA available
  • Dedicated Customer Success Manager and priority Slack support on every account

Disadvantages

  • No public pricing or free trial on the website
  • Onboarding is configured with the vendor, so you go through sales first
  • 24/7 on-call coverage is limited to Enterprise plans
  • Several differentiators are vendor claims worth testing in a pilot

Best Use Cases

Attention Excels For:

  • Sales teams that want CRM notes and opportunity fields updated after every call
  • Sales leaders coaching reps against BANT or MEDDIC
  • RevOps teams that want plain-language answers across call, CRM and warehouse data
  • Sellers in healthcare-related markets who need HIPAA compliance and a BAA

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Teams that want self-serve sign-up with published prices
  • Teams looking mainly for a prospect database
  • Very small teams that record few calls

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Attention do?

It records and transcribes sales calls, then uses AI to update your CRM, draft follow-ups, score reps, and answer questions about your pipeline.

Which tools does Attention integrate with?

Its integrations page lists Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Apollo, Clay, Gmail, Outlook, Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, RingCentral and Slack.

Is Attention secure?

Attention's security page says it is SOC 2 Type II compliant and HIPAA compliant, maintains a BAA, encrypts data in transit (TLS 1.2 and 1.3) and at rest, and runs annual penetration tests.

How much does Attention cost?

Attention does not publish pricing. Contact sales for a quote.

Does Attention update my CRM automatically?

Yes. After each call it updates the related opportunity and can sync call notes with one click. You choose which fields to map and whether to append or overwrite.
