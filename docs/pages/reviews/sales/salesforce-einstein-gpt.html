Overview

Einstein GPT was announced by Salesforce on March 7, 2023, as what it called the world’s first generative AI for CRM. It combined Salesforce’s own models, partner models such as OpenAI’s, and real-time data from Data Cloud to generate content across sales, service, marketing, commerce and IT. At launch it was in closed pilot.

For sales, the launch announcement listed drafting emails, scheduling meetings and preparing for the next customer interaction. For service, it generated knowledge articles from past case notes and personalized replies for agents. The assistant was then sold as Einstein Copilot, generally available in April 2024.

Salesforce’s own editor’s notes now say that Einstein GPT has evolved into Agentforce and that Einstein Copilot has been upgraded to Agentforce. Agentforce is Salesforce’s AI agent platform: agents use the Atlas Reasoning Engine to break a request into smaller tasks and draw on business data, including Data 360. For sales teams, Salesforce lists agents such as Sales Development Representative (SDR) and Sales Coach.

Agentforce is sold through Flex Credits, per conversation, or per-user add-ons and editions, and every Salesforce customer can start for free with Salesforce Foundations.

Key Features

Agent Builder

Configure agents, subagents, actions and instructions for an Agentforce deployment.

Atlas Reasoning Engine

Breaks the initial prompt into smaller tasks and evaluates each step before acting.

SDR Agent

Engages prospects around the clock, answering questions, handling objections and scheduling meetings.

Sales Coach

Runs personalized role-play sessions for sales reps.

Grounded in CRM Data

Agents draw on trusted business data, including external data brought in through Data 360.

Prompt Builder

Included with Salesforce Foundations, alongside Agent Builder and Agent Script.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Built directly into the Salesforce platform and its CRM data
  • Free starting point for existing customers through Salesforce Foundations
  • Prebuilt sales agents such as SDR and Sales Coach
  • Several ways to buy: Flex Credits, per conversation or per user
  • One platform covers sales, service and other Salesforce clouds

Disadvantages

  • Only relevant if your company runs on Salesforce
  • The product changed names several times (Einstein GPT, Einstein Copilot, Agentforce), so older documentation can be confusing
  • Usage-based Flex Credits make monthly costs harder to predict
  • Per-user add-ons are charged on top of existing Salesforce licenses

Pricing

Einstein GPT has no price of its own any more. Salesforce publishes these Agentforce prices (USD):

Salesforce Foundations ($0)

Start with Agentforce for free; includes Agent Builder, Prompt Builder and Agent Script.

Flex Credits ($500 per 100,000 credits)

Each Agentforce action uses 20 Flex Credits; each Agentforce Voice action uses 30.

Conversations ($2 per conversation)

Per-conversation pricing as an alternative to Flex Credits.

Agentforce add-ons ($125/user/month)

For Sales, Service, Field Service and Operations; $150 per user per month for Industries Clouds.

Max Edition (from $550/user/month)

Includes the Agentforce add-on and 2.75 million Flex Credits per org per year.

Agentforce User License ($5/user/month)

Requires Flex Credits. An Agentforce Coworker User License is listed at $50 per user per month.

Salesforce states that pricing is subject to change and recommends contacting sales for detailed pricing.

Best Use Cases

Salesforce Einstein GPT Excels For:

  • Sales Cloud customers who want AI help with emails, meetings and follow-ups
  • Teams that want an always-on agent to qualify and book prospects
  • Sales managers who want AI role-play coaching for reps
  • Organizations already using Data 360 for customer data

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Companies that do not use Salesforce
  • Buyers who need a fixed, predictable monthly price
  • Small teams that only need a simple AI writing assistant

Frequently Asked Questions

What happened to Einstein GPT?

Salesforce says Einstein GPT has evolved into Agentforce, its agentic AI platform. In between, the assistant was sold as Einstein Copilot (generally available April 2024).

Is Einstein Copilot the same as Agentforce?

Salesforce states that Einstein Copilot has been upgraded to Agentforce.

How much does it cost?

Agentforce is billed with Flex Credits ($500 per 100,000 credits), at $2 per conversation, or with per-user add-ons from $125 per user per month. Salesforce Foundations is free.

Do I need Salesforce?

Yes. Agentforce runs on the Salesforce platform and is offered to Salesforce customers.

What sales tasks did Einstein GPT handle?

At launch, Salesforce listed drafting emails, scheduling meetings and preparing for the next customer interaction.

Which sales agents are available now?

Salesforce lists a Sales Development Representative (SDR) agent and a Sales Coach agent, among others.
