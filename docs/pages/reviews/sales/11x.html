Overview

11x is one of the most talked-about autonomous AI SDR platforms of 2026, built around its flagship AI agent "Alice" — an AI sales rep that prospecting, writes outreach, handles follow-ups, and books meetings entirely without human intervention. Founded in 2022 and backed by notable investors, 11x is positioning itself at the forefront of the agentic sales revolution where AI doesn't just assist reps, it replaces repetitive SDR work at scale.

Unlike traditional outbound tools that require humans to set up sequences, 11x's Alice operates as a true autonomous agent. She researches prospects using publicly available signals (LinkedIn activity, company news, funding rounds), personalizes outreach based on these triggers, and adapts her messaging dynamically based on how prospects respond. The platform supports email, LinkedIn, and phone outbound in a multi-channel workflow without requiring manual configuration at each step.

In 2026, 11x has expanded its agentic capabilities to include more sophisticated objection handling and multi-touch sequences that span weeks without human oversight. The platform targets mid-market and enterprise companies looking to dramatically reduce SDR cost-per-meeting while maintaining pipeline quality. With companies reporting 3-5x more meetings booked per dollar spent vs. traditional SDR teams, 11x is reshaping expectations around outbound economics.

Key Features

Alice — Autonomous AI SDR

Alice is 11x's core AI agent: she researches prospects, crafts personalized outreach, manages multi-touch sequences, handles replies, and books meetings — all without a human in the loop.

Signal-Based Personalization

Alice monitors intent signals like LinkedIn posts, job changes, funding rounds, and company news to trigger timely, hyper-relevant outreach at the exact right moment.

Multi-Channel Outreach

Runs coordinated sequences across email, LinkedIn, and phone — automatically determining the optimal channel and timing based on prospect behavior and engagement patterns.

Autonomous Reply Handling

When prospects respond, Alice reads intent, classifies the reply (interested, objection, not right time), and crafts an appropriate response — escalating to human reps only when genuinely needed.

Calendar Booking

Integrates with Calendly and Google Calendar to book qualified meetings directly on rep calendars, handling all scheduling logistics without human coordination.

CRM Integration

Syncs with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other major CRMs. All activities, notes, and meeting outcomes are logged automatically, keeping CRM data clean without manual entry.

How It Compares

11x vs AiSDR

Both are autonomous AI SDR platforms, but 11x typically targets larger companies with higher deal values and more sophisticated multi-channel orchestration. AiSDR has a lower entry price and stronger HubSpot-native focus. 11x's signal-based personalization is generally more advanced.

11x vs Clay + Human SDR

Clay provides data enrichment and signal identification; humans still do the outreach. 11x automates the entire loop including the outreach and conversation. Clay + human SDR wins on personalization depth for strategic accounts; 11x wins on cost and scale for volume prospecting.

11x vs Outreach / Salesloft

Outreach and Salesloft are sequence automation platforms that still require humans to write messages and manage replies. 11x automates the entire SDR workflow including follow-up conversations, representing a generational step forward in automation depth.

Final Verdict

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "autonomous" mean in 11x's context?+
Autonomous means Alice handles the full outbound cycle end-to-end: researching prospects, writing personalized outreach, sending emails and LinkedIn messages, reading and classifying prospect replies, handling objections, and booking meetings — all without requiring a human to approve or intervene at each step. Humans are only looped in when a prospect is ready for a sales conversation.
How good is 11x's personalization compared to human SDRs?+
11x's personalization is strong for volume outbound, using real signals like LinkedIn activity, company funding news, and job changes to craft relevant openers. For strategic accounts requiring deep relationship nuance and expert-level industry knowledge in every message, skilled human SDRs still have an edge. The trade-off is scale: 11x contacts far more prospects with good personalization vs. a human's fewer prospects with exceptional personalization.
Which CRMs does 11x integrate with?+
11x integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other major CRM platforms. All prospecting activity, email sends, replies, and meeting outcomes are automatically logged to the CRM, keeping records current without manual data entry.
Is 11x worth the price for a smaller company?+
11x's pricing puts it in range for mid-market companies and above. For early-stage startups or small businesses, the cost may be hard to justify before outbound messaging is validated. Companies spending $5,000+/month on human SDR headcount should evaluate 11x as a replacement — the economics typically become compelling at that level.
