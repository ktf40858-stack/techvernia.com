Overview

HubSpot AI is the set of AI features built into HubSpot's CRM platform. Breeze Assistant is the entry point, and Agent Hub provides prebuilt agents (Prospecting, Customer, Data, Content, Campaign, Nurture and Revenue agents, plus Smart Deal Progression) along with Agent Builder for custom agents.

For sales teams, the Prospecting Agent monitors accounts for buying intent and reaches out when an account is ready, while the Data Agent answers questions from CRM records, calls and documents. Agent Hub is included in all Starter, Professional and Enterprise editions, and many AI actions use HubSpot Credits.

It is aimed at companies that run, or want to run, sales and marketing on HubSpot. Because the agents work directly on CRM data, there is no separate integration to maintain.

Key Features

Breeze Assistant

HubSpot's AI assistant, which gives access to agents wherever your team works.

Prospecting Agent

Monitors accounts for buying intent and reaches out the moment an account is ready to buy.

Customer Agent

Resolves common customer questions 24/7 using past contact and contract records.

Data Agent

Answers custom questions about contacts and companies from CRM data, calls and documents.

Content Agent

Drafts blog posts, social content and landing pages in your brand voice.

Agent Builder

Build custom agents on one canvas using your customer data; these consume HubSpot Credits.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • AI built into the CRM, so agents act on native records
  • Agent Hub included in every paid edition (Starter, Professional, Enterprise)
  • Published per-seat prices, plus a free Sales Hub tier for up to 2 users
  • SOC 2 Type 2 audits; third-party AI providers are contractually barred from training on your data
  • Monthly HubSpot Credits included with each paid plan

Disadvantages

  • Many AI actions consume credits, and unused credits do not roll over
  • Professional and Enterprise require one-time onboarding fees ($1,500 and $3,500)
  • Most of the value depends on using HubSpot as your CRM
  • Agent Hub is listed for paid editions, not the free tools

Pricing

Prices below are Sales Hub per-seat prices from HubSpot's pricing page (USD). Agent Hub is included in paid editions; many AI actions use HubSpot Credits.

Free ($0)

Free for up to 2 users, no credit card. Includes the mobile app, 1-to-1 email and a basic meetings link.

Starter (from $7/seat/mo)

Listed as "Starts at $7/mo/seat," with $20/mo/seat also shown depending on billing. Includes 500 HubSpot Credits per month.

Professional (from $90/seat/mo)

$90 to $100/seat/month depending on billing, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee. Includes 3,000 credits per month.

Enterprise ($150/seat/mo)

Plus a one-time $3,500 onboarding fee. Includes 5,000 credits per month.

Extra HubSpot Credits cost $0.010 each. Credits reset monthly and unused credits do not roll over.

Best Use Cases

HubSpot AI Excels For:

  • Teams already using HubSpot CRM
  • Small and mid-size businesses that want published prices and a free starting point
  • Marketing and sales teams that want content, campaigns and prospecting on one platform
  • RevOps teams building custom agents on CRM data

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Companies committed to Salesforce or another CRM
  • Teams that need deep conversation intelligence or forecasting as their core tool
  • Budgets that cannot absorb onboarding fees at the Professional level

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HubSpot AI?

The AI features built into HubSpot: Breeze Assistant, Agent Hub with prebuilt agents (Prospecting, Customer, Data, Content and others) and Agent Builder for custom agents.

Is HubSpot AI free?

Agent Hub is included in Starter, Professional and Enterprise editions. Sales Hub has a free tier for up to 2 users, but HubSpot does not present AI agents as part of its free tools.

What are HubSpot Credits?

A usage currency for many AI features and custom agents. Sales Hub Starter includes 500 per month, Professional 3,000 and Enterprise 5,000; extra credits cost $0.010 each and do not roll over.

What does the Prospecting Agent do?

It monitors accounts for buying intent and reaches out automatically when an account is ready to buy.

Is my data used to train AI models?

HubSpot says third-party AI providers are contractually prohibited from training their models on your data, and that the platform undergoes SOC 2 Type 2 audits.
