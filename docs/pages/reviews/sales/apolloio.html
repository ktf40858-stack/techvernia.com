Overview

Apollo.io is a go-to-market platform that pairs a B2B contact and company database with outreach and AI tools. Apollo says its data covers more than 240 million contacts and 30 million companies, and that over 600,000 companies use the product.

The platform is organized in three layers: Data (search and waterfall enrichment), Intelligence (AI agents that run in the background, trained on your ICP, products and playbooks) and Execution (email campaigns, a parallel dialer, a Chrome extension and an AI assistant). It serves sales reps, sales leaders, RevOps, marketers and developers.

Its main draw is breadth at a low entry point: a free plan with no time limit, then Basic, Professional and Custom tiers that add credits, AI features, call recording and security options.

Key Features

Apollo Data & Waterfall Enrichment

Search 240M+ contacts and 30M+ companies, and enrich records from several data sources in sequence.

Email Campaigns & Deliverability

Automated outreach and follow-ups on every account, with deliverability tools. Free plans connect Gmail only; paid plans add Microsoft and other providers.

Parallel Dialer

Dials several prospects at once to increase the number of live conversations.

AI Assistant & Agents

Agents trained through the GTM Harness and AI Context Center on your ICP, products and playbooks, with memory that improves over time.

Chrome Extension & Apollo Anywhere

Use Apollo data and actions from your browser and the other tools you work in.

Apollo MCP & Integrations

Connect Apollo to your CRM and to AI tools through its MCP server. API access is on Custom plans.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Free plan with no time limit
  • Large database: 240M+ contacts and 30M+ companies
  • Data, email outreach, dialer and AI agents in one tool
  • Credits are pooled across the team
  • Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time

Disadvantages

  • Most data actions use credits, and unused credits expire at the end of each billing cycle
  • On the free plan, email campaigns connect to Gmail accounts only
  • API access, SSO and advanced security are reserved for Custom plans
  • Call recording and advanced CRM integrations require the Professional tier

Pricing

Apollo has four tiers. Monthly plans are month-to-month; annual plans are paid upfront at a discounted rate. Check apollo.io/pricing for current per-user prices and credit amounts.

Free

No time limit. For exploring Apollo or light use; email campaigns connect to Gmail only.

Basic

For individual sellers doing regular outbound. Adds email sequencing and core integrations.

Professional

For full-time reps: higher credit limits, AI features, call recording and advanced CRM integrations.

Custom

Custom volumes, SSO, advanced security, API access and dedicated support, through Apollo's sales team.

Credits are pooled across the team, granted at the start of each billing cycle, and expire at the end of it. Add-on credits can be bought in settings. A free trial gives access to most features of the selected plan, with some limits.

Best Use Cases

Apollo.io Excels For:

  • SDRs and founders who need contact data and outreach in one tool
  • Small and mid-size teams that want to start free
  • RevOps teams enriching CRM records with waterfall enrichment
  • Outbound teams that make a lot of calls (parallel dialer)

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Teams whose core need is conversation intelligence or forecasting
  • Organizations that need SSO or API access but do not want a Custom plan
  • Users who do not want to manage credit consumption

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Apollo.io free?

Yes. Apollo has a free plan with no time limit. On the free plan, email campaigns connect only to Gmail accounts.

How do Apollo credits work?

Credits are pooled across the team and granted at the start of each billing cycle. Unused credits expire at the end of the cycle. Add-on credits can be bought from a slider in settings and renew each cycle until removed.

How large is Apollo's database?

Apollo says it covers 240M+ contacts and 30M+ companies.

Does Apollo have an API?

Yes. API access is available on Custom plans.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The trial includes credits and access to most features of the selected plan, with some trial limits. When it ends, you can stay on the free plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly plans are month-to-month and can be cancelled at any time. Annual plans are paid upfront for the year at a discounted rate.
