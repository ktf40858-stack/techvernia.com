Overview

People.ai has rebranded as Backstory, and people.ai now redirects to backstory.ai. The company says it is the "Same product, same data infrastructure," repositioned around answers rather than dashboards.

Backstory captures emails, meetings, calls and CRM records automatically, then tells sales leaders which deals are real, which are at risk and what to do next. Features include Backstory Assistant, engagement scoring, deal summaries, forecasting, waterfall charts, automated account plans, MEDDPICC scoring and an AI sales coach.

It is built mainly for CROs and VPs of Sales making commit and pipeline decisions; the company says it is "not a rep productivity tool." It integrates with Salesforce, Dynamics, Oracle, Gmail, Outlook, Zoom, Teams and Slack, and the company says most teams go live in 2 to 4 weeks.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Automatic capture of emails, meetings and calls
  • Hybrid pricing: viewers such as managers and finance do not pay
  • No feature tiers; everyone gets the full product
  • Supports Salesforce, Dynamics and Oracle CRMs
  • MCP server for AI assistants

Disadvantages

  • Recent rebrand: older People.ai documentation and reviews may be out of date
  • No published prices or free trial
  • Built for leadership decisions, not rep productivity
  • Implementation typically takes 2 to 4 weeks

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based; contact sales. Backstory says its pricing is "hybrid, not per-seat."

Connected people

You pay for the customer-facing people whose activity feeds the platform, such as reps, CS and channel.

Consumption

Charges apply when Backstory delivers outputs such as a surfaced risk or a deal summary; reading a dashboard is free. Consumption is pre-packaged for the term and overuse is not back-billed.

Best Use Cases

People.ai Excels For:

  • CROs and VPs of Sales making commit calls
  • RevOps teams that want activity data captured automatically
  • Organizations on Salesforce, Dynamics or Oracle
  • Teams that qualify deals with MEDDPICC

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Reps looking for a prospecting or outreach tool
  • Small teams that want self-serve pricing
  • Teams that need a same-day setup

Frequently Asked Questions

Is People.ai still available?

Yes, under the name Backstory. The company says: "People.ai is now Backstory. Same product, same data infrastructure."

What does Backstory do?

It captures emails, meetings, calls and CRM records, then gives sales leaders direct answers on which deals are real, which are at risk and what to do next.

How much does Backstory cost?

Prices are not published. Pricing is hybrid, not per-seat: you pay for connected customer-facing people and for the outputs your team consumes. Request a quote.

Which tools does Backstory integrate with?

Salesforce, Dynamics, Oracle, Gmail, Outlook, Zoom, Teams and Slack, plus an MCP server for AI tools.

How long does implementation take?

The company says most teams go live in 2 to 4 weeks.
