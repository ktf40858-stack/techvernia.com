Overview

Instantly.ai is a cold email platform that has rapidly become the go-to tool for outbound sales teams who send high volumes of cold email. Founded in 2021, Instantly built its reputation on solving cold email's biggest technical challenge: deliverability. By automating inbox warm-up, rotating sending across multiple email accounts, and managing sending limits intelligently, Instantly helps teams scale cold email without getting their domains blacklisted.

Cold email at scale is technically challenging: send too many emails from one address, use spam trigger words, or skip warm-up, and your emails land in spam rather than inboxes. Instantly automates all the technical infrastructure — creating, warming, and rotating multiple email accounts across a campaign — so sales teams can focus on messaging and strategy rather than deliverability mechanics.

In 2026, Instantly has added B2B lead database access (160M+ contacts), AI email writing assistance, and CRM integrations. The platform serves sales agencies, SaaS startups, and enterprise outbound teams as the infrastructure layer for high-volume cold outreach.

Key Features

Inbox Rotation & Warmup

Automatically rotates sending across multiple email accounts and progressively warms new inboxes. Maximizes deliverability at scale.

Unlimited Email Accounts

Connect unlimited sending accounts (Gmail, Outlook, SMTP). Scale volume without per-seat limits on sending accounts.

B2B Lead Database

160M+ verified B2B contacts with email, phone, company, and title data. Find prospects without leaving the platform.

AI Email Writing

AI generates and optimizes cold email copy, subject lines, and personalization variables. A/B test AI-suggested variations automatically.

Campaign Analytics

Track open rates, reply rates, positive reply rates, and bounce rates across campaigns and sending accounts. Identify deliverability issues early.

Unibox

Unified inbox across all connected email accounts. Manage all replies from one place regardless of which sending account received the reply.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is inbox rotation and why does it matter?+
Inbox rotation means distributing email sends across multiple sending accounts rather than blasting from one address. This is critical because email providers rate-limit and flag accounts that send too many emails. Rotating across multiple accounts (each sending within safe limits) allows high-volume sending while maintaining deliverability.
What does "inbox warmup" mean?+
New email accounts have no sending reputation and will land in spam. Warmup is the process of gradually increasing sending volume while generating positive engagement signals (opens, replies) from other accounts. Instantly automates this process, bringing new inboxes to full sending capacity without manual effort.
Does Instantly work with Gmail and Outlook?+
Yes — Instantly connects with Gmail (Google Workspace), Outlook (Microsoft 365), and any SMTP-compatible email provider. You can mix different providers across your sending accounts, which can improve deliverability by diversifying infrastructure.
Is Instantly suitable for agency use?+
Yes — Instantly is popular with cold email agencies running outbound for multiple clients. The multi-workspace support, client management features, and pricing structure (which doesn't charge per-seat for sending accounts) make it well-suited for agency use cases.
