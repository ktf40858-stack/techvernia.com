Overview

Troops.ai was, in Salesforce’s words, “a leading provider of a revenue communications solution.” Its team spent more than seven years building tools that bring real-time data from systems like Salesforce into systems like Slack.

The goal, as described by Salesforce, was to help sales teams move faster, forecast better, gain visibility and collaborate, without leaving the chat tool where they already work.

Salesforce announced the acquisition on May 9, 2022 and completed it on July 15, 2022. Troops became part of Slack, which Salesforce also owns, and it is no longer offered as a separate product with its own plans.

Teams looking for the same idea today should look at Salesforce’s own Slack and Sales Cloud offerings. In August 2023, Salesforce launched Slack Sales Elevate, which brings Sales Cloud into Slack so sales teams can manage pipeline and receive alerts there.
