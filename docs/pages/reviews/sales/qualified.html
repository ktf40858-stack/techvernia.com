Overview

Qualified is an AI-powered pipeline generation platform built natively on Salesforce, designed to help B2B companies identify, engage, and convert high-value buyers visiting their website. Founded in San Francisco in 2018 by Salesforce veterans (CEO Kraig Swensrud was Salesforce CMO), Qualified focuses on a specific, high-value problem: when a potential customer visits your website, they represent a warm lead — but most leave without converting. Qualified catches them in real-time.

Qualified's Piper AI SDR automatically engages website visitors with personalized conversations based on their Salesforce data — knowing who they are, what they've viewed, what their company does, and what stage of the buying journey they're in. High-value accounts are instantly connected to human sales reps for live conversation while lower-priority visitors get automated nurturing.

In 2026, Qualified has expanded its AI SDR capabilities with Piper — an autonomous AI that books meetings, answers qualification questions, and initiates outreach to target accounts — all without human intervention. The platform is used by Salesforce's own sales team and hundreds of enterprise B2B companies.

Key Features

Piper AI SDR

Autonomous AI sales development representative that engages website visitors, qualifies leads, answers questions, and books meetings 24/7 without human intervention.

Real-Time Visitor Intelligence

See exactly who is on your website in real-time with full Salesforce account context. Know whether a visitor is from a target account, existing customer, or new prospect.

Instant Sales Rep Routing

Connect high-value visitors to the right sales rep instantly via live chat, voice, or video. Strike while intent is highest.

AI-Powered Meeting Booking

Piper AI books qualified meetings directly on sales rep calendars, handling scheduling and confirmation automatically.

Salesforce-Native Architecture

Built entirely on Salesforce — no data synchronization or middleware required. All engagement data flows directly into Salesforce records.

Account-Based Marketing Integration

Integrates with 6sense, Demandbase, and ABM platforms to prioritize engagement based on account signals and intent data.

How It Compares

Qualified vs Drift

Drift is a conversational marketing platform (recently acquired by Salesloft). Qualified is Salesforce-native with deeper CRM integration and AI SDR focus. For Salesforce shops, Qualified's native integration is a significant advantage.

Qualified vs Intercom

Intercom is primarily customer success and support with sales features. Qualified is purpose-built for pipeline generation on B2B websites. Different primary use cases.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Qualified has solved a real, expensive problem: website visitor anonymity and missed conversion opportunities. For enterprise B2B companies running Salesforce, Piper AI SDR transforms a previously passive website into an active, always-on pipeline generation machine. The Salesforce-native architecture eliminates integration friction, and the real-time buyer intelligence creates the right conditions for high-value conversations. The ROI case is compelling for any B2B company with significant website traffic and an enterprise deal size.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Qualified require Salesforce?+
Yes — Qualified is built natively on the Salesforce platform and requires a Salesforce subscription. This is both a feature (zero sync issues, full CRM context) and a constraint (not useful for non-Salesforce companies).
What does Piper AI SDR actually do?+
Piper is an autonomous AI that engages website visitors in real-time conversations — answering questions about your product, qualifying intent, and booking meetings on sales rep calendars. It operates 24/7 without human involvement and escalates high-priority visitors to live reps when they're available.
How does Qualified identify anonymous website visitors?+
Qualified uses IP lookup, reverse DNS, and integrations with account identification platforms (6sense, Clearbit, Demandbase) to de-anonymize website visitors — identifying their company even before they fill out a form. Known contacts are identified via CRM cookies or email link tracking.
Is Qualified worth it for mid-market companies?+
Qualified is primarily priced for enterprise. Mid-market companies (100-1000 employees) with active outbound Salesforce usage and meaningful website traffic can see strong ROI, particularly if their average deal size justifies the investment. Smaller companies often find it expensive relative to alternatives.
