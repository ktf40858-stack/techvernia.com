Overview

Clay is an AI-powered data enrichment and GTM (go-to-market) automation platform that has become the backbone of modern outbound sales operations. Founded in New York in 2021, Clay combines data from 75+ enrichment sources with an AI research agent (Claygent) to build hyper-personalized prospect lists and automate outreach at scale — making what used to require a team of SDRs achievable with a fraction of the headcount.

Clay's core innovation is its waterfall enrichment approach: instead of paying for one expensive data provider, Clay automatically queries multiple data sources in priority order, only charging when data is found. This dramatically reduces data costs while improving coverage. Add AI-powered research on top (Claygent can browse company websites, LinkedIn, and news to answer custom research questions), and the result is a prospect list with a level of depth and personalization that manual SDR research can't match at scale.

In 2026, Clay has become the default prospecting and enrichment tool for high-growth startups and enterprise sales teams alike. The platform connects to nearly every sales tool (Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Apollo, etc.) and has spawned an ecosystem of "Clay experts" — operators who build Clay workflows for clients.

Key Features

Waterfall Data Enrichment

Automatically queries 75+ data providers in priority order to find emails, phones, company data, and more. Only pay when data is found.

Claygent AI Research

AI agent that browses websites, LinkedIn, and news to answer custom research questions about prospects. "Does this company use Salesforce?" answered at scale.

AI Personalization Engine

Generate personalized email snippets, value propositions, and icebreakers at scale using enriched data and AI. Hyper-personalization for thousands of prospects.

Table-Based Workflow Builder

Spreadsheet-like interface for building enrichment and automation workflows. No-code for most use cases, formula/API support for advanced users.

CRM Integration

Bidirectional sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other CRMs. Keep prospect data current automatically.

Sequence Trigger Automation

Automatically trigger outreach sequences in connected tools (Outreach, Salesloft, Instantly) when prospects meet enrichment criteria.

How It Compares

Clay vs Apollo.io

Apollo is an all-in-one prospecting + sequencing tool. Clay is a specialized enrichment and automation layer that works with any sequencer. Clay has superior enrichment; Apollo is more self-contained.

Clay vs ZoomInfo

ZoomInfo is a traditional data provider with its own prospecting features. Clay's waterfall approach often finds better data at lower cost by combining multiple sources including ZoomInfo as one of many providers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Clay credits?+
Credits are Clay's currency — each enrichment action costs credits based on the data source used. The waterfall enrichment only charges credits when data is actually found (not for failed lookups). Credits refresh monthly based on your plan.
Do I need technical skills to use Clay?+
Clay has a spreadsheet-like interface that non-technical sales ops users can learn. For advanced automation and custom API integrations, technical skills help. Most core use cases — enrichment tables, AI research columns, CRM sync — are achievable without coding.
What is Claygent?+
Claygent is Clay's AI research agent that can browse websites, LinkedIn profiles, and news sources to answer custom questions about any prospect. You define a question (e.g., "What CRM does this company use?"), and Claygent researches each prospect automatically, returning answers at scale.
Does Clay replace my existing sales tools?+
No — Clay is a data enrichment and automation layer that enhances your existing tools. You still need a sequencer (Outreach, Instantly, etc.) for actual email sending and a CRM for pipeline management. Clay is the intelligence layer that feeds those tools with better data.
