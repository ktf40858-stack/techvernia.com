Overview

AiSDR is an AI-powered Sales Development Representative platform that automates outbound prospecting, personalized outreach, and meeting booking — functioning as an autonomous SDR that works 24/7 without the overhead of a human hire. Founded in 2023, AiSDR targets the cost and efficiency problem of traditional SDR teams: most SDRs spend only 20-30% of their time on actual selling, with the rest consumed by research, data entry, and scheduling.

AiSDR integrates natively with HubSpot and uses your CRM data to personalize outreach automatically. When a prospect replies, AiSDR handles follow-up conversations autonomously — answering objections, providing information, and booking meetings on sales rep calendars. The result is a scalable outbound motion that runs continuously without requiring full SDR headcount.

In 2026, AiSDR has expanded its AI conversation capabilities to handle more complex objections and personalization scenarios. The platform is used by SaaS companies, agencies, and B2B businesses that want to scale outbound without proportionally scaling headcount.

Key Features

Autonomous Outbound

AI researches prospects, writes personalized outreach, and sends emails/LinkedIn messages automatically. No human needed for initial contact.

AI Follow-up Conversations

When prospects reply, AiSDR handles the conversation autonomously — answering questions, handling common objections, and moving toward meeting booking.

Meeting Booking Automation

Books meetings directly on sales rep calendars via Calendly integration. Manages scheduling logistics without human intervention.

HubSpot Native Integration

Deep HubSpot integration uses existing CRM data for personalization. All activity syncs back to HubSpot records automatically.

Prospect Research

AI researches each prospect's company, role, and relevant signals to craft contextually relevant outreach.

Performance Analytics

Track email metrics, reply rates, meeting booked rates, and conversation outcomes. Identify what messaging works for which segments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AiSDR fully replace a human SDR?+
AiSDR can handle many of the tasks a human SDR performs — prospecting, initial outreach, follow-up conversations, and meeting booking. However, for complex enterprise deals, highly technical products, or sales requiring deep relationship building, human SDR skills remain important. Most companies use AiSDR for volume prospecting while human reps focus on strategic accounts.
How personalized is AiSDR's outreach?+
AiSDR uses your HubSpot data, company information, and AI research to personalize emails. Personalization is good for volume outreach but generally less detailed than a skilled human SDR with tools like Clay. The trade-off is scale: AiSDR contacts far more prospects than a human could.
Does AiSDR require HubSpot?+
AiSDR has the deepest integration with HubSpot but also supports other CRMs. HubSpot users get the best experience with bidirectional sync and CRM-powered personalization. Contact AiSDR for integration details with other CRM platforms.
What happens when a prospect replies?+
AiSDR's AI handles reply conversations autonomously — reading the prospect's message, understanding their intent (interested, objecting, asking for more info), and responding appropriately. It can answer common questions, handle objections like "not the right time" or "already have a solution," and book meetings when prospects show interest.
