Overview

FLUX is the AI image generation model suite developed by Black Forest Labs, founded in August 2024 by Robin Rombach, Andreas Blattmann, and Patrick Esser — the same researchers who created Stable Diffusion at Stability AI. With this pedigree, FLUX arrived with exceptionally high expectations, and it has consistently delivered, earning recognition as the most technically impressive image generator available in 2026.

What makes FLUX exceptional is its 12-billion parameter hybrid transformer architecture trained with rectified flow, which produces images with industry-leading prompt adherence, photorealism up to 4 megapixels, and — critically — best-in-class text rendering inside images. Accurate text in AI-generated images has long been a weakness of competing models, and FLUX solves this problem convincingly.

Black Forest Labs raised a $300M Series B from a16z, NVIDIA, and Salesforce Ventures in 2025, valuing the company at approximately $3.25 billion. The company released FLUX.2 in November 2025, pushing photorealism and typography even further. FLUX is available via API, via third-party platforms like Replicate and fal.ai, and through self-hosted enterprise deployments.

FLUX Model Family

FLUX.1 [pro]

Production-grade model optimized for low-latency commercial use. Best balance of speed and quality for API integrations.

FLUX.1 [dev]

Open-weights model for research and development. Free to self-host, ideal for fine-tuning and experimentation.

FLUX.1 [schnell]

Speed-optimized variant for rapid generation. Apache 2.0 license — fastest FLUX model, ideal for real-time applications.

FLUX.2 [pro]

Latest flagship model (Nov 2025). Superior photorealism, dramatically improved text rendering, and enhanced typography accuracy.

FLUX.1 Kontext

Context-aware editing model — edit specific parts of an image using natural language instructions. Up to 10 reference images.

FLUX Fill / Ultra

Specialized variants for inpainting (Fill) and ultra-high resolution generation (Ultra) — ideal for product and fashion photography.

Key Features

Best-in-Class Text Rendering

FLUX produces accurate, legible text inside generated images — a breakthrough that competitors like Midjourney and DALL-E still struggle with. Essential for logos, posters, and branded content.

4MP Photorealism

Generate images up to 4 megapixels with exceptional photorealistic detail. Textures, lighting, skin, fabrics — all rendered with unprecedented accuracy for an AI model.

Superior Prompt Adherence

FLUX follows complex, detailed prompts more accurately than any competing model. Specify compositions, colors, lighting conditions, and styles with precision and get exactly what you described.

Multi-Reference Control

Provide up to 10 reference images to guide generation. Control style, composition, subject appearance, and atmosphere using multiple visual references simultaneously.

Kontext Editing

Edit any image using natural language instructions. Change backgrounds, swap objects, adjust colors, remove elements — all without re-generating from scratch using the FLUX.1 Kontext model.

Open-Weight Dev Model

FLUX.1 [dev] is open-weights — downloadable, self-hostable, and fine-tunable. The developer community has built thousands of fine-tuned LoRA models available via Civitai and HuggingFace.

Enterprise Self-Hosting

Deploy FLUX on-premises or in your private cloud. Enterprise plans start at $999/month per model with 100k images included — ideal for studios and agencies with data privacy requirements.

Third-Party Platform Access

Access FLUX via Replicate, fal.ai, together.ai, and dozens of other platforms — not just the official BFL API. This gives developers maximum flexibility in how they integrate FLUX.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

FLUX is the most technically capable AI image generator available in 2026. For developers, agencies, and studios needing the highest photorealism, most accurate text rendering, and most faithful prompt following, FLUX is the clear choice. The $0.03–$0.04 per image API pricing is competitive, and the open-weight [dev] model enables fine-tuning at zero API cost.

The main trade-off is that FLUX lacks a polished consumer UI — you'll use it via the BFL API, Replicate, fal.ai, or similar platforms. If you want a beautiful, self-contained creative tool, Midjourney still wins on artistic output and user experience. But for technical excellence, brand content, marketing materials, and anything requiring accurate text in images, FLUX is unmatched.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who founded Black Forest Labs?+
Black Forest Labs was founded in August 2024 by Robin Rombach, Andreas Blattmann, and Patrick Esser — the core researchers who created Stable Diffusion while at Stability AI. Their departure and formation of BFL brought significant talent and expertise to a new venture, backed by $300M from a16z, NVIDIA, and Salesforce Ventures.
Is FLUX better than Midjourney?+
It depends on the use case. FLUX is better for photorealism, prompt adherence, text rendering, and technical precision — making it superior for commercial/brand content. Midjourney remains the leader for artistic, stylized, and aesthetically creative outputs. Many professionals use both: FLUX for client work requiring accuracy, Midjourney for creative exploration.
Can I use FLUX for free?+
The official BFL API has no free tier. However, FLUX.1 [schnell] is Apache 2.0 licensed and freely downloadable for self-hosting. FLUX.1 [dev] is open-weight for non-commercial research use. Some third-party platforms (Replicate, fal.ai) offer free trial credits that can be used with FLUX models.
What is the FLUX Kontext model?+
FLUX Kontext is a context-aware image editing model. Unlike text-to-image generation, Kontext takes an existing image and natural language instructions, then modifies the image accordingly — removing objects, changing backgrounds, swapping colors, or adjusting styles — while preserving the rest of the image. It supports up to 10 reference images for multi-reference control.
What is the difference between FLUX.1 and FLUX.2?+
FLUX.2 [pro], released November 25, 2025, improves on FLUX.1 in two key areas: photorealism (more accurate skin, materials, and lighting) and typography (even more reliable and accurate text rendering in images). FLUX.2 is available at $0.03 per megapixel — slightly different pricing from FLUX.1's flat per-image rate.
