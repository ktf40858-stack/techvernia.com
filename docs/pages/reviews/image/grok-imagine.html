Overview

Grok Imagine is xAI's image and video generation feature integrated directly into Grok — the AI assistant built by Elon Musk's xAI company and natively embedded in the X (formerly Twitter) platform. Launched in December 2024, Grok Imagine is powered by Aurora, an autoregressive mixture-of-experts model trained on interleaved text and image data, giving it a unique multimodal understanding of context.

What distinguishes Grok Imagine from competitors like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, and FLUX is its deep integration with the X social platform — available to all X users with Grok access and to SuperGrok subscribers. The Aurora model delivers photorealistic image generation, precise text rendering, and notably fewer content restrictions than competing platforms, making it popular among creators who find other tools overly conservative.

In July 2025, xAI expanded Grok Imagine to include Grok Imagine video generation — 6-second animated audiovisual clips from text prompts. The Imagine 1.0 update released February 1, 2026 improved audio quality for generated video clips. Enterprise access expanded in December 2025 with Grok Business and Enterprise tiers.

Video Generation Included: Grok Imagine launched video generation (6-second clips with audio) on July 28, 2025. The Imagine 1.0 update (Feb 1, 2026) significantly improved audio quality in generated video clips. This makes Grok Imagine one of the few image tools that also generates short video content.

Key Features

Aurora Image Generation

The Aurora model generates photorealistic images with accurate text rendering, realistic human portraits, and precise object detail. Under 5 seconds per image — comparable to Midjourney v6 generation speed.

Video Generation (6 sec)

Generate 6-second animated video clips with synchronized audio from text prompts. Imagine 1.0 (Feb 2026) delivers improved audio quality and visual coherence across frames.

X Platform Integration

Natively embedded into X (Twitter) — generate images directly from posts, DMs, and the Grok sidebar. No separate app needed for X Premium and SuperGrok subscribers.

Less Restrictive Content Policy

Grok Imagine applies fewer content restrictions than competitors like DALL-E 3 or Imagen 3, offering more creative freedom for artistic and adult-oriented creative projects on SuperGrok.

Multimodal Input

Aurora is trained on interleaved text and image data — enabling reference image input alongside text prompts for more controlled, context-aware image generation.

Developer API

Access Aurora via the xAI API (docs.x.ai) with usage-based pricing. Build image and video generation into your applications using xAI's developer platform released in March 2025.

Text in Image Accuracy

Aurora excels at generating readable, accurate text within images — useful for poster design, logo concepts, social media graphics, and branded content creation.

Enterprise Tiers

Grok Business and Enterprise plans launched December 2025 — offering higher generation limits, API access, team management, and dedicated support for organizations.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Grok Imagine occupies a unique and interesting position in the AI image generation landscape. Its deep integration with the X platform gives it a built-in distribution advantage — millions of X users can generate images directly in their social media workflow without signing up for a separate tool. The Aurora model delivers genuinely strong results, particularly for text rendering and photorealistic portraits.

The most distinctive feature is the combination of image AND video generation in one subscription — something no other image tool on this list offers. At $30/month for SuperGrok, you get access to Grok AI, image generation, and 6-second video clips — a compelling bundle if you're already a heavy X user.

However, Grok Imagine doesn't lead in any single image quality metric — FLUX is more photorealistic, Midjourney is more artistic, and DALL-E 3 has a larger developer ecosystem. Its reduced content policy, while popular with some creators, has attracted controversy. The tool is best suited for X power users and creators who want integrated social media + AI generation in one place.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between "Grok Aurora" and "Grok Imagine"?+
"Aurora" is the internal name of the AI model that powers xAI's image generation. "Grok Imagine" is the official public product name for the image and video generation feature. Tech media sometimes refers to the product as "Grok Aurora" (using the model name), but xAI officially brands the consumer-facing product as "Grok Imagine." Think of it like "ChatGPT" (product) vs "GPT-4o" (model).
Do I need X Premium to use Grok Imagine?+
Free X users have very limited Grok Imagine access with slower generation speeds. X Premium (~$8/month) provides standard image generation access through the X platform. SuperGrok ($30/month) unlocks the full Grok Imagine experience including video generation, faster speeds, and extended Grok AI features. SuperGrok Heavy ($300/month) is for heavy developers and power users.
Can Grok Imagine generate video?+
Yes — Grok Imagine generates 6-second animated video clips with audio, launched July 28, 2025. The Imagine 1.0 update (February 1, 2026) significantly improved audio quality. Videos are generated from text prompts, similar to image generation. This makes Grok Imagine one of the few image generation tools that also produces short video content.
Is there a Grok Imagine API for developers?+
Yes. xAI released the Aurora API in March 2025, available at docs.x.ai. Developers can access image generation programmatically with usage-based pricing. The API supports text-to-image generation and reference image input. Enterprise API access is available through the Grok Business and Enterprise plans launched in December 2025.
Why is Grok Imagine controversial?+
Grok Imagine has attracted controversy for applying fewer content restrictions than competitors like DALL-E 3 and Imagen 3. Critics argue this enables generation of harmful or misleading content more easily. xAI counters that responsible creative freedom is important and that the tool still prohibits clearly illegal content. Users and regulators continue to debate the appropriate balance between creative freedom and AI safety guardrails.
