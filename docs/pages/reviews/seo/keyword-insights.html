Overview

Keyword Insights is a specialized AI SEO platform that excels at a specific, high-value task: clustering thousands of keywords by SERP similarity to identify which keywords can be targeted on the same page and which need separate content. This SERP-based clustering approach is more accurate than simple semantic grouping.

With 15,000+ users including agencies and enterprise SEO teams, Keyword Insights has built a loyal following for its practical, results-focused toolset. Beyond clustering, it offers AI-powered content briefs and an AI Writer Agent that produces SEO-optimized drafts based on SERP analysis.

In 2026, Keyword Insights expanded to cover AI search signals alongside traditional Google data, helping users understand how their target topics appear in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT and Perplexity — an important addition for forward-thinking SEO strategies.

SERP-Based Keyword Clustering

How It Compares

Keyword Insights vs Surfer SEO

Surfer SEO focuses on on-page optimization and content grading. Keyword Insights focuses on keyword strategy and clustering before you start writing. They're complementary tools — many teams use both in sequence.

Keyword Insights vs SEMrush Keyword Magic

SEMrush has more data volume. Keyword Insights' SERP-based clustering is more accurate than SEMrush's semantic grouping for content consolidation decisions. For agencies specifically focused on content strategy, Keyword Insights often delivers better clustering quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes SERP-based clustering better than semantic clustering?+
Semantic clustering groups keywords by topic similarity (AI similarity scores). SERP-based clustering groups keywords by whether they trigger similar search results pages. Two keywords can be semantically similar but target different intents — showing up on different SERPs means they need separate content. SERP-based clustering is therefore more accurate for content strategy decisions.
Can I upload my own keyword lists?+
Yes — you can export keyword lists from Ahrefs, SEMrush, or any tool and upload them to Keyword Insights for clustering and brief generation.
Does the AI Writer produce publication-ready content?+
The AI Writer produces solid first drafts guided by SERP analysis, but they typically require editing, fact-checking, and personalization before publication. They significantly accelerate writing without replacing the need for human review.
Is Keyword Insights suitable for solo SEO consultants?+
Yes — the Pro plan at $99/month is well-suited for freelancers and solo SEO consultants who work with multiple clients and need to produce keyword research and content briefs efficiently at scale.
