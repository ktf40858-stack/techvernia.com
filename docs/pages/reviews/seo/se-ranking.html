Overview

SE Ranking is a comprehensive all-in-one SEO platform trusted by over 1 million users worldwide. Founded in Ukraine in 2013, it has grown into one of the most complete SEO suites on the market, offering keyword research, rank tracking, backlink analysis, site auditing, competitor intelligence, and AI-powered content tools — all under one roof.

What makes SE Ranking particularly notable in 2026 is its forward-thinking approach to AI search. The platform now tracks rankings not just in Google, but in ChatGPT Ads — an emerging channel as OpenAI monetizes its search product. Combined with SE Visible (their competitive intelligence tool), SE Ranking gives marketers a complete picture of organic, paid, and AI search visibility.

SE Ranking also bundles SE Ranking + Planable (social media management) + SE Visible (competitive intelligence) in a unified ecosystem, making it exceptional value compared to buying these tools separately. Pricing is accessible with plans starting at $55/month — significantly below Semrush and Ahrefs.

How It Compares

SE Ranking vs Semrush

Semrush has more data volume and advertising analytics depth. SE Ranking is significantly more affordable with comparable core SEO features. For most SMB and mid-market teams, SE Ranking delivers 90% of Semrush's value at 40% of the cost.

SE Ranking vs Ahrefs

Ahrefs has the best backlink database. SE Ranking is more affordable and better for content SEO with AI features. For teams prioritizing backlink analysis above all else, Ahrefs still leads. But for all-around SEO with AI capabilities, SE Ranking is highly competitive.
