Overview

Mangools AI Search Grader is one of the first dedicated tools for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — the emerging discipline of optimizing your brand and content to appear in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, and other large language model search engines. As AI search engines increasingly become the first stop for information queries, appearing in their answers is becoming as important as ranking on traditional Google SERPs.

Built by Mangools — the company behind the popular KWFinder, SERPChecker, and LinkMiner tools — AI Search Grader lets you enter your brand name or domain and see how often and how favorably it appears in AI search engine responses across the major platforms. The tool queries multiple AI engines with relevant questions in your niche and tracks whether your brand is mentioned, in what context, and how your visibility compares to competitors.

In 2026, GEO has moved from a niche concept to a mainstream priority for SEO professionals, as studies show that a significant portion of informational queries — particularly "best [product/service] for [use case]" and comparison queries — are now being answered directly by AI engines without users visiting traditional search results. Mangools AI Search Grader gives brands a clear measurement of their AI search presence and actionable data to improve it.

Key Features

Multi-Platform AI Monitoring

Tracks brand mentions and appearances across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, and Bing Copilot — giving a cross-platform picture of your AI search visibility in a single dashboard.

Brand Visibility Score

Calculates a GEO score measuring how frequently your brand appears in AI-generated responses compared to competitors in your category — giving a clear benchmark to track over time.

Competitor Comparison

Compare your AI search visibility against named competitors in your niche — seeing side by side which brands AI engines recommend most frequently for queries in your category.

Query Coverage Analysis

Shows which types of queries are driving AI mentions of your brand (comparison queries, "best for" queries, how-to queries) and which are being won by competitors — identifying GEO content gaps.

GEO Recommendations

Generates specific content and optimization recommendations to improve AI search visibility — including which topics to publish, how to structure content for AI citation, and which authoritative sources to build presence on.

Trend Tracking Over Time

Tracks changes in your AI visibility score over weeks and months — letting you measure the impact of GEO content initiatives and detect when algorithm changes in AI engines affect your brand's presence.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GEO and why does it matter?+
GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization — the practice of optimizing your brand and content to appear in AI-generated answers from engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. As users increasingly ask AI engines "what's the best tool for X?" or "compare A vs B," being mentioned in these responses drives brand awareness and purchase consideration. Studies in 2025-2026 show that AI search is capturing a significant share of informational queries that previously went to Google, making GEO a critical new discipline for digital marketers.
Which AI engines does Mangools AI Search Grader monitor?+
The tool monitors ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, and Bing Copilot as of 2026. Mangools has indicated plans to expand coverage as new AI search engines emerge. Each platform is tracked separately so you can see where your brand has strong vs. weak presence.
How can I improve my AI search visibility after seeing my score?+
The tool's GEO Recommendations section provides specific guidance, but general principles include: publishing comprehensive, factual content that AI engines can cite; building a strong presence on trusted third-party sites (G2, Capterra, TrustPilot, industry publications) that AI engines use as training and retrieval sources; earning mentions in authoritative content; ensuring your brand's Wikipedia page and structured data are accurate and complete; and creating content that directly answers the types of comparison and "best for" questions AI engines receive in your category.
Do I need a full Mangools subscription to use AI Search Grader?+
A limited free version lets you run basic brand visibility checks without a subscription. Full access to trend tracking, competitor comparison, GEO recommendations, and multi-domain monitoring requires a Mangools subscription starting at ~$29/month. For existing Mangools users, AI Search Grader is included in all paid plans at no additional cost.
