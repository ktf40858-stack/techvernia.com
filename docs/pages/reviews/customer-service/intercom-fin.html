Overview

Fin is Intercom's AI agent for customer service. It answers questions from your support content, runs multi-step workflows (Procedures) such as refunds or account updates, and hands off to human agents with full context.

Fin works natively inside the Intercom helpdesk, but it can also be added to other helpdesks, including Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot and Freshdesk. It covers voice, chat, email, Slack and social channels.

Its pricing model sets it apart: you pay $0.99 per outcome, at most once per conversation, and nothing if Fin cannot answer or the customer asks for a human. Intercom says Fin is used by more than 12,000 customers, with an average resolution rate of 76% (vendor figure).

Key Features

Works with any helpdesk

Native in Intercom, and also available for Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, Freshdesk and other helpdesks.

Procedures

Runs complex, multi-step workflows that read and write to third-party systems, such as processing refunds.

Omnichannel, including voice

Works across chat, email, voice, Slack and social; Fin Voice uses a low-latency model.

Apex AI models

Runs on Intercom's own Apex models, trained on customer service interactions.

Integrations via API, data connectors and MCP

Pulls in customer data and takes actions in your systems.

Enterprise security

SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, ISO 27701, AIUC-1 and HIPAA, with SSO, SCIM and US, EU or Australian data hosting.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Transparent, outcome-based pricing: $0.99 per outcome, no charge if Fin fails or hands off
  • Can be added to an existing helpdesk without migrating
  • 14-day free trial with no credit card
  • Broad channel coverage, including voice (Fin Voice)
  • Extensive certifications listed: SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, HIPAA
  • Teams can configure tone, knowledge and behavior without engineering help

Disadvantages

  • Costs scale with volume; high-volume teams need to model outcome charges carefully
  • Fin on another helpdesk has a minimum monthly commitment
  • Voice outcomes cost more ($1.99) and are sold through sales
  • Advanced analysis features (Pro add-on) start at $100 per workspace per month
  • Intercom helpdesk seats are an additional cost if you use Fin on Intercom

Best Use Cases

Intercom Fin Excels For:

  • Support teams that want to start with an AI agent without migrating helpdesks
  • Companies that prefer paying per resolved outcome rather than per bot license
  • Software, ecommerce, fintech and gaming companies with high chat volumes
  • Teams that want to manage the AI agent themselves without engineering support

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Very high-volume operations where per-outcome fees exceed a flat license
  • Teams that need a free plan for the long term
  • Teams that want a simple chatbot without per-use fees

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Intercom Fin cost?

$0.99 per outcome, charged at most once per conversation. You pay nothing if Fin cannot answer, fails an action or the customer asks for a human.

Can I use Fin with Zendesk or Salesforce?

Yes. Fin works with Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, Freshdesk and other helpdesks for $0.99 per outcome, with a minimum monthly commitment and no seat fees.

Does Intercom offer a free trial?

Yes. Intercom offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

What counts as an outcome?

An outcome is counted when a customer confirms the issue is resolved, does not ask for more help after Fin responds, or when Fin completes a Procedure, including handoffs.

Is Fin secure?

Intercom lists SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, ISO 27701, AIUC-1 and HIPAA, supports SSO, SCIM and 2FA, and offers data hosting in the US, EU or Australia.
