Overview

Gorgias is the leading AI-powered customer support platform purpose-built for e-commerce, with particularly deep integration with Shopify. Founded in Paris in 2015, Gorgias has grown to serve over 15,000 e-commerce merchants including Steve Madden, THINX, and Olipop. Its platform centralizes customer support across email, chat, social media DMs, and SMS, with AI automation that handles order inquiries, returns, and product questions — the most common e-commerce support scenarios.

Gorgias' strength is its e-commerce DNA. Every feature is built around the needs of online retailers: one-click order lookup, automated returns processing, social media comment management, and AI that understands product catalogs and shipping status. The Shopify integration is industry-leading — agents can see full order history, issue refunds, and update orders without leaving the Gorgias interface.

In 2026, Gorgias has expanded its AI automation capabilities significantly, with "Automate" handling a growing percentage of tickets without human intervention. The platform's AI is trained on e-commerce support patterns, making it more effective for common scenarios like "where is my order" and "I want to return this" than general-purpose AI tools.

Key Features

Shopify-Native Integration

Deep Shopify integration: view orders, issue refunds, update shipping addresses, and cancel orders directly within Gorgias. No tab switching required.

E-commerce AI Automation

AI handles "where is my order," return requests, and product questions automatically. Trained specifically on e-commerce support patterns.

Omnichannel Inbox

Unified inbox for email, live chat, Facebook/Instagram DMs, SMS, and voice. Manage all customer conversations in one interface.

Revenue Attribution

Track how support interactions drive or prevent revenue. See which conversations lead to purchases and which result in returns.

Macros & Rules Engine

Create automated response templates (macros) and rules-based automation to handle common scenarios consistently at scale.

Social Media Management

Monitor and respond to product comments and DMs on Facebook and Instagram. Never miss a customer complaint or sales opportunity in social.

How It Compares

Gorgias vs Zendesk

Zendesk is more powerful and flexible for complex enterprises. Gorgias wins for e-commerce — especially Shopify stores — with deeper commerce integration and e-commerce AI.

Gorgias vs Freshdesk

Freshdesk is a good general-purpose helpdesk. Gorgias is far superior for e-commerce with its Shopify integration and commerce-specific AI automation.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Gorgias is the definitive AI customer support platform for e-commerce. If you're running a Shopify store or DTC brand, no other platform matches the depth of Gorgias' commerce integration, e-commerce AI automation, and revenue attribution. The platform has matured significantly since 2015 and its AI automation now handles the majority of common order-related inquiries automatically. For e-commerce brands looking to scale support without scaling headcount, Gorgias is the obvious choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gorgias only for Shopify stores?+
Gorgias integrates most deeply with Shopify, but also supports WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, and other e-commerce platforms. Non-Shopify stores benefit from Gorgias but get slightly less integration depth than Shopify merchants.
What percentage of tickets can Gorgias automate?+
Gorgias customers typically achieve 20–40% automation rates for routine tickets like order status inquiries, shipping questions, and return requests. Some high-volume merchants with well-configured automation reach 50%+ automated resolution.
How does Gorgias pricing work?+
Gorgias prices by monthly ticket volume. The Starter plan covers 50 tickets/month at $10; pricing scales with volume up to the Advanced plan at $900/month for 5000 tickets. Custom pricing is available for higher volumes.
Can Gorgias manage Instagram and Facebook comments?+
Yes — Gorgias includes social media management for Facebook and Instagram, allowing teams to monitor product comments, DMs, and mentions from the same unified inbox as email and chat.
