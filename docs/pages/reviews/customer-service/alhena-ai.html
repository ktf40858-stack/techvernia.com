Overview

Alhena AI is a purpose-built AI support agent for e-commerce brands, with a depth of Shopify integration that goes far beyond what general-purpose chatbots can offer. Founded in 2023 in the US, Alhena was built on a specific insight: the overwhelming majority of e-commerce customer service volume comes from a handful of predictable request types — order status checks, return requests, exchange processing, refund inquiries, and delivery issue reports. If an AI agent can handle those autonomously — not just answer questions about them, but actually execute the transactions — support ticket volume drops dramatically.

This distinction between information retrieval and action execution is Alhena's core differentiator. Where most AI chatbots (including Tidio Lyro) can tell a customer "your return window is 30 days," Alhena can open the return, generate the return label, update the order record in Shopify, trigger the refund, and send the confirmation email — all without a human agent touching the ticket. This transforms the ROI calculation: instead of deflecting a question, Alhena is completing a workflow.

By 2026, Alhena has expanded its integration surface to include WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and a growing set of third-party tools (Loop Returns, Returnly, Gorgias, Klaviyo). The AI agent learns from every interaction, improving its ability to handle edge cases over time. For D2C brands and e-commerce operators handling hundreds or thousands of support tickets per day, Alhena represents a fundamentally different approach to support automation — one where the AI is a true agent that does work, not just an answering machine.

Key Features

Deep Shopify Integration

Native Shopify integration gives Alhena full read/write access to order data, customer records, inventory, and fulfillment status. The AI can look up, modify, and act on real order data in real-time — not just access a knowledge base.

Autonomous Returns & Refunds

When a customer requests a return, Alhena checks return eligibility against your policy, generates a prepaid return label, updates the order status in Shopify, queues the refund, and closes the ticket — without human involvement at any step.

Order Tracking & Delivery Issues

Integrates with shipping carriers to provide real-time tracking information and proactively identify delivery exceptions (delays, lost packages). When issues are detected, Alhena initiates the resolution workflow before the customer even contacts support.

Policy-Aware Decision Making

Alhena is trained on your specific return policy, warranty terms, exchange rules, and exception handling procedures — making decisions that are consistent with your policies, not generic best practices. Policy updates propagate to the AI automatically.

Multi-Channel Support

Handles support across your website chat widget, email inbox (via integrations with Gorgias, Zendesk, Helpscout), and SMS — maintaining consistent policy enforcement and resolution quality across all channels simultaneously.

Support Analytics & ROI Dashboard

Real-time dashboard tracking tickets resolved autonomously, average resolution time, escalation rate, and estimated labor hours saved — letting operations teams quantify Alhena's ROI and identify categories of tickets that still require human handling.

How It Compares

Alhena AI vs Tidio Lyro AI

Tidio Lyro is an excellent chatbot for answering questions — accessible, affordable, and fast to set up. Alhena AI is an agent that takes actions. Lyro tells a customer "here's how to initiate a return"; Alhena processes the return autonomously. For stores where support is primarily informational (FAQs, policies, product questions), Lyro is sufficient and far cheaper. For stores where support is primarily transactional (returns, exchanges, order modifications), Alhena's action-capable architecture delivers meaningfully better outcomes.

Alhena AI vs Gorgias AI

Gorgias is the dominant helpdesk for Shopify brands, and their AI features (auto-response, sentiment routing) are solid. But Gorgias AI is still fundamentally an assist-and-suggest tool for human agents — it helps agents work faster, not replace them on routine tickets. Alhena is designed to replace human handling entirely for transactional workflows. Many brands run both: Gorgias for the complex tickets that need human attention, Alhena for the high-volume routine ones.

Alhena AI vs Ada

Ada is a general-purpose enterprise AI support platform suitable for any industry, including e-commerce, but not specifically optimized for the Shopify transaction layer. Alhena's Shopify-native integration and e-commerce-specific workflow logic gives it an edge in order management and returns resolution depth that Ada's more generic platform architecture doesn't match out of the box.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Alhena AI represents where AI customer support is heading: from answering questions to completing transactions. For e-commerce brands where a significant portion of support volume is return requests, order status inquiries, and exchange processing, Alhena's ability to autonomously execute those workflows — not just respond to them — creates a qualitatively different ROI than information-retrieval chatbots. The Shopify integration depth is genuine and powerful. But for mid-to-large D2C brands ready to automate their support operations seriously, Alhena is one of the most compelling AI tools in the e-commerce ecosystem in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "deep Shopify integration" actually mean for Alhena AI?+
Alhena has read/write access to your Shopify store data — orders, customers, inventory, fulfillment, and refunds. This means the AI doesn't just tell customers information about their orders; it can modify order records, create return labels via integrated return management tools (Loop Returns, Returnly), trigger refunds through Shopify's payment system, and update order tags and notes — all in real-time without a human agent. This is meaningfully different from chatbots that only read Shopify data to answer questions.
What happens when Alhena AI encounters a case outside its decision authority?+
Alhena has configurable escalation rules that define which scenarios require human review. For example, you might configure it to autonomously approve returns under $100, but escalate returns over $100 for a manager review. When a case hits an escalation trigger, Alhena hands it off to your human agent queue (in Gorgias, Zendesk, or Helpscout) with full context — the customer's history, what Alhena attempted, and why it escalated. The agent sees everything and can resolve without asking the customer to repeat themselves.
How long does Alhena AI take to implement?+
Most Alhena implementations are live within 2-4 weeks from contract signing. The timeline includes policy configuration (training Alhena on your return rules, warranty terms, and exception handling), Shopify and helpdesk integration setup, testing with synthetic tickets across your most common scenarios, and a phased rollout starting with lower-risk ticket types before enabling full autonomous resolution. Alhena's implementation team typically manages this process with your operations lead as the primary point of contact.
Does Alhena AI work with WooCommerce and BigCommerce?+
Yes, though Shopify is Alhena's native and most deeply integrated platform. WooCommerce and BigCommerce integrations are available but may have some feature gaps compared to the Shopify version — particularly around real-time refund processing and native return label generation, which depend on Shopify's infrastructure. If you're on WooCommerce or BigCommerce, discuss the specific capabilities available for your platform during a demo to understand any limitations relative to the Shopify feature set.
