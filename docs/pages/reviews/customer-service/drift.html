Overview

Drift was a website chat and chatbot product used mainly by B2B sales and marketing teams to engage site visitors, qualify leads and route them to sellers. Salesloft acquired Drift in February 2024.

Drift is no longer a product to buy. The old drift.com address now redirects to Salesloft, whose Drift page states: "We've transitioned from Drift to 1mind." Salesloft now promotes AI chat agents from 1mind that feed buyer signals into its revenue platform.

Existing users should also know about the August 2025 security incident: Salesloft disclosed that attackers stole OAuth tokens through Drift integrations, affecting connected systems at hundreds of organizations. Salesforce disabled Drift integrations as a precaution.

Key Features

Website chat agents

Engaged website visitors in real time and answered their questions.

Lead qualification and routing

Qualified visitors and routed high-intent buyers to the right sellers.

Personalization

Used web activity, CRM data and past conversations to tailor conversations.

Salesloft integration

Passed buyer signals into Salesloft workflows such as Rhythm.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Was built specifically for B2B website conversations and lead qualification
  • Tight integration with Salesloft's sales engagement platform
  • Salesloft provides a stated migration path (1mind)

Disadvantages

  • Being phased out: Salesloft says it has transitioned from Drift to 1mind
  • Not available as a new purchase on its own website (drift.com redirects to Salesloft)
  • August 2025 breach involving stolen OAuth tokens from Drift integrations
  • Focused on sales and marketing, not full customer service

Pricing

Drift is no longer sold as a standalone product. Salesloft's Drift page directs visitors to talk to sales about its 1mind-based chat agents. No public prices are listed.

Best Use Cases

Drift Excels For:

  • Existing Salesloft customers planning their migration to 1mind
  • Teams that need historical context on Drift before switching tools

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Any new deployment, since the product is being phased out
  • Customer service teams looking for a helpdesk or support AI agent
  • Security-sensitive teams that have not yet reviewed the 2025 Drift integration breach

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Drift being discontinued?

Salesloft's Drift page states that it has transitioned from Drift to 1mind, and drift.com now redirects to Salesloft. Treat Drift as a product being phased out.

What replaces Drift?

Salesloft points customers to 1mind, whose AI chat agents feed buyer signals into Salesloft's revenue platform.

Who owns Drift?

Salesloft, which acquired Drift in February 2024.

What happened in the 2025 Drift breach?

In August 2025, Salesloft disclosed that attackers had stolen OAuth tokens through Drift integrations, affecting connected systems at hundreds of organizations. Salesloft advised customers to revoke and rotate API keys.

Was Drift a customer service tool?

Mostly not. Drift focused on B2B website conversations for sales and marketing: engaging visitors, qualifying leads and routing them to sellers.
