Overview

Cognigy, now marketed as NiCE Cognigy, is a conversational and agentic AI platform for enterprise contact centers. It is used to build AI agents that handle customer conversations over voice, chat and messaging.

The core product, Cognigy.AI, combines an AI agent studio, knowledge AI, agent evaluation, AI operations and analytics. Alongside it, Cognigy offers a voice gateway for phone connectivity and an Agent Copilot that assists human agents in real time.

Cognigy targets large contact centers in sectors such as airlines, automotive, banking, healthcare, insurance, retail, telecom and utilities, and integrates with contact center platforms including Genesys, Avaya, Amazon (AWS), Microsoft, 8x8 and NiCE CXone.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Built for large contact centers, with voice as a first-class channel
  • Integrates with the major contact center platforms (Genesys, Avaya, AWS, NiCE CXone, 8x8)
  • Covers both AI agents and human-agent assistance (Agent Copilot)
  • Free Cognigy.AI trial account available
  • Named a Leader in the Forrester Wave 2026 for Conversational AI Platforms for Customer Service (per Cognigy)

Disadvantages

  • No published pricing; enterprise deals are quote-based
  • Aimed at enterprise contact centers, which makes it heavy for small teams
  • Trial length is not stated on the signup page
  • Product direction now depends on NiCE following the 2025 acquisition
  • Compliance details sit in a trust center rather than on the main site

Pricing

Best Use Cases

Cognigy Excels For:

  • Enterprise contact centers automating high volumes of phone calls
  • Organizations already running Genesys, Avaya, NiCE CXone or Amazon Connect
  • Teams that want both AI agents and real-time assistance for human agents
  • Multilingual, multi-channel service operations

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Small businesses that need a simple website chatbot
  • Buyers who want transparent, self-serve pricing
  • Teams without technical resources to design and maintain AI agents

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cognigy part of NiCE?

Yes. Cognigy was acquired by NiCE in September 2025, and the product is now branded NiCE Cognigy.

Does Cognigy offer a free trial?

Yes. Cognigy's signup page says the Cognigy.AI trial is provided free of charge. It does not state how long the trial lasts.

How much does Cognigy cost?

Cognigy does not publish prices. Pricing is quote-based; contact sales.

Which platforms does Cognigy integrate with?

Cognigy lists integrations with Avaya, AWS, Genesys, NiCE CXone, Microsoft and 8x8.

Is Cognigy secure?

Cognigy publishes a trust center at trust.cognigy.com. See the vendor's security page for current certifications.
