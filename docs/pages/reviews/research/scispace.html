Overview

SciSpace describes itself as an end-to-end platform for discovering, analyzing and writing scientific literature. Its tools cover literature review, chat with PDF, data extraction, an AI writer, a paraphraser, a citation generator and an AI detector.

The Literature Review tool searches a library of 280M+ articles and returns tabular insights with customizable columns. You can chat with individual papers, scan many papers at once and save them to a library. SciSpace Agent and a Biomedical Agent handle multi-step research tasks.

SciSpace is run by PubGenius Inc. in Milpitas, California, and was previously known as Typeset.io. It offers a Chrome extension and a mobile app, and a free version with limits.

Key Features

Literature Review

Find, analyze and cite papers from a 280M+ article library, with results in customizable columns.

Chat with PDF

Ask questions about a paper and get simplified explanations of highlighted passages.

SciSpace Agent

Runs multi-step research tasks; a separate Biomedical Agent targets life-science work.

Extract Data

Pull structured information from multiple papers into tables.

AI Writer and Paraphraser

Draft and rework academic text, with a citation generator for references.

Chrome extension and mobile app

Use SciSpace while browsing or on your phone.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Broad toolset covering search, reading and writing
  • Free version available
  • Premium is $12/month when billed annually
  • Team plans for 2 to 300 seats
  • Chrome extension and mobile app

Disadvantages

  • Usage is credit-based: Premium includes 1,200 credits
  • Advanced ($90/month) and Max ($200/month) are expensive when billed monthly
  • The money-back guarantee only covers the first 24 hours
  • The free version's limits are only detailed on the pricing page
  • Library size claims vary across pages (270M+ to 287M)

Pricing

SciSpace has a free version and the following paid plans:

Premium ($20/mo)

Or $12/month billed annually. 1,200 credits, up to 4 concurrent tasks.

Advanced ($90/mo)

Or $70/month billed annually. Unlimited usage tier, up to 8 concurrent tasks.

Max ($200/mo)

Or $160/month billed annually. Unlimited usage tier, up to 16 concurrent tasks.

Teams

From $15 per user per month billed annually (Premium), $75 (Advanced) or $175 (Max), for 2 to 300 seats.

All plans can be cancelled anytime and carry a 100% money-back guarantee for 24 hours. Enterprise options are available through SciSpace for Enterprise.

Best Use Cases

SciSpace Excels For:

  • Students and researchers who want search, reading and writing in one place
  • Understanding difficult papers through chat and explanations
  • Extracting data from groups of papers into tables
  • Labs and teams that want shared plans

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Users who want a single, simple tool at a flat price
  • Formal systematic reviews that need documented screening
  • Heavy users who would hit Premium's credit limit

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use SciSpace for free?

Yes. SciSpace has a free version with limitations, detailed on its pricing page.

How much does SciSpace Premium cost?

$20/month, or $12/month billed annually, with 1,200 credits.

Can I get a refund?

All plans have a 100% money-back guarantee for 24 hours, and you can cancel anytime.

Are there team plans?

Yes. Team plans cover 2 to 300 seats, starting at $15 per user per month billed annually.

Is SciSpace the same as Typeset?

Yes. SciSpace was formerly Typeset.io, and typeset.io now redirects to scispace.com.

Final Verdict

4.7/5
Excellent Choice

SciSpace bundles a large paper search, chat with PDF, data extraction and writing tools into one subscription, with a usable free version. Premium is affordable on annual billing, but credits run out for heavy users and higher tiers get expensive. It suits people who want one tool for the whole research workflow.
