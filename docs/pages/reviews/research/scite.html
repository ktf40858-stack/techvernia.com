Overview

Scite helps researchers discover and evaluate scientific literature through Smart Citations. Instead of only counting citations, it shows whether later studies supported, challenged or discussed a paper.

Its Assistant answers questions with each claim linked to the specific sentence in the specific paper it came from. Full-text search goes beyond abstracts across more than 300 million articles, preprints, books, patents and datasets, and Scite says it has indexed more than 1.6 billion citations.

Scite has agreements with Wiley, SAGE and dozens of other publishers, and reports about 2 million users. It is now part of Research Solutions.

Key Features

Smart Citations

Shows whether citing papers support, contrast or simply discuss a claim.

Assistant

Research answers in which every claim links to the exact sentence in its source paper.

Full-text search

Search beyond abstracts across 300M+ articles, preprints, books, patents and datasets.

Collections, alerts and dashboards

Organize and monitor papers, with citation alerts and dashboards on every plan.

Integrations

Browser extension, Zotero plugin, an MCP server for AI agents, and an API.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Shows the context of citations, not just their number
  • Assistant claims link to exact sentences
  • Publisher agreements give access to full-text citation statements
  • Zotero plugin, browser extension, MCP and API
  • 7-day free trial on paid plans

Disadvantages

  • No permanent free plan, only a 7-day trial
  • Prices are shown for yearly billing only
  • API access requires the Pro plan ($40/month billed yearly)
  • Patent, clinical trial and regulatory datasets are reserved for higher tiers
  • Student discounts require recommending Scite to your institution

Pricing

Scite publishes these plans (prices billed yearly):

Basic ($12/mo)

Full Assistant and Search, 250 MCP credits per month, Smart Citation reports, collections up to 1,000 papers, alerts and dashboards.

Pro ($40/mo)

Everything in Basic plus API access, 2,500 MCP credits per month, collections up to 10,000 papers, patent, clinical trial and grant datasets.

Enterprise (Custom)

Unlimited users with pooled usage, SSO/IP access, collections up to 50,000 papers, regulatory datasets, extended reporting.

Basic and Pro include a 7-day free trial; you are enrolled in the chosen plan unless you cancel before it ends.

Best Use Cases

Scite Excels For:

  • Checking whether a finding has been supported or contradicted before citing it
  • Researchers who want AI answers they can verify sentence by sentence
  • Universities and companies needing institution-wide access

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Users looking for a free, permanent plan
  • Visual literature mapping
  • Formal systematic review screening

Comparison

Scite vs Competitors

vs Consensus

Consensus has a free plan and the Consensus Meter for yes/no questions, but no citation-level supporting/contrasting labels.

vs Semantic Scholar

Semantic Scholar is free and flags Highly Influential Citations, but does not classify citations as supporting or contrasting.

vs Elicit

Elicit focuses on systematic reviews and data extraction, with a free plan and Pro at $49 per user per month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Smart Citations?

They show the context of each citation, so you can see whether later research supported, challenged or only mentioned a paper.

Is Scite free?

There is no permanent free plan. Basic and Pro come with a 7-day free trial, after which you are billed unless you cancel.

How much does Scite cost?

Basic is $12/month and Pro is $40/month, both billed yearly. Enterprise is priced on users and usage.

Who owns Scite?

Scite is now part of Research Solutions.

Does Scite integrate with Zotero?

Yes. Scite offers a Zotero plugin, a browser extension, an MCP server for AI agents and an API.
