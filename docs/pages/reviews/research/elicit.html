Overview

Elicit is an AI research tool for synthesizing evidence, mapping a field and supporting decisions. It searches more than 138 million papers and backs every AI-generated claim with sentence-level citations from the source papers.

Beyond search, Elicit generates research reports through a process inspired by systematic reviews, and offers a full systematic review workflow that automates screening and data extraction. Results come back as interactive tables, with one column per piece of information you want to extract.

Its users are academic researchers, research teams in companies, and institutions running evidence reviews. Elicit says more than 5 million researchers have used it.

Key Features

Paper search

Unlimited search across 138M+ papers, with summaries and chat on full text, even on the free plan.

Research reports

Briefs generated through a systematic-review-style process, with sentence-level citations.

Systematic literature review

Automated screening and extraction: up to 5,000 papers on Pro and up to 40,000 on Enterprise.

Data extraction tables

Extract custom columns from papers or your own uploads: 20 columns on Pro, 30 on Scale.

Routines and library

Track new research on a topic and keep sources organized for later projects.

API and Zotero import

Import from Zotero on every plan; API access starts on Pro.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Every claim links to the exact sentence in the source paper
  • Strong systematic review workflow with screening and extraction
  • Free plan includes unlimited search, summaries and chat with papers
  • Zotero import and API access
  • Enterprise adds SSO/SAML, 2FA and no training on your data by default

Disadvantages

  • Pro costs $49 per user per month, more than most research assistants
  • Research Agent and reports are limited on the free plan
  • Real-time collaboration only comes with Scale at $169 per user per month
  • Screening and column limits depend on the plan
  • The pricing page names no compliance certifications

Pricing

Elicit publishes four plans on its pricing page:

Basic (Free)

Unlimited paper search, summaries and chat with papers; limited Research Agent and reports; Zotero import.

Pro ($49/user/mo)

Or $588 billed annually. Systematic reviews up to 5,000 papers, 20 columns, Routines, custom extractions, API access.

Scale ($169/user/mo)

Or $2,028 billed annually. 5x usage, figure extraction, live team collaboration, 30 columns, admin panel.

Enterprise (Custom)

Up to 40,000 papers and 40 columns, custom data sources, unlimited API, SSO/SAML and dedicated support.

Best Use Cases

Elicit Excels For:

  • Systematic and scoping reviews that need screening and data extraction
  • Research teams building evidence tables across hundreds of papers
  • Policy, health and R&D teams preparing evidence briefs
  • Researchers who need every AI claim traceable to a sentence

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Casual users who only need occasional paper search on a budget
  • Small teams that need collaboration but not the Scale price
  • Work that depends on web sources outside the academic literature

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Elicit free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free and includes unlimited paper search, summaries and chat with papers, with limited Research Agent and report usage.

Can Elicit run a systematic review?

Yes. Pro supports screening up to 5,000 papers with data extraction; Enterprise goes up to 40,000 papers.

How many papers does Elicit search?

More than 138 million academic papers.

Does Elicit cite its sources?

Yes. Every AI-generated claim comes with sentence-level citations from the underlying papers.

Is my data secure?

Enterprise lists SSO/SAML, 2FA, domain verification, single-tenant options and no training on your data by default. For certifications, see the vendor's security page.
