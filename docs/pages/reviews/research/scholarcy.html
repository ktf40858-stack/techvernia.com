Overview

Scholarcy helps you summarize, analyze and organize your reading. It converts long, complex texts into interactive summary flashcards that highlight the key information, in the same structured format for every document.

It accepts PDFs, book chapters, articles, studies and plain text, and imports from Zotero, Google Drive and YouTube. You can adjust a summary's depth from a single sentence to a researcher-level overview, highlight and annotate, and export flashcards to other apps or a formatted bibliography.

Scholarcy is a UK company (Scholarcy Limited, England and Wales) aimed at students and researchers. It says more than 600,000 people use it.

Key Features

Flashcard summaries

Interactive summaries of papers, chapters, articles and videos in a consistent structure.

Enhance and Spotlight

Change summary depth from one sentence to researcher level, and jump straight to key findings.

Highlights and notes

Highlight, annotate and take notes, then pick up where you left off.

Zotero import

Convert a Zotero library into flashcards for faster article screening.

Literature Matrix

Compare papers side by side and export to Excel and other formats.

One-click bibliographies

Export flashcards to a citation manager or generate a formatted bibliography in Word.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Accepts many sources, including Zotero, Google Drive and YouTube
  • Consistent flashcard format makes papers easy to compare
  • Literature Matrix and one-click bibliographies
  • 7-day free trial of the paid plan
  • Summaries stay in your account if you cancel

Disadvantages

  • The free summarizer is heavily limited
  • It summarises documents you already have; it is not a paper search engine
  • Prices are localized and only shown on the live pricing page
  • No team or institution plan on the pricing page
  • Bulk export is capped at 100 flashcards at a time

Pricing

Scholarcy has a free summarizer and one paid plan, billed monthly or yearly.

Free Article Summarizer ($0)

Import a range of file formats, with a limited number of summaries and single-flashcard export.

Scholarcy Plus (monthly)

Unlimited summaries, enhanced summaries, saved flashcards, notes, collections, Literature Matrix and bibliographies. 7-day free trial.

Scholarcy Plus (yearly)

The same features with a 25% discount compared with monthly billing.

Scholarcy shows prices in your local currency on its pricing page, so we do not quote a figure here.

Best Use Cases

Scholarcy Excels For:

  • Students who need to get through many papers quickly
  • Screening a Zotero library before deep reading
  • Building a comparison matrix for a literature review
  • Creating revision notes from papers, chapters and lectures

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Finding new papers on a topic
  • Teams that need shared workspaces and central billing
  • Systematic reviews with formal screening protocols

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Scholarcy free?

There is a free Article Summarizer with a limited number of summaries. Unlimited summaries require Scholarcy Plus.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Scholarcy Plus is free for the first 7 days, and you can cancel before you are billed.

What can I summarize?

PDFs, book chapters, articles, studies, plain text, YouTube videos, and documents from Zotero or Google Drive.

Does Scholarcy work with Zotero?

Yes. You can import your Zotero library and convert it into flashcards.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep your summaries and can resubscribe at any time.
