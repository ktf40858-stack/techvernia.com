Overview

Maki is a Paris-based AI-powered skills assessment and video interview platform designed to help companies evaluate candidates based on skills and potential rather than CV credentials. The platform creates customized skill assessments, analyzes video interview responses, and provides objective candidate scoring — reducing the role of unconscious bias in early hiring screening. Maki's French origins and European focus give it strong GDPR compliance and sensitivity to European hiring regulations.

Maki's core philosophy is "skills-first" hiring: moving away from credential-based filtering (university name, prior employer prestige) toward objective measurement of the skills actually needed for the role. Assessments can test cognitive abilities, technical skills, personality traits, and role-specific competencies, all within a positive candidate experience.

In 2026, Maki has grown across Europe and expanded internationally, serving companies from startups to enterprises that want to make hiring more objective, faster, and fairer. The platform's AI analysis of video responses evaluates communication skills and consistency with responses, while specifically avoiding analysis of facial features or demographic characteristics.

Key Features

Custom Skills Assessments

Build assessments tailored to any role: cognitive ability tests, technical skills, situational judgment, personality, and role-specific competencies.

AI Video Interview Analysis

Analyzes candidate video responses for communication quality, response coherence, and engagement — without analyzing appearance or demographic features.

Bias Reduction Tools

Blind CV screening, structured assessment scoring, and bias awareness reporting help hiring teams make more objective decisions.

Candidate Experience Focus

Assessments designed to be engaging and respectful of candidates' time. Positive experience improves employer brand even for rejected candidates.

GDPR-Compliant Design

Built for European data protection requirements. Candidate data handling, retention policies, and consent management meet GDPR standards.

Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive reporting on assessment results, pipeline diversity, time-to-screen, and predictive validity of assessments for job performance.

How It Compares

Maki vs HireVue

HireVue is a larger US-based video interview platform. Maki has stronger GDPR compliance, less controversy around facial analysis (Maki explicitly avoids it), and better candidate experience focus.

Maki vs TestGorilla

TestGorilla specializes in skills testing with a large question library. Maki combines skills assessment with video interviews and has stronger bias-reduction features.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Maki represents the European approach to AI-powered hiring — rigorous, fair, and privacy-conscious. The skills-first philosophy, combined with explicit avoidance of problematic AI analysis (facial recognition, demographic inference), addresses the legitimate concerns about AI in hiring. For European companies wanting to improve hiring quality while meeting GDPR requirements and reducing bias, Maki is one of the most thoughtfully designed platforms available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Maki analyze facial expressions in video interviews?+
No — Maki explicitly avoids analyzing facial features, appearance, or demographic characteristics in video interview analysis. It focuses on communication quality, response coherence, and verbal content — avoiding the ethical and legal concerns associated with facial analysis AI.
Is Maki GDPR compliant?+
Yes — Maki is designed from the ground up for GDPR compliance. This includes proper consent management, defined data retention policies, candidate data access rights, and processing agreements. It's well-suited for European companies with strict data protection requirements.
How does Maki reduce bias in hiring?+
Maki reduces bias through blind CV screening, structured assessment scoring (same criteria for all candidates), objective skill measurement, and bias awareness reporting that shows hiring managers where decisions may be influenced by non-relevant factors.
Can Maki integrate with our ATS?+
Yes — Maki integrates with major ATS platforms and HRIS systems. Assessment results and candidate scores flow automatically into your existing hiring workflow.
