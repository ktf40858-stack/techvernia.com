Overview

Workable is an applicant tracking system and HR platform. The ATS handles requisitions, sourcing, pipelines, interviews, offers, a careers page builder and a talent CRM, and posts jobs to 200+ job boards. The HR side adds onboarding, an employee portal, time off, time tracking, performance reviews, e-signatures and payroll preparation.

Its AI is the Workable Agent, which sources passive candidates from a pool of over 400 million profiles, screens applicants against up to 14 job criteria, messages them to fill gaps and builds shortlists with visible reasoning that recruiters can override. It runs on AI credits.

Workable says 35,000+ teams use it. It offers 270+ integrations, an MCP server and a REST API, mobile apps for iOS and Android, and states ISO 27001, ISO 27017, SOC 2 Type II and SOC 3 certifications.

Pricing

Workable publishes its prices. Plans are priced by company size, from 1-20 up to 1,000+ employees; the figures below are those displayed on its pricing page:

Standard: $299/mo

Monthly or annual billing. ATS, sourcing suite, unlimited active jobs (fair use), HRIS, onboarding, time off and time tracking, payroll preparation.

Premier: $599/mo

Billed annually. Everything in Standard plus texting, video interviews, assessments, referrals, and performance and engagement.

Enterprise: $719/mo

Billed annually. Same inclusions as Premier.

Workable Agent uses AI credits: 1 credit per candidate evaluated, 2 per passive candidate sourced, 10 per chat. Every paid account starts with 3,000 free credits; packs cost $600 for 5,000, $1,000 for 10,000 and $4,750 for 50,000. Standard add-ons: Texting+ $89/mo, Video interviews+ $109/mo, Assessments+ $59/mo, Performance and Engagement+ $39/mo. Free trial: 15 days of the Standard plan, no credit card, Workable Agent not included.

Best Use Cases

Workable AI Excels For:

  • Small and mid-size companies that want an ATS and HR system in one tool
  • Teams that want AI sourcing and screening without a separate vendor
  • Buyers who want to test with a free trial and see prices up front
  • Companies hiring across countries (9 native languages)

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Large enterprises with complex, global HCM requirements
  • Teams that only need a sourcing database
