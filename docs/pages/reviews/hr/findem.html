Overview

Findem sells people intelligence software for hiring, retention and workforce planning. It turns career histories and relationships into labeled signals, which it calls Success Signals, Relationship Signals and 3D Data, and uses them for search and matching.

Its Talent Solutions cover the whole funnel: job boards and multichannel sourcing, a talent CRM, authenticity and ID verification, AI and one-way video interviews, coding simulations and assessments, scheduling, analytics and learning.

Two other product lines set it apart. Embedded Solutions let job boards and HR platforms build on Findem's data and agents through an API and MCP. Findem Studio is an agent platform where users run pre-built agents, build their own, or call Studio's MCPs from the AI tool they already use.

Multichannel Sourcing

Search and source candidates across channels, with calibration and veteran sourcing agents.

Engage pipelines and map relationships between candidates and your network.

Assessments, coding simulations and an AI assessment assistant.

Findem Studio

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Sourcing, engagement, verification, interviews and assessments in one suite
  • Built-in candidate fraud and identity checks
  • Studio agents can be tried for free
  • API and MCP access for partners and in-house AI tools
  • Outcome-based pricing for agentic AI

Disadvantages

  • No price list beyond outcome-based pricing for agents
  • Very broad product range can be hard to scope
  • Homepage does not name security certifications; you must check the Trust Center
  • Specific integrations are not named on the homepage
  • Performance claims such as 24x faster sourcing are vendor-reported

Pricing

Findem does not publish a price list. Its agentic AI is priced on outcomes ("You pay for results, not clicks"), and anyone can try an expert-built Studio agent for free. For the talent suite, pricing is quote-based; contact sales.

Best Use Cases

Findem Excels For:

  • Talent acquisition teams that want sourcing, verification and interviews from one vendor
  • Executive search and VC or PE talent teams that rely on network and relationship data
  • Job boards and HR platforms that want to embed sourcing data through an API or MCP
  • Teams worried about candidate fraud in remote hiring

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Small companies that only need a basic ATS
  • Buyers who need fixed, published pricing

Comparison

Findem vs Competitors

  • vs SeekOut: SeekOut focuses on sourcing and applicant evaluation, publishes a $149/month entry plan and states SOC 2 Type II; Findem adds verification, interviewing and assessment tools.
  • vs HireVue: HireVue is centered on interviewing and assessments; Findem starts from sourcing data and adds interviews on top.
  • vs Fetcher: Fetcher is limited to sourcing and outreach, and Juicebox acquired its assets in October 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Findem?

A people intelligence platform that uses labeled career and relationship data for sourcing, engagement, verification, interviewing, assessment and workforce planning.

What is Findem Studio?

Findem's agent platform. You can run a pre-built agent, build your own, or call Studio's MCPs from another AI tool.

Does Findem have a free trial?

Anyone can try an expert-built Studio agent for free. The full talent suite is sold through demos.

How is Findem priced?

Agentic AI is priced on outcomes. For the rest of the suite, pricing is quote-based.

Does Findem help with candidate fraud?

Yes. Its Authenticity Suite includes AI proctoring and an ID Verify Agent.

Is Findem secure?

Findem links to a Trust Center and a Responsible AI page; see the vendor's security page for current certifications.
