Overview

Sense (Sense Talent Labs) sells a recruiting automation and engagement platform that connects to your applicant tracking system. It pitches the product as turning an ATS into an active talent engine that screens, matches and engages candidates.

The platform includes workflows and database cleanup, interview scheduling, a talent CRM, mass texting over SMS and WhatsApp, an AI chatbot for job matching and pre-screening, candidate matching, career sites, referrals and analytics. Its AI Recruiter line adds AI agents, an AI Interviewer and, for staffing, an AI Receptionist.

Sense serves both corporate talent acquisition teams and staffing firms. Integrations include Bullhorn, JobDiva, Avionte, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, iCIMS, Greenhouse and Salesforce, and named customers include Dell, HCA Healthcare, ManpowerGroup and Insight Global.

Bullhorn, JobDiva, Avionte, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, iCIMS, Greenhouse and more.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Published starting prices for every package
  • Strong fit for staffing firms on Bullhorn, JobDiva or Avionte
  • SMS and WhatsApp messaging built in
  • Each package includes a customer success manager, implementation manager and 24/7 support
  • Voice AI and AI agents for screening and scheduling

Disadvantages

  • Prices are starting points, not final quotes
  • No free trial stated
  • Full platform starts at $2,000 to $7,000 a month, out of reach for small teams
  • Security page does not name its own SOC 2 report type
  • Value depends on the quality of your ATS data

Best Use Cases

Sense Excels For:

  • Staffing and recruiting agencies running Bullhorn, JobDiva or Avionte
  • Corporate talent teams in healthcare, manufacturing and logistics hiring at volume
  • Teams that want to re-engage candidates already in their ATS

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Small businesses with modest hiring budgets
  • Teams without an ATS to connect it to

Comparison

Sense vs Competitors

  • vs Paradox: Paradox's Olivia also automates frontline screening and scheduling by text and is now part of Workday; it does not publish prices.
  • vs Humanly: Humanly offers an AI recruiter, AI interviewer and its own ATS for hourly hiring, with quote-based pricing.
  • vs Workable: Workable is a full ATS and HR platform starting at $299/month; Sense is an engagement layer on top of an existing ATS.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Sense do?

It adds automation, texting, an AI chatbot, scheduling, matching and AI recruiting agents on top of your ATS.

How much does Sense cost?

Published starting prices range from $500/month (messaging or scheduling) to $7,000/month (Talent Acquisition platform).

Is Sense built for staffing firms?

Yes. It has dedicated staffing packages and integrates with Bullhorn, JobDiva, Avionte and Labor Edge.

Does Sense offer a free trial?

No free trial is mentioned; the site offers demos and a pricing and RFP request form.

Is Sense secure?

Its security page shows an AICPA SOC logo without naming the report; see the vendor's security page or ask for details.

Final Verdict

4.7/5
Excellent Choice

Sense is a mature engagement and automation layer for staffing firms and volume talent teams, with the rare benefit of published starting prices. It needs a well-kept ATS underneath and a budget in the thousands per month.
