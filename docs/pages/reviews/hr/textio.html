Overview

Textio is an AI writing tool for HR. It gives real-time guidance on job posts, recruiting messages and performance feedback, aiming to remove biased or off-brand language and make the writing more effective.

There are two main products. Textio Recruiting helps talent teams write job posts and outreach that match the employer brand without turning candidates away. Textio Feedback helps managers write useful, growth-oriented feedback and lets HR hold them accountable for it. A newer Interview Intelligence product is offered under the Lavalier name.

Textio says its AI uses 30+ models trained on more than one billion HR documents. It runs on textio.com or as an extension inside an ATS or performance management tool, and the company says 25% of the Fortune 500 have used it.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Free trial account available
  • Focused tool that improves job posts and feedback quality
  • Trained on a very large set of HR documents
  • Works inside existing ATS and performance tools
  • Security page states ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II

Disadvantages

  • No published prices; custom packages via sales
  • Writing guidance only, not a sourcing or tracking system
  • Interview Intelligence sits under a separate brand (Lavalier)
  • Specific ATS integrations are not named on the homepage
  • Value depends on teams actually using the suggestions

Pricing

Textio offers a free trial account and custom packages through sales. It does not publish prices; pricing is quote-based.

Best Use Cases

Textio Excels For:

  • Talent teams that publish many job posts and want consistent, inclusive language
  • HR teams that want better-quality performance feedback from managers
  • Employers working on employer brand and diversity goals

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Teams looking for sourcing, screening or an ATS
  • Very small companies that rarely write job posts

Comparison

Textio vs Competitors

  • vs Workable: Workable is a full ATS and HR platform with an AI recruiting agent; Textio only improves the writing and plugs into an ATS.
  • vs Phenom: Phenom covers career sites, CRM and onboarding end to end; Textio is a specialized writing layer for posts and feedback.
