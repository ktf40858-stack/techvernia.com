Trend Micro Vision One Review

TrendAI (Trend Micro) • Enterprise security platform: XDR, SIEM, exposure management

★★★★★4.5/5
Updated: October 2026
AI-Powered
Update — October 2026: Trend Micro announced on March 23, 2026 that its enterprise cybersecurity business now operates as TrendAI, and the platform is now named TrendAI Vision One. Since January 2026, all Vision One licenses use a credit-based model (TrendAI Flex).

Overview

TrendAI Vision One, formerly Trend Micro Vision One, is Trend Micro's enterprise security platform. In March 2026 Trend Micro renamed its enterprise business TrendAI, describing the change as a move from a portfolio of products to a unified enterprise AI cybersecurity platform.

The platform groups Cyber Risk Exposure Management, Security Operations (XDR, Agentic SIEM and SOAR), Threat Intelligence and AI Security, which protects AI applications and agentic systems from development to production. It also covers cloud, data, email, endpoint, identity and network security.

Vision One is licensed with TrendAI Flex credits: one license whose credits can be allocated and reallocated across more than 30 Vision One solutions. Credits are bought from Trend sales or through the AWS and Azure marketplaces. A 30-day platform trial can be requested online or through AWS Marketplace, and several solutions, including Endpoint Security, offer their own 30-day trials.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • One platform for endpoint, email, cloud, identity, network and SOC tools
  • Credits can be moved between more than 30 solutions
  • Credits can be bought on the AWS and Azure marketplaces
  • 30-day trials for the platform and for individual solutions

Disadvantages

  • No public price list for credits
  • Credit consumption has to be estimated with Trend's calculator
  • Platform trials require approval before activation
  • Product names are in transition between Trend Vision One and TrendAI Vision One

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based and expressed in credits. Trend does not publish the dollar price of a credit; the total depends on which solutions you enable and how many endpoints, users or cloud resources they cover.

TrendAI Flex credits

The only license sold since January 2026. Credits are allocated to services and return to the balance when a service is disabled.

Cloud Risk Management

Tiered by resource count: from 1,000 credits for up to 500 resources, capped at 8,000 credits per account.

Where to buy

From Trend sales and partners, or through AWS Marketplace and Azure Marketplace.

Free trials

30-day platform trial on request; 30-day trials for Endpoint Security, Sandbox Analysis and Zero Trust Secure Access.

Best Use Cases

Ideal For:

  • Existing Trend Micro customers consolidating endpoint, email and cloud tools
  • SOC teams that want XDR, SIEM and SOAR in the same console
  • Organizations buying security through AWS or Azure marketplace commitments
  • Teams that want to shift budget between security functions over time

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Buyers who need a fixed, published per-endpoint price
  • Small teams that only need antivirus
  • Organizations that require fully on-premises management

Frequently Asked Questions

Was Trend Micro Vision One renamed?

Yes. Since Trend Micro's enterprise business became TrendAI in March 2026, the platform is called TrendAI Vision One.

How is it licensed?

With TrendAI Flex credits. Since January 2026 all Vision One licenses have moved to this credit model, and existing licenses were converted automatically.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A 30-day platform trial can be requested online or through AWS Marketplace; Endpoint Security and other solutions also have 30-day trials.

What does the platform cover?

Exposure management, XDR, SIEM and SOAR, threat intelligence, AI security, and cloud, data, email, endpoint, identity and network security.

Can I buy through a cloud marketplace?

Yes. Credits can be bought through AWS Marketplace and Azure Marketplace as well as from Trend sales.
