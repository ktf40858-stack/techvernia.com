Autonomous Response

Overview

Darktrace is a pioneering AI cybersecurity company that uses self-learning AI to detect and respond to cyber threats across the entire digital infrastructure. Unlike signature-based solutions, Darktrace learns the "pattern of life" for every user, device, and network to identify anomalous behavior.

The platform's flagship Antigena technology provides autonomous response capabilities, taking action to neutralize threats in real-time without human intervention. This is particularly valuable for fast-moving attacks where human response times are insufficient.

Darktrace covers network, email, cloud, endpoints, and OT/IoT environments, providing unified visibility and protection across the entire digital estate.

Key Features

Self-Learning AI

Unsupervised machine learning models the normal behavior of every entity in your organization.

Antigena Response

Autonomous response takes surgical action to contain threats in seconds without disrupting business.

Email Protection

AI-powered email security stops phishing, BEC, and account takeover attacks.

Cloud & SaaS

Native protection for AWS, Azure, GCP, Microsoft 365, and other cloud services.

Network Detection

Full visibility into east-west traffic, encrypted traffic analysis, and lateral movement detection.

Cyber AI Analyst

AI investigator automatically triages alerts and produces human-readable incident reports.
