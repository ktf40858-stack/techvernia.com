McAfee MVISION Review

Former McAfee enterprise security line, now Trellix and Skyhigh Security

★★★★★4.4/5
Updated: October 2026
AI-Powered
Update — October 2026: McAfee MVISION is no longer sold under that name. Symphony Technology Group (STG), which agreed in March 2021 to buy McAfee’s enterprise business, merged it with FireEye and launched the combined company as Trellix in January 2022. In March 2022 STG launched the cloud security portfolio (including MVISION Cloud) as a separate company, Skyhigh Security. On the endpoint side, MVISION EDR became Trellix EDR and MVISION ePO became Trellix ePolicy Orchestrator - SaaS.

Overview

McAfee MVISION was the brand McAfee used for its cloud-managed enterprise products, including MVISION Endpoint, MVISION EDR, MVISION ePO and MVISION Cloud. The brand has been retired: the endpoint products are now sold by Trellix and the cloud security products by Skyhigh Security.

Trellix’s endpoint offering centers on Trellix Endpoint Security (ENS), a single agent that Trellix says covers on-premises, cloud and disconnected environments. It is managed from Trellix ePolicy Orchestrator (ePO), either installed on premises or as ePO - SaaS, a multi-tenant service that Trellix hosts on AWS and keeps upgraded. ePO - SaaS also manages Trellix EDR and Trellix Endpoint, which adds zero-day detection to Microsoft Defender Antivirus on managed Windows 10 systems.

In April 2023 Trellix launched its Endpoint Security Suite, which combines ENS, Trellix Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) and Trellix Endpoint Forensics and connects to the Trellix XDR engine. Trellix does not publish prices; the products are sold through Trellix sales and partners on quote.

Key Features

Trellix Endpoint Security (ENS)

One agent for prevention across on-premises, cloud and disconnected environments.

Trellix EDR

Formerly MVISION EDR (rebranded in version 4.1.0); endpoint detection and investigation.

ePolicy Orchestrator

One console to deploy agents, set security policies, monitor and respond to events.

ePO - SaaS

Formerly MVISION ePO; multi-tenant management service run by Trellix on AWS.

Endpoint Forensics

Part of the Endpoint Security Suite, aimed at root-cause analysis of threats.

Trellix Endpoint

Adds zero-day detection to Microsoft Defender Antivirus on managed Windows 10 systems.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Central policy management through ePO, on premises or as SaaS
  • One agent for on-premises, cloud and disconnected systems
  • Suite combines prevention, EDR and forensics from one vendor
  • Natively connected to the Trellix XDR engine

Disadvantages

  • The MVISION brand is retired, so documentation mixes old and new product names
  • No published pricing and no self-service purchase
  • Former MVISION Cloud (CASB, secure web gateway) is now sold by a separate company, Skyhigh Security

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based. Trellix does not publish list prices for its endpoint products.

What determines the price

The products included (ENS alone or the suite with EDR and Forensics), the management option (ePO on premises or SaaS) and the number of systems covered.

How to buy

Through Trellix sales or a Trellix partner.

Former MVISION Cloud customers deal with Skyhigh Security, which sells the secure service edge portfolio separately.

Best Use Cases

Best For:

  • Organizations that already manage endpoints with McAfee or Trellix ePO
  • Environments with disconnected or isolated systems that still need managed protection
  • Teams that want endpoint prevention, EDR and forensics from a single vendor

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Buyers looking for the former MVISION Cloud products (now Skyhigh Security)
  • Small businesses that want published prices and online checkout

Comparison with Competitors

Where the former MVISION products sit today

  • vs Sophos Endpoint: Sophos publishes a 30-day free trial and sells per user; Trellix endpoint products are quote-only.
  • vs Microsoft Sentinel: Sentinel is a SIEM rather than an endpoint agent; it can ingest alerts from endpoint tools, while Trellix connects its suite to its own XDR engine.
  • vs Skyhigh Security: not a competitor but the other half of the old MVISION line, covering CASB, secure web gateway and zero trust network access.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happened to McAfee MVISION?

McAfee’s enterprise business was acquired by STG, merged with FireEye and relaunched as Trellix in January 2022. The cloud security products were spun out as Skyhigh Security in March 2022.

What replaced MVISION EDR and MVISION ePO?

Trellix EDR and Trellix ePolicy Orchestrator - SaaS.

Is MVISION Cloud part of Trellix?

No. MVISION Cloud belongs to the Skyhigh Security portfolio.

How is it managed?

Through Trellix ePolicy Orchestrator, installed on premises or used as the SaaS service hosted by Trellix.

How much does it cost?

Trellix does not publish prices. Request a quote from Trellix or a partner.
