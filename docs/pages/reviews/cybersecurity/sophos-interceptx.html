Sophos Intercept X Review

by Sophos • Endpoint protection, now sold as Sophos Endpoint

★★★★★4.7/5
Updated: October 2026
AI-Powered
Update — October 2026: Sophos’s product pages now present the endpoint protection long sold as Intercept X under the name Sophos Endpoint, which is included in Sophos EDR, XDR and MDR. In July 2026 Sophos announced Sophos Fusion, which it describes as the evolution of Sophos Central; Sophos Endpoint is now managed from Sophos Fusion, with several Fusion components reaching general availability between August and October 2026.

Overview

Sophos Endpoint, the product long marketed as Intercept X, is Sophos’s endpoint protection. Sophos says multiple AI models identify known and never-seen malware before execution, more than 60 exploit mitigations are enabled by default on every running process, and CryptoGuard automatically reverts files encrypted by ransomware.

Adaptive Attack Protection raises the level of protection when Sophos detects an active attacker, and Account Health Check flags posture drift and high-risk misconfigurations. The product supports Windows, macOS and Linux, and an optional add-on, Sophos Endpoint for Legacy Platforms, covers older operating systems.

Sophos Endpoint is managed in Sophos Fusion and is included in higher tiers: Sophos EDR, Sophos XDR and Sophos MDR, a 24/7 managed service. Sophos uses per-user pricing without public list prices, sells through partners and AWS Marketplace, and offers a 30-day free trial.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • 30-day free trial
  • Automatic rollback of ransomware-encrypted files with CryptoGuard
  • Exploit mitigations active by default
  • Same console as other Sophos products
  • Clear path from endpoint protection to EDR, XDR and managed MDR

Disadvantages

  • No public prices; quotes come through Sophos sales or partners
  • Detection and response tools require Sophos EDR or higher
  • Coverage for legacy operating systems is a separate add-on
  • The move from Sophos Central to Sophos Fusion means updated interfaces and documentation

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based and per user. Sophos offers these tiers:

Sophos Endpoint

Endpoint protection (formerly marketed as Intercept X).

Sophos EDR

Adds endpoint detection and response.

Sophos XDR

Adds visibility across the wider attack surface, including third-party security tools.

Sophos MDR

Managed detection and response by Sophos analysts 24/7; includes Sophos XDR.

Legacy Platforms add-on

Optional protection for critical systems on older operating systems.

Sophos describes its pricing as per user with no hidden extras. Buy through a Sophos partner, request a quote, or purchase through AWS Marketplace.

Best Use Cases

Best For:

  • Organizations that want strong anti-ransomware and exploit protection
  • Businesses planning to add EDR, XDR or a managed MDR service later
  • Companies already using other Sophos products, such as firewalls
  • Environments with legacy Windows or Linux systems (with the add-on)

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Buyers who want published, self-service pricing
  • Teams that need detection and response but only budget for the base tier

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Intercept X still available?

Yes. Sophos’s pages now call the product Sophos Endpoint, and it is included in Sophos EDR, XDR and MDR.

What is Sophos Fusion?

Sophos’s management and security operations platform, announced in July 2026 as the evolution of Sophos Central.

How is it priced?

Per user, on quote from Sophos or a partner. It is also available through AWS Marketplace.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, a free 30-day trial of Sophos Endpoint.

How does the ransomware protection work?

CryptoGuard detects malicious encryption based on behavior and automatically reverts affected files to their original state.
