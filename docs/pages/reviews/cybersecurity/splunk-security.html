Splunk Security Review

by Splunk, a Cisco company • SIEM and security operations

★★★★☆4.7/5
Updated: October 2026
Enterprise
Update — October 2026: Cisco completed its acquisition of Splunk on March 18, 2024, paying $157 per share in cash, about $28 billion in equity value. Splunk security products are still sold under the Splunk name.

Overview

Splunk’s security offering centers on Splunk Enterprise Security (ES), a SIEM built on the Splunk platform, with Splunk SOAR for automation and Splunk Attack Analyzer for automated threat analysis. Searches and detections are written in Splunk’s Search Processing Language (SPL), and ES uses risk-based alerting to group risky activity before raising alerts.

ES is sold in two editions. The Essentials Edition includes SIEM, threat intelligence, Detection Studio, Exposure Analytics and AI features such as the AI Assistant. The Premier Edition adds SOAR, user and entity behavior analytics (UEBA) and automated threat analysis.

Splunk does not publish ES prices. It offers activity-based, workload or ingest pricing for ES, on quote. Splunk SOAR integrates with more than 300 third-party tools, supports more than 2,800 automated actions, can be deployed in the cloud, on premises or hybrid, and has a free trial.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Three pricing models: activity-based, workload or ingest
  • Premier Edition bundles SOAR, UEBA and automated threat analysis
  • SOAR deployable in the cloud, on premises or hybrid
  • SPL is a powerful language for search and detection

Disadvantages

  • No published prices
  • SOAR and UEBA only in the Premier Edition
  • ES offers an interactive tour rather than a self-service trial
  • SPL takes time to learn

Best Use Cases

Best For:

  • Large security operations centers with high data volumes
  • Organizations already running Splunk for IT or observability data
  • Teams that want SIEM, SOAR and UEBA from one vendor (Premier)
  • Analysts and engineers skilled in SPL

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Small teams that need published, predictable pricing
  • Buyers who want SOAR or UEBA without the Premier Edition

Comparison with Competitors

Splunk Enterprise Security vs Alternatives

  • vs Microsoft Sentinel: Sentinel publishes per-GB and commitment-tier pricing, offers a 31-day free trial and ingests several Microsoft sources for free; Splunk ES is quote-based with activity, workload or ingest pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Splunk part of Cisco?

Yes. Cisco completed its acquisition of Splunk on March 18, 2024.

What is the difference between ES Essentials and Premier?

Both include SIEM, threat intelligence, Detection Studio, Exposure Analytics and AI features. Premier adds SOAR, UEBA and automated threat analysis.

How is Splunk Enterprise Security priced?

On quote, with activity-based, workload or ingest pricing.

Is there a free trial?

Splunk SOAR has a free trial; Enterprise Security offers an interactive tour.

What is risk-based alerting?

An ES approach that attributes risk to users and systems and alerts when accumulated risk crosses a threshold, rather than alerting on every single event.

Final Verdict

4.7/5
Excellent

Splunk Enterprise Security remains a heavyweight SIEM for large SOCs, now backed by Cisco. The Premier Edition brings SOAR and UEBA into one package, and the choice of activity, workload or ingest pricing gives flexibility. The trade-offs are quote-only pricing, no self-service ES trial and the time needed to master SPL.
