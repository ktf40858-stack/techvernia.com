Okta Review

by Okta, Inc. • Workforce and Customer Identity

★★★★☆4.7/5
Updated: October 2026
8,000+ Integrations

Overview

Okta is an identity provider with two product lines: Workforce Identity, for employee and contractor access, and Customer Identity, for customer-facing applications, which includes Auth0. The Okta Integration Network lists more than 8,000 pre-built integrations.

Workforce Identity is sold as suites. Starter includes Single Sign-On, MFA, Universal Directory and 5 Workflows. Essentials adds Adaptive MFA, Lifecycle Management, Access Governance, Privileged Access for two admins and 50 Workflows. Professional adds Device Access, Identity Threat Protection and Identity Security Posture Management (ISPM), and Enterprise adds API Access Management, Access Gateway and machine-to-machine tokens.

All suites are billed annually, and Workforce Identity has a $1,500 annual contract minimum. Customer Identity starts with an Enterprise base platform at $3,000 per month, billed annually; Auth0 has its own pricing.

Key Features

Single Sign-On

One login to apps in the Okta Integration Network, which lists 8,000+ pre-built integrations.

MFA and Adaptive MFA

MFA in every suite; Adaptive MFA from Core Essentials upward.

Universal Directory

Central directory for users and groups, included from Starter.

Lifecycle Management

Automated provisioning and deprovisioning, from Core Essentials upward.

Okta Workflows

No-code identity automation: 5 workflows in Starter, 50 in Essentials, maximum in Professional and Enterprise.

Identity Threat Protection

Included in Professional and Enterprise, an add-on in lower suites.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Published prices for the entry suites (Starter, Core Essentials, Essentials)
  • 30-day free trial of Workforce Identity with no commitment
  • More than 8,000 pre-built integrations
  • Clear upgrade path from SSO and MFA to governance and privileged access
  • Free Integrator plan for developers building on Customer Identity

Disadvantages

  • Annual billing only, with a $1,500 annual minimum for Workforce Identity
  • Professional and Enterprise suites are quote-only
  • Identity Threat Protection and ISPM are add-ons or unavailable below Professional
  • Customer Identity starts at $3,000 per month

Best Use Cases

Best For:

  • Companies standardizing single sign-on across many SaaS applications
  • IT teams automating joiner, mover and leaver processes with Lifecycle Management
  • Organizations adding access governance or privileged access to their identity stack
  • Developers adding login to customer-facing apps (Customer Identity or Auth0)

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Very small teams for whom the $1,500 annual minimum is high
  • Buyers who need monthly billing
  • Organizations that only need the identity features already included in their existing licenses

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Okta cost?

Workforce Identity starts at $6 per user per month for Starter, $14 for Core Essentials and $17 for Essentials, billed annually with a $1,500 annual minimum. Professional and Enterprise are quoted.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Okta offers a 30-day free trial of Workforce Identity with no commitment.

What is the difference between Workforce and Customer Identity?

Workforce Identity secures employee and contractor access to apps; Customer Identity (including Auth0) handles sign-up and login for an organization’s own customers.

Is there a free plan for developers?

The Okta Integrator Free Plan supports up to 10 monthly active users in a non-production environment and does not expire.

Which suite includes Lifecycle Management?

Core Essentials and every suite above it.
