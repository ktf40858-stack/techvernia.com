Fortinet FortiGate Review

by Fortinet • Next-generation firewall and Secure SD-WAN

★★★★☆4.7/5

FortiOS

Overview

FortiGate is Fortinet’s next-generation firewall line. Every model runs FortiOS, Fortinet’s single operating system, and hardware models use Fortinet’s purpose-built security and networking ASICs (FortiASIC) to accelerate inspection and networking.

FortiGate comes as hardware appliances, from entry-level branch models to mid-range campus and high-end data center models, plus ruggedized models for harsh environments, virtual machines for private and public clouds, and FortiGate-as-a-Service. Security services come from FortiGuard, sold as ENT, UTP and ATP bundles or individually: intrusion prevention, antivirus, URL and DNS filtering, DLP, CASB, attack surface security, inline malware prevention and OT security.

Secure SD-WAN is built into FortiGate, and the firewall sits at the center of the Fortinet Security Fabric with management tools such as FortiManager and FortiAnalyzer, which include the FortiAI-Assist assistant. FortiOS 7.6.1 added post-quantum cryptography for IPsec VPN. Fortinet does not publish prices: you request a quote or buy through a reseller.

Key Features

FortiOS and FortiASIC

One operating system across form factors, with hardware acceleration on appliances.

FortiGuard Services

ENT, UTP and ATP bundles, or IPS, antivirus, URL/DNS filtering, DLP and CASB individually.

Secure SD-WAN

SD-WAN orchestration and secure branch interconnection without a separate appliance.

Flexible Form Factors

Hardware, ruggedized, VM, public cloud and FortiGate-as-a-Service.

Security Fabric

Shared policies and telemetry with other Fortinet products across hybrid networks.

AI Features

AI-based inline malware prevention (ENT bundle) and FortiAI-Assist in FortiAnalyzer and FortiManager.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Same FortiOS from small branch boxes to data center and cloud
  • ASIC acceleration on hardware models
  • SD-WAN included in the firewall
  • Choice between FortiGuard bundles and individual services
  • Post-quantum IPsec VPN support since FortiOS 7.6.1

Disadvantages

  • No published prices; total cost combines hardware, FortiGuard subscriptions and support
  • Some protections, such as AI-based inline malware prevention, require the ENT bundle
  • Much of the value assumes other Fortinet products in the Security Fabric
  • The product page offers a demo, not a self-serve trial

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based. Fortinet lists no model or bundle prices; the main cost drivers are:

Hardware or VM

The FortiGate model, sized by performance, capacity and redundancy, or the VM/cloud license.

FortiGuard Bundle

ENT (comprehensive), UTP (web and network) or ATP (essential protection), or individual services.

Support

Ongoing support subscriptions renewed alongside security services.

How to Buy

Request a quote from Fortinet or buy through a regional reseller.

Fortinet’s firewall pricing guide explains these cost factors but does not give FortiGate prices.

Best Use Cases

Best For:

  • Distributed branches: firewall and SD-WAN in one device
  • Data centers: high-end appliances with ASIC acceleration
  • Hybrid cloud: the same FortiOS on VMs and public cloud
  • Fortinet shops: organizations using FortiManager, FortiAnalyzer and other Fabric products
  • OT environments: ruggedized models and the FortiGuard OT security service

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Buyers who need published prices to compare online
  • Organizations that only need endpoint or cloud workload protection

Comparison with Competitors

  • Palo Alto Networks NGFW: also offers hardware, VM and cloud firewalls with subscription security services; also quote-based.
  • Cisco Secure Firewall: a common alternative in Cisco-centric networks.
  • Check Point Quantum: another established NGFW family with its own management platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FortiOS?

The single operating system that runs on all FortiGate firewalls, whether hardware, virtual or cloud.

What are the FortiGuard bundles?

ENT (comprehensive), UTP (web and network protection) and ATP (essential first line of defense). Services can also be bought individually.

Does FortiGate include SD-WAN?

Yes. Secure SD-WAN is integrated into FortiGate.

How much does a FortiGate cost?

Fortinet does not publish prices. Quotes depend on the model, the FortiGuard bundle and support, and are available from Fortinet or resellers.

Which certification covers FortiGate?

NSE 4: FortiOS, which validates configuring and administering FortiGate devices.
