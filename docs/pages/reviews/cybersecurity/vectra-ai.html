Overview

The Vectra AI Platform is a network detection and response (NDR) product that Vectra extends beyond traditional network monitoring to identity and cloud. Vectra AI is headquartered in San Jose, California.

Network detections surface lateral movement in east-west and north-south traffic across data centers, campuses, remote work, cloud and IoT/OT. Identity detections cover credential theft, account compromise and privilege escalation in Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, Microsoft 365, Azure and AWS. Cloud detections span AWS, Google Cloud, IBM Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, Microsoft Copilot for M365 and Oracle Cloud.

Vectra describes its AI in three steps: triage (separating threats from benign activity), stitching (correlating activity across domains) and prioritization (ranking risk). The platform is agentless and can run on-premises, as SaaS or in a hybrid model. Vectra also offers MDR, where its analysts take on part or all of the response work, and optional 24x7x365 Premium Support. Pricing is not published; Vectra offers demos and a platform tour.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Agentless coverage of network, identity and cloud in one platform
  • Identity detections for both on-prem AD and Microsoft Entra ID
  • On-premises, SaaS and hybrid deployment options
  • Optional MDR for teams without a 24x7 SOC

Disadvantages

  • No public pricing or free trial; demo only
  • Network coverage requires access to network traffic
  • Detection and response only; it does not replace endpoint protection
  • 24x7x365 Premium Support is optional

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based. Vectra does not publish prices; the quote depends on the following:

Coverage domains

Which of network, identity and cloud detections you deploy, and the size of each environment.

Deployment model

On-premises, SaaS or hybrid.

Services

Optional MDR and optional 24x7x365 Premium Support.

Best Use Cases

Best For:

  • SOC teams that need visibility into lateral movement inside the network
  • Microsoft-centric organizations worried about Entra ID and M365 account compromise
  • Hybrid and multi-cloud environments
  • Organizations with OT or IoT segments where agents cannot be installed

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Small businesses looking for a self-service product with published prices
  • Teams that need endpoint prevention rather than detection
  • Environments where network traffic cannot be mirrored to sensors

Comparison

Vectra AI vs Alternatives

  • vs Darktrace and ExtraHop RevealX: the other main NDR vendors; all three analyze network traffic, while Vectra puts identity and cloud detections in the same platform.
  • vs Microsoft Defender for Identity: included with Microsoft 365 E5 and focused on Active Directory; Vectra adds network and multi-cloud detections.
  • vs EDR tools such as CrowdStrike Falcon: EDR sees what happens on endpoints; Vectra sees traffic and identity activity, including on unmanaged devices.
