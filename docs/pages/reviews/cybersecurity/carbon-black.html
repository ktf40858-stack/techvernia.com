Carbon Black Review

Broadcom (formerly VMware) • Endpoint protection and EDR

★★★★★4.6/5
Updated: October 2026
Endpoint Security
Update — October 2026: Broadcom completed its acquisition of VMware in November 2023. In March 2024 it combined Carbon Black and Symantec in a new Enterprise Security Group, and Carbon Black products are now presented on broadcom.com.

Overview

Carbon Black is an endpoint and workload security portfolio owned by Broadcom. Broadcom’s solution brief describes Carbon Black Cloud as letting teams inspect each endpoint, network connection and process “with one agent and one console”, applying behavioral analytics to endpoint events to detect, prevent and respond to malware, non-malware and living-off-the-land attacks.

The cloud platform is modular. Next-generation antivirus with behavioral EDR (Endpoint Standard) covers prevention; Enterprise EDR adds threat hunting and containment for SOC and incident response teams; Audit and Remediation gives real-time device assessment. Broadcom also lists vulnerability management, device control for USB devices, a host-based firewall, managed detection and response, identity intelligence and network analysis for XDR. Carbon Black also pioneered application control.

Sensors are available for Windows desktops and servers, macOS and Linux, with per-OS requirements published in Broadcom’s documentation. Broadcom does not publish prices; Carbon Black is sold through Broadcom and its partners.

Key Features

Next-Gen Antivirus

NGAV and behavioral EDR that protect against malware, non-malware and living-off-the-land attacks.

Enterprise EDR

Threat hunting and containment with continuous visibility for SOC and IR teams.

Audit and Remediation

Real-time device assessment and remediation to change endpoint system state.

Vulnerability Management

Risk-prioritized view of vulnerabilities on endpoints and workloads.

Device Control and Host Firewall

Granular USB control and a rule-based host firewall managed from the same console.

Managed Detection and Response

Broadcom analysts monitor Carbon Black Cloud data and recommend policy changes.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • One sensor and one console for several modules
  • Behavioral prevention aimed at non-malware and living-off-the-land attacks
  • Enterprise EDR for threat hunting and remote response
  • Sensors for Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Host firewall and device control help consolidate endpoint agents

Disadvantages

  • No published pricing; quotes go through Broadcom or its partners
  • Key capabilities are sold as separate modules
  • Two ownership changes since 2019 have moved sales and support channels
  • Documentation is spread across broadcom.com, docs.vmware.com and the Broadcom knowledge base
  • macOS sensor features differ between system extension and kernel extension modes

Pricing

Pricing is quote-based. Broadcom does not publish list prices for Carbon Black.

Modules

The quote depends on which modules you license: NGAV/Endpoint Standard, Enterprise EDR, Audit and Remediation, vulnerability management and others.

Coverage

The number of endpoints and workloads to protect.

Managed Services

Managed detection and response is an additional service built on Carbon Black Cloud.

How to Buy

Through Broadcom and its channel partners.

Broadcom’s product pages offer contact and demo requests rather than a self-serve trial.

Best Use Cases

Best For:

  • Antivirus replacement: organizations consolidating legacy endpoint agents
  • SOC and IR teams: threat hunting and containment with Enterprise EDR
  • Ransomware defense: behavioral prevention on endpoints and workloads
  • Compliance: device assessment and audit with Audit and Remediation
  • Existing Broadcom customers: buyers already working with Broadcom or VMware

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Small businesses that want to buy online at a published per-device price
  • Teams without staff to tune policies and investigate alerts, unless they add MDR

Comparison with Competitors

  • CrowdStrike Falcon: also a single-agent cloud platform, but CrowdStrike publishes per-device prices (Falcon Go from $59.99 per device per year).
  • Microsoft Defender for Endpoint: the natural choice for organizations already standardized on Microsoft 365 security licensing.
  • Symantec Endpoint Security: Broadcom’s other endpoint line, managed by the same Enterprise Security Group.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns Carbon Black?

Broadcom. It came with Broadcom’s acquisition of VMware (completed November 2023) and has sat in Broadcom’s Enterprise Security Group with Symantec since March 2024.

What are the main Carbon Black Cloud modules?

Endpoint Standard (NGAV and behavioral EDR), Enterprise EDR, Audit and Remediation, plus vulnerability management, device control, host-based firewall and managed detection and response.

Which operating systems are supported?

Windows desktop, Windows Server, macOS and Linux. Broadcom publishes operating environment requirements for each sensor.

How much does Carbon Black cost?

Broadcom does not publish prices. Quotes depend on the modules and the number of endpoints and workloads.

Is there a managed option?

Yes. Managed detection and response is built on Carbon Black Cloud, with Broadcom analysts monitoring and recommending policy changes.
