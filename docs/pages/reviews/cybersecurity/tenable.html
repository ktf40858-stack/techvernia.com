Update — October 2026: Tenable renamed Tenable.io to Tenable Vulnerability Management in 2023. Tenable's product and pricing pages now list it as Tenable One Vulnerability Management.

Overview

Tenable.io was Tenable's cloud-managed vulnerability management product. Tenable renamed it Tenable Vulnerability Management in 2023, and its website now sells it as Tenable One Vulnerability Management. Tenable describes it as built on Tenable Nessus technology and managed in the cloud.

The product covers continuous discovery of known and unknown assets, including cloud and remote-workforce assets, vulnerability assessment enriched with Tenable Research threat intelligence, and agentless coverage for workloads and containers. Findings are ranked with the Vulnerability Priority Rating (VPR), which Tenable says is powered by generative AI, enriched threat intelligence and context-aware scoring. Remediation is tracked with bi-directional ticketing, and Hexa AI adds an agentic layer that can act on security tasks.

Tenable publishes a price for 100 assets: $3,500 for one year, $6,825 for two years and $9,975 for three years. A free evaluation gives full access to the platform and includes Web App Scanning. Organizations that need on-premises management are pointed to Tenable Security Center or Tenable Enclave Security.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Prices are published, with discounts on two- and three-year terms
  • Free evaluation with full platform access, including Web App Scanning
  • Built on Tenable Nessus technology
  • VPR helps focus on exploitable vulnerabilities

Disadvantages

  • Cloud-managed only; on-premises needs Tenable Security Center

  • Patch Management requires its own license
  • Trial length is not stated on the product page

Best Use Cases

Best For:

  • Security teams that want vulnerability management without running their own console
  • Organizations already using Nessus scanners
  • Teams that want to prioritize by exploitability (VPR) rather than CVSS alone
  • Hybrid environments with cloud workloads and remote endpoints

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Environments that must stay fully on-premises (see Tenable Security Center)
  • Consultants who only need a single scanner (see Nessus Professional)
  • Teams that only need web application scanning

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tenable.io still available?

Yes, under a new name. It became Tenable Vulnerability Management in 2023 and is now listed as Tenable One Vulnerability Management.

How much does it cost?

Tenable lists $3,500 for one year, $6,825 for two years and $9,975 for three years, each for 100 assets.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Tenable offers a free evaluation with full platform access that also includes Web App Scanning. The page does not state its length.

Can it run on-premises?

No. It is managed in the cloud; Tenable points on-premises buyers to Tenable Security Center or Tenable Enclave Security.

What is VPR?

The Vulnerability Priority Rating, Tenable's score for focusing on the highest-risk vulnerabilities using threat intelligence and context.
