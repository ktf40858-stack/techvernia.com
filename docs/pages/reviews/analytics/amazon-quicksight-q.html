Overview

QuickSight Q was the natural-language question-and-answer capability of Amazon QuickSight, the cloud BI service from AWS. AWS now delivers these capabilities as Amazon Quick Sight, which provides “AI-powered BI capabilities within Quick,” a broader workspace that also includes research, flows and automation agents.

In Quick Sight, users can run analysis in natural language, explore data conversationally and answer “what-if” questions with scenarios. Teams can also embed interactive analytics in their own applications. AWS says it connects to databases, data warehouses, documents, emails and knowledge bases.

Pricing is now set per user for Amazon Quick. On the pricing page, viewing Quick Sight dashboards is included in Professional and Enterprise, while creating dashboards, reports and datasets is listed for Enterprise only.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Fits naturally for teams whose data already lives in AWS
  • Published per-user prices, plus a free Amazon Quick plan
  • 30-day Enterprise trial for up to 25 users, no credit card required
  • Compliance support listed for FedRAMP, HIPAA, PCI DSS, ISO and SOC
  • Analytics can be embedded in your own apps

Disadvantages

  • Renamed products (QuickSight Q → Amazon Quick, Quick Sight) make older guides confusing
  • Dashboard authoring needs Enterprise: $40/user/month plus a $250 account infrastructure fee
  • SPICE capacity, pixel-perfect reports and alerts are paid add-ons
  • Professional and Enterprise require an AWS account

Pricing

Amazon Quick plans (per user per month):

Free ($0)

Sign up with email or social credentials; no AWS account required.

Plus ($20 annual / $25 monthly)

For teams of up to 300 users.

Max ($100 annual / $125 monthly)

For frequent users who need more usage capacity.

Professional ($20 + $250/account)

Requires an AWS account. Includes viewing and interacting with Quick Sight dashboards, reports and stories.

Enterprise ($40 + $250/account)

Requires an AWS account. Adds creating Quick Sight dashboards, reports and datasets.

Add-ons: SPICE at $0.38/GB/month, pixel-perfect reports at $1 per report unit/month (500-unit minimum), and alerts and anomaly detection priced by metrics evaluated. The 30-day Enterprise trial waives fees for up to 25 users.

Best Use Cases

Amazon QuickSight Q Excels For:

  • Organizations that already store their data in AWS
  • Product teams that want to embed analytics in their own applications
  • Business users who prefer asking questions in plain language
  • Teams that need what-if scenario analysis next to their dashboards

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Organizations that do not use AWS
  • Teams that want a standalone BI tool without an AWS account
  • Small teams that need dashboard authoring but not the $250 account fee

Frequently Asked Questions

What happened to QuickSight Q?

AWS now offers QuickSight and its Q capabilities as Amazon Quick Sight, part of Amazon Quick (since October 9, 2025).

How much does it cost?

Amazon Quick ranges from a free plan to Enterprise at $40 per user per month plus a $250 infrastructure fee per account. Dashboard authoring is listed for Enterprise.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A 30-day Enterprise trial covers up to 25 users per account, with no credit card required to begin.

Can I embed Quick Sight dashboards?

Yes. AWS says you can embed interactive analytics in your applications and business workflows.

Which compliance programs does it support?

The Quick Sight page lists FedRAMP, HIPAA, PCI DSS, ISO and SOC. Check AWS's compliance documentation for your region and workload.
