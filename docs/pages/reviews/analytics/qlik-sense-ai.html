Overview

Qlik Sense is Qlik’s analytics product, now sold as Qlik Cloud Analytics. Its analytics engine is designed to surface patterns, associations and blind spots across data, and users explore it through interactive visualizations and dashboards that connect to hundreds of data sources.

AI features include Answers Agents for conversational questions over structured and unstructured data, predictive analytics powered by automated machine learning (on Premium), AI anomaly detection and an MCP server for connecting AI tools. A no-code automation builder triggers actions across systems, and Qlik Talend Cloud handles data integration.

Pricing is published. Starter is priced by users; from Standard upward, plans are priced by data volume, with no cost for additional users.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Published prices for three of four tiers
  • From Standard upward, no charge for additional users
  • Data integration through Qlik Talend Cloud
  • AutoML-based predictions built in on Premium
  • Free trial with no cost to get started

Disadvantages

  • Starter costs $300/month (10 users), steep for individuals
  • Capacity is priced by data volume, so costs grow with your data
  • AutoML predictions start at Premium ($2,750/month)
  • Enterprise requires a quote
  • Trial length is not stated on the pricing page

Pricing

Qlik Cloud Analytics plans (billed annually):

Starter (from $300/month)

For small businesses: 10 users and 10 GB of data; additional users available. Includes AI-powered analytics, Answers Agents and the MCP Server.

Standard (from $825/month)

25 GB of data, no cost for additional users, GenAI on unstructured data, additional Answers Agents, shared spaces for governance.

Premium (from $2,750/month)

50 GB of data, predictive analytics with automated ML, more GenAI capacity, anonymous public access, SAP data extraction.

Enterprise (Get a quote)

Starts at 250 GB of data, with added scale for large deployments.

Extra capacity is sold in 25 GB packs (and 250 GB packs on Premium).

Best Use Cases

Qlik Sense AI Excels For:

  • Organizations with many users that prefer capacity-based pricing
  • Teams that combine data integration and analytics in one vendor
  • Analysts who explore data through associations rather than fixed drill paths
  • Businesses that want AutoML predictions inside their BI tool

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Individuals or very small teams on a tight budget
  • Organizations with very large data volumes but few users
  • Teams that need a free plan rather than a trial

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Qlik Sense cost?

Qlik Cloud Analytics starts at $300/month for 10 users (Starter), then $825/month (Standard) and $2,750/month (Premium), billed annually. Enterprise is quoted.

Is Qlik priced per user?

Only Starter. Standard and above are priced by data volume, with no cost for additional users.

What are Qlik Answers Agents?

Conversational AI agents that answer questions using your data, including unstructured content on higher tiers.

Does Qlik include machine learning?

Yes. Premium and above include predictive analytics powered by automated machine learning.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Qlik offers a free trial with no cost to get started; the pricing page does not state its length.
