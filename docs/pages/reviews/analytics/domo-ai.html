Overview

Domo is a cloud platform that combines data integration, BI dashboards, low-code apps, workflow automation and AI agents. The company was founded in 2010 by Josh James and is based in American Fork, Utah.

Domo says it can bring together 1,000+ data sources and prepare them with drag-and-drop ETL. On top of that data, Domo AI provides AI chat that can be embedded in dashboards, plus custom and pre-built AI agents that handle tasks such as reporting, alerts and approvals. App Studio covers low-code apps, and Domo Everywhere handles embedded analytics.

Pricing is consumption-based: Domo sells credits that are used when you store data, update tables, run workflows or use features such as ML inference, with unlimited users and no per-user charges. In September 2026, Progress Software acquired Domo’s AI and data platform business.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Unlimited users, with no per-user charges
  • 30-day free trial with no credit card required
  • ETL, dashboards, apps, workflows and AI agents in one platform
  • Large connector library (1,000+ sources)
  • Failed processes do not consume credits, according to Domo

Disadvantages

  • Credit prices are not published; every contract is custom
  • Costs depend on usage (storage, table updates, workflow runs) and need monitoring
  • The 2026 sale to Progress Software adds roadmap uncertainty
  • HIPAA-compliant environment and AWS PrivateLink are listed only with custom pricing
  • Broad platform with more to learn than a dashboard-only tool

Pricing

Domo uses credit-based pricing: you pay for what you use, with unlimited users.

30-Day Free Trial

Full platform, unlimited users, onboarding support, self-service education and one training session. No credit card required.

Custom Pricing

Everything in the trial, plus a dedicated account team, volume discounts, custom add-ons, support packages, AWS PrivateLink and a HIPAA-compliant environment.

Credits are consumed by actions such as storing data, updating tables, running workflows and ML inference. Domo can also be bought through cloud marketplaces.

Best Use Cases

Domo AI Excels For:

  • Organizations that want integration, BI and automation from one vendor
  • Companies with many viewers who would pay a lot under per-user pricing
  • Teams building AI agents for reporting, alerts and approvals on governed data
  • Businesses that embed dashboards for customers or partners

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Teams that need a fixed, predictable per-seat budget
  • Small teams that only need a few dashboards
  • Buyers wary of the platform changing owners

Comparison

Domo AI vs Competitors

  • vs Power BI: Power BI is priced per user (Pro at $14/user/month); Domo charges for consumption and does not charge per user.
  • vs Qlik Cloud Analytics: Qlik publishes tier prices (Starter from $300/month for 10 users) based on users and data volume; Domo pricing is custom.
  • vs ThoughtSpot: ThoughtSpot centers on natural-language search with per-user plans from $25/user/month; Domo adds ETL, apps and workflows to BI.
