Overview

Alteryx makes analytics automation software. Its best-known product, Alteryx Designer, lets analysts build data preparation, blending and analysis workflows on a visual drag-and-drop canvas instead of writing code. Each step is documented on the canvas, so a workflow can be rerun, reviewed and shared.

Designer ships with more than 300 drag-and-drop tools, including spatial and prescriptive analytics and extraction from PDFs, text files and images. Alteryx says it integrates with 80+ data sources and outputs to over 70 tools. The products are now sold as Alteryx One, in Starter, Professional and Enterprise editions.

AI features sit on top of the workflow engine: Ask Alteryx builds workflows from natural-language requests, and the AI Tool Palette brings large language models into workflows. Alteryx was founded in 1997, is based in Irvine, California, and was taken private by Clearlake Capital and Insight Partners in March 2024.

Pros & Cons

Advantages

  • Analysts can build complex data prep without SQL or Python
  • Large built-in tool library, including spatial analytics
  • Workflows document themselves and can be rerun on new data
  • Published entry price and a 30-day free trial
  • Native connectors to cloud warehouses such as Snowflake and Databricks

Disadvantages

  • Starter costs $250 per user per month, billed annually
  • Professional and Enterprise prices are not published
  • Designer desktop needs Windows; on a Mac it requires a virtual machine
  • Starter includes only 50 automation runs
  • Demanding hardware: quad-core CPU, 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB for high performance)

Pricing

Alteryx publishes one price; the other editions go through sales:

Starter ($250/user/month, billed annually)

Code-free cloud data preparation and analytics for individual analysts and small teams. Includes 50 automation runs.

Professional (Contact sales)

Advanced and automated analytics, hybrid deployment, AI-powered assistance and collaborative workflows for growing data teams.

Enterprise (Contact sales)

Full-scale automation, enterprise governance and private data handling. Includes 15,000 automation runs and free viewer licenses.

Add-ons are available for Professional and Enterprise. A 30-day free trial of Alteryx One is available.

Best Use Cases

Alteryx AI Excels For:

  • Analysts who prepare and blend data from many sources by hand today
  • Teams replacing fragile spreadsheet processes with repeatable workflows
  • Location and spatial analysis without GIS programming
  • Organizations that need documented, auditable data preparation

May Not Be Ideal For:

  • Individuals or small teams on a tight budget
  • Mac-only teams that do not want to run a Windows virtual machine
  • Teams whose main need is dashboards rather than data preparation

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Alteryx used for?

Building data preparation, blending and analysis workflows on a visual canvas, without writing code. Workflows can be rerun and automated.

How much does Alteryx cost?

The Starter edition costs $250 per user per month, billed annually. Professional and Enterprise prices are available from sales.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Alteryx offers a 30-day free trial of Alteryx One.

Does Alteryx Designer run on a Mac?

Designer requires Windows 10 (64-bit) or later. On macOS it must run in a virtual machine.

What AI features does Alteryx include?

Ask Alteryx builds workflows from natural-language requests and the AI Tool Palette brings LLMs into workflows. The pricing page also lists Agent Studio and an MCP Server.

Who owns Alteryx?

Clearlake Capital and Insight Partners took Alteryx private in a $4.4 billion deal completed on March 19, 2024.
