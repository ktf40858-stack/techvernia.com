Overview

The Zabbix 7.0 Certified Expert (ZCE) represents the pinnacle of Zabbix certification, validating expert-level mastery of Zabbix architecture, advanced optimization techniques, custom development, and complex enterprise integrations. This elite certification demonstrates your ability to architect, optimize, and troubleshoot the most demanding Zabbix deployments at global scale.

ZCE-certified professionals are recognized as subject matter experts capable of designing innovative monitoring solutions, developing custom extensions, optimizing massive deployments (50,000+ hosts), and solving the most complex technical challenges. They serve as technical leaders, architects, and advisors for enterprise-wide monitoring strategies.

The ZCE certification is ideal for principal engineers, monitoring architects, Zabbix consultants, and technical leads responsible for the most challenging Zabbix implementations and strategic monitoring initiatives.

Key Topics Covered

The ZCE exam tests expert-level knowledge and skills in the following advanced areas:

  • Architecture Design: Designing global-scale Zabbix architectures, multi-tier deployments, geo-distributed monitoring, and disaster recovery strategies
  • Performance Engineering: Advanced performance tuning, database partitioning at scale, query optimization, and handling 50,000+ host environments
  • Custom Development: Developing loadable modules in C, creating advanced external scripts, and extending Zabbix core functionality
  • Advanced API: Building complex automation systems, developing custom monitoring portals, and creating advanced integrations using Zabbix API
  • Database Expertise: Advanced PostgreSQL/MySQL optimization, replication strategies, backup/recovery, and migration planning
  • Enterprise Integration: Integrating with ITSM platforms, CMDB systems, automation frameworks, and building comprehensive monitoring ecosystems
  • Security Hardening: Implementing advanced security controls, compliance configurations, encrypted communications, and vulnerability management
  • Custom Protocols: Implementing custom monitoring protocols, developing specialized data collection methods, and protocol debugging
  • Capacity Planning: Designing for scalability, forecasting infrastructure requirements, and planning for massive growth
  • Migration & Upgrades: Planning and executing major version upgrades, platform migrations, and zero-downtime updates
  • Expert Troubleshooting: Diagnosing the most complex issues, performance forensics, and resolving production emergencies
  • Team Leadership: Mentoring teams, establishing best practices, and driving monitoring strategy at organizational level
