Überblick

Resemble AI is a cutting-edge voice cloning and speech synthesis platform that specializes in creating ultra-realistic AI voices with unprecedented control. What sets Resemble AI apart is its real-time voice conversion technology and neural audio editing capabilities, allowing users to not just generate speech but also modify existing audio with surgical precision.

The platform excels at voice cloning, enabling users to create digital replicas of any voice with minimal audio samples. Whether you're a game developer needing dynamic character voices, a filmmaker requiring ADR (automated dialogue replacement), or a content creator building personalized voice experiences, Resemble AI provides professional-grade tools that were previously only available to major studios.

Resemble AI's neural audio editing is revolutionary - you can type to edit spoken audio just like editing text in a document. Need to change a word in a recorded sentence? Simply type the correction and the AI generates that word in the exact voice and tone of the original recording. This capability alone has transformed workflows for podcast producers, audiobook creators, and video editors.

Häufig gestellte Fragen

What is neural audio editing? +
Neural audio editing allows you to edit spoken audio by typing, similar to editing a text document. You can change words, fix mistakes, or add new content, and Resemble AI will generate the corrections in the exact same voice, tone, and style as the original recording. This eliminates the need for re-recording entire segments.
How much audio do I need to clone a voice? +
For basic voice cloning, you can start with as little as 3-5 minutes of clean audio. However, for the highest quality results with full emotional range, 30-60 minutes of varied speech is recommended. The audio should be clear, with minimal background noise and showcase different speaking styles.
Can I use Resemble AI for game development? +
Yes, Resemble AI is excellent for game development and offers native integrations with Unity and Unreal Engine. You can generate dynamic NPC dialogue, create character voices, and even implement real-time voice conversion for player interactions. Many game studios use Resemble AI for producing vast amounts of varied dialogue.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes, Resemble AI offers a free trial with 10 minutes of generation credit. This allows you to test voice cloning, neural editing, and other features before committing to a paid plan. Note that free trial outputs are watermarked and have some feature limitations.
How does Resemble AI prevent voice misuse? +
Resemble AI takes voice security seriously with multiple safeguards: voice consent verification is required for cloning, audio watermarking helps track generated content, deepfake detection tools can identify synthetic audio, and enterprise features include voice biometric protection. They have strict terms of service prohibiting misuse.
Can I localize content to other languages? +
Yes, Resemble AI's localization engine can translate and synthesize content in 60+ languages while preserving the original speaker's voice characteristics. This is particularly useful for creating multilingual content without hiring multiple voice actors or re-recording in each language.
