Überblick

Mem is revolutionizing personal knowledge management with its self-organizing AI workspace. Unlike traditional note-taking apps that require manual organization, Mem automatically connects related information, surfaces relevant context, and helps you find exactly what you need when you need it.

The magic of Mem lies in its AI-powered organization system. You simply capture thoughts, ideas, meeting notes, and research without worrying about folders or tags. Mem's AI automatically identifies connections, extracts key concepts, and creates a living knowledge graph that grows smarter over time.

With over 100,000 users including knowledge workers, researchers, and creative professionals, Mem has proven itself as the note-taking app for people who think in connections rather than hierarchies. It's perfect for those drowning in information who need a second brain that actually understands context.

Unsere Empfehlung

Mem is the best choice for knowledge workers who want their notes to organize themselves. The AI-powered organization and smart search are genuinely impressive - you can throw information at Mem and trust it will be findable later. However, the price is steep at $15/month, especially compared to free alternatives like Obsidian or Apple Notes. Mem is worth it if you're drowning in information and need AI to help manage it, but casual note-takers might find it overkill. The lack of a free tier is disappointing, but the 14-day trial lets you test thoroughly. Best for researchers, writers, and professionals who capture lots of information daily and need intelligent retrieval.

Häufig gestellte Fragen

Does Mem work offline?+
No, Mem currently requires an internet connection to function. All notes are stored in the cloud and AI features require connectivity. This is a limitation compared to local-first apps like Obsidian.
How does Mem's AI organization work?+
Mem uses natural language processing to understand note content, automatically extract key concepts, identify entities (people, places, topics), and create connections between related notes. The more you use it, the better it gets at understanding your knowledge base.
Can I import notes from other apps?+
Yes, Mem supports importing from various sources including plain text, Markdown, and can sync with email, Slack, and other platforms. However, migration from complex apps like Notion may require some manual work.
Is there a free version of Mem?+
No permanent free tier, but Mem offers a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. After the trial, you need to subscribe at $14.99/month or $10/month annually.
Can teams collaborate in Mem?+
Mem is primarily designed for personal knowledge management. While you can share individual notes, it lacks robust team collaboration features like real-time editing found in Notion or Google Docs.
How does Mem compare to Notion AI?+
Mem's AI is more integrated into the core experience (auto-organization, smart search) while Notion AI is an add-on writing assistant. Mem is simpler and better for personal knowledge; Notion excels at team collaboration and databases.
