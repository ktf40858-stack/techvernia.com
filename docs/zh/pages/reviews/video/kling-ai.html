概览

Kling AI is a revolutionary AI video platform that enables anyone to create professional spokesperson videos using AI avatars. With advanced voice cloning technology and multilingual video translation capabilities, Kling AI has become the go-to solution for businesses and creators who need scalable video content without the cost and complexity of traditional video production.

What makes Kling AI stand out is its incredibly realistic AI avatars and instant voice cloning feature. You can create a digital twin of yourself or choose from over 100 pre-made avatars, then generate videos in High quality languages with natural lip-sync and expressions. The platform's video translation feature can take any video and automatically translate it while matching the speaker's lip movements to the new language.

Used by over 5 million people worldwide, Kling AI is perfect for creating marketing videos, product demos, training content, social media posts, and personalized outreach at scale. The platform's ease of use and professional results have made it a favorite among marketers, sales teams, educators, and content creators.

经常被问到的问题

How realistic are Kling AI's AI avatars?+
Kling AI's avatars are among the most realistic in the industry. Premium avatars feature natural facial expressions, authentic gestures, and smooth lip-sync. While close inspection may reveal they're AI-generated, they're convincing enough for most professional use cases including marketing, sales, and training videos.
Can I create a custom avatar that looks like me?+
Yes, Kling AI offers custom avatar creation on Business and Enterprise plans. You'll need to record yourself reading a script (typically 2-5 minutes) with good lighting and audio. The process takes about 1-2 weeks, after which you can generate unlimited videos with your digital twin.
How does voice cloning work?+
Kling AI's instant voice cloning requires just 2 minutes of clear audio recording. You read a provided script, upload it, and within minutes you have a cloned voice. The quality depends on your recording quality - use a quiet room and good microphone for best results. The cloned voice can then speak any text you type.
What languages does Kling AI support?+
Kling AI supports High quality languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Italian, and many more. The video translation feature can automatically translate your content to any supported language while maintaining lip-sync with the original avatar.
How long does it take to generate a video?+
Most videos generate in 5-15 minutes depending on length and complexity. Simple 1-2 minute videos with a single avatar typically render in 5-7 minutes. Longer videos or those with translations may take 15-30 minutes. Pro and Business users get priority rendering for faster turnaround.
Can I use Kling AI videos commercially?+
Yes, all paid plans include commercial usage rights. You can use Kling AI videos for marketing, sales, advertising, social media, client projects, and more. The free plan is for personal testing only. Always ensure you have rights to any assets (scripts, images, music) you include in your videos.
