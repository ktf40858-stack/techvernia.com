Overview

Wiz is the fastest-growing cloud security company in history, revolutionizing how organizations secure their cloud environments. Its agentless architecture provides complete visibility across AWS, Azure, GCP, and Kubernetes without deploying any software.

The platform's Security Graph technology connects the dots between vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, identities, and exposed data to identify toxic risk combinations that actually matter. AI-powered prioritization helps teams focus on the issues that pose real risk.

Wiz has become the go-to choice for cloud-first organizations looking for comprehensive security without the operational overhead of agent-based solutions.
