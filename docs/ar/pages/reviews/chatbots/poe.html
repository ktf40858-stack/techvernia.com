Overview

Poe (Platform for Open Exploration) is Quora's innovative AI platform that gives you access to multiple AI models in one place. Instead of subscribing to ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini separately, Poe offers all of them (and more) under a single subscription.

What makes Poe unique is its marketplace approach to AI. Beyond the major models like GPT-4 and Claude, Poe hosts millions of custom bots created by users. You can also create and share your own specialized AI assistants, potentially earning money from their usage.

For users who want to compare different AI models or need access to multiple AIs for different tasks, Poe offers unmatched convenience and value. It's essentially the "Spotify of AI" - one subscription, many models.

Final Verdict

Our Recommendation

Poe is the best choice for users who want access to multiple AI models without multiple subscriptions. At $20/month, you get GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, and more - that's significant value compared to subscribing to each separately. The custom bot ecosystem adds unique functionality you won't find elsewhere. However, if you only need one AI and want the best native experience, the official ChatGPT or Claude apps are more polished. Poe is ideal for: (1) AI enthusiasts who want to compare models, (2) Users who need different AIs for different tasks, (3) Creators who want to build and monetize bots. Try the free tier first to see if the multi-model approach works for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Poe worth it compared to ChatGPT Plus?+
It depends on your needs. Poe ($20/mo) gives you access to GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, and more. ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) gives you better GPT-4 access with more features. If you only use GPT-4, ChatGPT Plus is better. If you want multiple models, Poe offers more value.
How do Poe compute points work?+
Poe subscribers get 1 million compute points per month. Different models cost different amounts - GPT-4 and Claude 3 Opus cost more points than smaller models. Points reset monthly. Most users find 1M points sufficient for regular use.
Can I really make money on Poe?+
Yes, Poe shares revenue with bot creators based on usage. Popular bots can earn significant amounts. However, like any creator economy, success requires building something valuable that people want to use regularly.
