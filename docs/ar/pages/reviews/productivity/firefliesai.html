لمحة عامة

Fireflies.ai is revolutionizing how teams handle meeting notes and follow-ups. This AI meeting assistant automatically joins your calls, records conversations, transcribes everything in real-time, and generates intelligent summaries with action items - all without you lifting a finger.

What makes Fireflies exceptional is its universal compatibility. Whether you're on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or even phone calls, Fireflies seamlessly integrates and captures everything. The AI doesn't just transcribe - it understands context, identifies speakers, extracts key topics, and even analyzes sentiment.

With over 300,000 teams relying on Fireflies daily, it's become the go-to solution for professionals who want to focus on conversations instead of frantically taking notes. The platform supports 69 languages and offers powerful search capabilities that let you find any discussion point across all your meetings in seconds.

الأسئلة المتكررة

Does Fireflies work with all video conferencing platforms?+
Yes! Fireflies works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, RingCentral, and many others. It can even dial into phone conferences. The AI assistant joins automatically or on-demand based on your settings.
How accurate is the transcription?+
Fireflies achieves 90%+ accuracy for clear audio in English. Accuracy varies with audio quality, accents, and background noise. You can edit transcripts manually and add custom vocabulary to improve accuracy for industry-specific terms.
Is Fireflies GDPR and SOC 2 compliant?+
Yes, Fireflies is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Enterprise plans offer additional security features including custom data retention policies and encryption. However, always inform meeting participants about recording.
Can I use Fireflies for free?+
Yes! The free plan includes 800 minutes of transcription per seat per month with unlimited storage. This is enough for most individuals. You can upgrade to Pro for more minutes and advanced AI features.
Does Fireflies work in languages other than English?+
Yes, Fireflies supports transcription in 69 languages including Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese, and many more. Language selection is automatic or can be manually specified.
Can I integrate Fireflies with my CRM?+
Absolutely! Fireflies integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and other major CRMs. It can automatically log call notes, update contacts, and sync action items. Available on Pro and higher plans.
