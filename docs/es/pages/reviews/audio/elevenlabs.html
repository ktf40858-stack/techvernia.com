Sinopsis

ElevenLabs has set a new standard for AI-generated speech. With remarkably natural-sounding voices, advanced emotion control, and industry-leading voice cloning capabilities, it's become the go-to platform for creators, developers, and businesses needing high-quality synthetic speech.

What makes ElevenLabs exceptional is the sheer quality of its voice generation. Unlike robotic-sounding TTS systems of the past, ElevenLabs produces speech that's virtually indistinguishable from human recordings. The platform supports 29 languages, offers instant voice cloning from just a few minutes of audio, and provides granular control over speaking style, emotion, and pacing.

From audiobook narration to video voiceovers, from AI assistants to accessibility tools, ElevenLabs powers millions of voice applications. Its combination of quality, flexibility, and developer-friendly API has made it the leading choice in the AI voice generation space.

Preguntas frecuentes

How realistic are ElevenLabs voices? +
ElevenLabs voices are among the most realistic AI-generated voices available. They include natural emotion, breathing, and intonation that make them nearly indistinguishable from human recordings. Many users report listeners can't tell the difference between ElevenLabs and real voice actors.
Can I clone anyone's voice? +
No. ElevenLabs requires explicit consent for voice cloning. You can only clone voices with permission, and the platform has safeguards to prevent misuse. For professional voice cloning, you need to verify you have rights to use the voice.
What's the difference between instant and professional voice cloning? +
Instant voice cloning requires just 1 minute of audio and is available on all paid plans. Professional voice cloning uses 3+ hours of audio to create higher-fidelity clones with better consistency and emotion control. It's available on Creator plans and above.
Can I use ElevenLabs voices commercially? +
Yes, all paid plans include commercial usage rights. You can use generated voices in YouTube videos, podcasts, audiobooks, advertisements, and any commercial projects. The free plan requires attribution and has usage restrictions.
How does the character limit work? +
Each plan has a monthly character limit that resets. One character equals one letter, number, or punctuation mark. For reference, 10,000 characters is roughly 1,500-2,000 words or about 10-15 minutes of audio. Unused characters don't roll over to the next month.
Is there an API for developers? +
Yes, ElevenLabs offers a robust API with streaming support, WebSocket connections, and SDKs for multiple languages. API usage is billed separately from subscriptions at $0.30 per 1,000 characters for most models, with volume discounts available.
