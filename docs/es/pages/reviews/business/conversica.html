Sinopsis

Conversica is a pioneering AI-powered Revenue Digital Assistant platform that automates lead engagement, qualification, and follow-up at scale. Unlike traditional marketing automation, Conversica's AI assistants conduct natural, two-way conversations via email and SMS, persistently engaging leads until they're ready to speak with a human sales rep.

What distinguishes Conversica is its ability to have authentic, multi-turn conversations that feel human. The platform's AI has been trained on over a decade of business conversation data, enabling it to understand context, detect buying signals, handle objections, and escalate hot leads to sales teams automatically. For organizations struggling with lead response time and follow-up consistency, Conversica delivers automated persistence that never sleeps.

Características clave

Revenue Digital Assistants

AI assistants for sales, marketing, and customer success. Pre-built personas tailored to specific use cases and industries.

Two-Way Conversations

Natural language processing enables authentic email and SMS conversations. Understands intent, sentiment, and buying signals.

Lead Qualification

Automatically qualifies leads through conversation. Identifies MQLs, SQLs, and routes hot prospects to appropriate sales reps.

Persistent Follow-Up

Never lets a lead go cold. Continues nurturing conversations over weeks or months until prospect responds or converts.

Revenue Analytics

Track conversation performance, conversion rates, pipeline contribution, and ROI. Detailed engagement metrics and insights.

CRM Integration

Seamless sync with Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, HubSpot, and other CRMs. Auto-logs all conversations and updates.

Additional Capabilities

  • Multi-Channel Engagement: Reach prospects via email, SMS, and integrated chat across their preferred channels
  • Customizable Personas: Create industry-specific AI assistants with custom messaging, tone, and conversation flows
  • Multilingual Support: Engage leads in multiple languages with culturally appropriate conversations
  • Lead Reactivation: Automatically re-engage cold leads, dormant opportunities, and aged prospects
  • Event Follow-Up: Post-event lead nurturing with context-aware conversations based on attendee engagement
  • Customer Success Use Cases: Renewal reminders, expansion opportunities, NPS follow-up, onboarding assistance

Preguntas frecuentes

How much does Conversica cost? +
Conversica uses custom enterprise pricing starting around $3,000-5,000/month for small deployments, with mid-market solutions ranging $7,000-15,000/month and enterprise packages exceeding $20,000/month. Pricing depends on lead volume, number of AI assistants, features, and use cases. Annual contracts are typical.
Does Conversica sound like a real person? +
Conversica's AI has been trained on over a decade of business conversations and produces remarkably natural, context-aware responses. While sophisticated recipients may recognize automated patterns, the quality is high enough that most prospects engage naturally. The platform's strength is persistent, relevant follow-up rather than perfect human simulation.
What CRMs does Conversica integrate with? +
Conversica offers native integrations with Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, HubSpot, and SugarCRM. The platform bidirectionally syncs conversation data, automatically updates lead records, creates tasks, and logs all interactions. Custom integrations are available for enterprise customers using other CRM systems.
How long does Conversica follow up with leads? +
Conversica can be configured to follow up persistently over weeks or months based on your sales cycle. The AI continues nurturing conversations until the lead responds, books a meeting, opts out, or reaches a configured endpoint. This persistent engagement is one of Conversica's key differentiators.
Is Conversica suitable for small businesses? +
Conversica is primarily designed for mid-market and enterprise organizations with significant lead volume (typically 100+ leads/month minimum). Small businesses with lower volumes may struggle to justify the investment. More affordable alternatives include Drift's chatbot features, HubSpot's conversation tools, or simpler email automation platforms.
