Sinopsis

Chorus.ai, now part of ZoomInfo's Revenue Operating System, is a leading conversation intelligence platform that transforms sales calls into strategic insights. The platform automatically records, transcribes, and analyzes customer conversations across calls, meetings, and emails, providing sales teams with actionable intelligence to close more deals.

What sets Chorus.ai apart is its deep AI-powered analysis of sales conversations. It doesn't just transcribe calls - it identifies key moments, tracks competitor mentions, analyzes sentiment, surfaces objections, and provides coaching opportunities. For revenue teams looking to standardize their sales process and accelerate rep productivity, Chorus.ai offers enterprise-grade conversation intelligence.

Características clave

Call Recording & Transcription

Automatically record and transcribe calls with 95%+ accuracy across 30+ languages. Searchable transcripts with speaker identification.

Deal Intelligence

AI analyzes every conversation to surface deal risks, buying signals, and next steps. Automatic CRM updates with conversation insights.

Conversation Analytics

Track talk-to-listen ratios, sentiment, keywords, competitor mentions, and objection patterns across your entire revenue team.

Sales Coaching

Identify winning behaviors from top performers. Create playlists of best calls for training. AI-powered coaching recommendations.

Smart Alerts

Real-time notifications for competitor mentions, pricing discussions, budget conversations, and deal risks requiring attention.

CRM Integration

Native integrations with Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, and deep ZoomInfo integration for complete revenue intelligence.

Preguntas frecuentes

How much does Chorus.ai cost? +
Chorus.ai uses custom enterprise pricing and doesn't publish standard rates. Pricing typically ranges from $100-150/user/month for smaller teams to $80-120/user/month for larger deployments, with volume discounts and ZoomInfo bundle options available. Contact ZoomInfo sales for specific quotes.
Does Chorus.ai require ZoomInfo? +
While Chorus.ai can function independently, it's now part of ZoomInfo's Revenue Operating System and delivers maximum value when integrated with ZoomInfo's go-to-market intelligence platform. The deep integration provides unified insights across prospecting, conversation intelligence, and revenue operations.
What CRMs does Chorus.ai integrate with? +
Chorus.ai offers native integrations with Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, and HubSpot. The platform automatically syncs conversation insights, call recordings, and next steps to your CRM, eliminating manual data entry and ensuring your pipeline data reflects actual customer conversations.
How accurate is Chorus.ai transcription? +
Chorus.ai achieves 95%+ transcription accuracy across 30+ languages with speaker identification. The AI continuously improves through machine learning and can handle various accents, industry terminology, and audio quality levels. Transcripts are searchable and include timestamps for easy navigation.
Is Chorus.ai suitable for small businesses? +
Chorus.ai is primarily designed for enterprise B2B sales organizations with complex sales processes. Small businesses may find the pricing and feature set excessive for their needs. More affordable alternatives like Fireflies.ai, Avoma, or basic features in CRMs like HubSpot may be better fits for smaller teams.
