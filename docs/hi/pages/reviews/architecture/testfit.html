TestFit Review

AI-Powered Feasibility & Site Planning for Real Estate

अवलोकन

TestFit is a cloud-based computational design platform that combines parametric design with an open marketplace of reusable building blocks. Launched in 2015, it has attracted over 15,000 users who appreciate its unique approach to architectural automation—creating configurable building tools that can be chained together to build complex workflows.

Unlike traditional parametric tools that require deep programming knowledge, TestFit provides a visual interface where architects can combine pre-built "functions" (like space planning algorithms, structural systems, or facade generators) to create custom design solutions. The platform's API-first architecture makes it particularly appealing to tech-savvy firms looking to automate repetitive design tasks.

What makes TestFit stand out is its marketplace model. Developers can create and share building configurators that other architects can use directly in their projects. This creates a growing ecosystem of design intelligence that becomes more valuable over time—similar to how app stores work for mobile platforms.

Open Marketplace

TestFit's marketplace contains dozens of ready-to-use building configurators for everything from parking layouts to MEP routing. Use them as-is or combine them to create custom workflows.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

Do I need programming skills to use TestFit?
Not for basic use. The marketplace configurators can be used without any coding. However, creating custom functions or advanced workflows benefits from programming knowledge, particularly C# or Python.
Can I monetize configurators I create?
Yes, TestFit allows developers to publish paid configurators to the marketplace. You set your own pricing and earn revenue when other users purchase your tools.
How does TestFit compare to Grasshopper?
TestFit is cloud-based and more accessible for sharing tools, while Grasshopper is local and requires Rhino. TestFit focuses on reusable building blocks and API access, while Grasshopper offers more flexibility for one-off parametric scripts.
Can I export to Revit or other BIM software?
Yes, TestFit supports export to Revit (RVT), IFC, DWG, and other common formats. The quality of the export depends on the complexity of your model and which configurators you used.
Is there a limit to model complexity on the free tier?
The free tier has compute limits rather than model complexity limits. Simple models process quickly, while complex calculations may require a Professional account for adequate computational resources.
Can I use TestFit offline?
No, TestFit is a fully cloud-based platform and requires an internet connection. All geometry processing happens on TestFit's servers, which is what enables the powerful computational capabilities.
