अवलोकन

Tempus is a technology company advancing precision medicine through AI-powered genomic analysis and clinical data integration. Founded in 2015 by Eric Lefkofsky, Tempus has built the world's largest library of molecular and clinical data, partnering with over 7,000 physicians and 65% of US academic medical centers. The platform combines next-generation sequencing with machine learning to provide personalized cancer treatment recommendations based on each patient's unique molecular profile.

What sets Tempus apart is its comprehensive approach to precision oncology, integrating genomic sequencing, clinical data analytics, and real-world evidence into a unified platform. Physicians order Tempus testing through a simple process - tumor and blood samples are sequenced using validated panels covering hundreds of cancer-related genes. Advanced AI algorithms analyze the molecular data alongside clinical information, treatment histories, and outcomes from thousands of similar patients to identify targeted therapies, immunotherapies, and clinical trials matching the patient's specific cancer profile.

Tempus serves oncologists, cancer patients, pharmaceutical companies, and research institutions. The platform has demonstrated clinical utility in guiding treatment decisions for solid tumors and hematologic malignancies, with studies showing improved patient outcomes when therapy is selected based on molecular profiling. With CLIA-certified laboratory operations, comprehensive bioinformatics pipelines, and continuous algorithm improvements from real-world data, Tempus represents the cutting edge of AI-driven personalized cancer care.

How It Compares

Tempus vs Foundation Medicine

Both are leaders in cancer genomic profiling, but with different strengths. Tempus offers broader data integration with clinical outcomes and real-world evidence, while Foundation Medicine (Roche) provides deeply validated genomic testing with extensive pharmaceutical partnerships. Tempus's AI algorithms leverage a larger clinical database for treatment matching, while Foundation Medicine's FoundationOne CDx has FDA approval as a companion diagnostic for multiple drugs. Tempus may have advantages in clinical trials matching and outcomes data, while Foundation Medicine excels in regulatory validated testing for specific targeted therapies. Many cancer centers use both platforms for complementary insights.

Tempus vs Standard Cancer Treatment

Traditional oncology relies on cancer type, stage, and limited biomarkers to guide treatment decisions. Tempus provides comprehensive molecular profiling revealing hundreds of potential therapeutic targets and resistance mechanisms invisible to standard testing. Studies show genomic profiling identifies actionable mutations in 30-40% of patients, with 15-20% receiving treatments they wouldn't have otherwise. While not all patients benefit from genomic testing, those with rare cancers, treatment-resistant disease, or uncommon presentations often gain valuable therapeutic options. The integration of real-world outcomes data helps predict which treatments are most likely to work for each patient's specific molecular profile.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

What types of cancer can Tempus analyze?+
Tempus provides genomic testing for virtually all solid tumors including lung, breast, colorectal, prostate, ovarian, melanoma, and rare cancers. The platform also covers hematologic malignancies like leukemias and lymphomas. Different sequencing panels are optimized for specific cancer types - the xT panel covers 500+ genes for comprehensive solid tumor profiling, while specialized panels address unique requirements of blood cancers. Tempus testing is most commonly used for advanced or metastatic cancers where treatment selection could be guided by molecular findings.
How much does Tempus testing cost?+
Tempus testing costs vary by panel but typically range from several thousand dollars. However, most patients pay little to nothing out-of-pocket because Tempus is covered by Medicare, Medicaid, and most major commercial insurance plans when medically necessary. Tempus handles insurance authorization and billing directly. For uninsured patients or when insurance denies coverage, Tempus offers financial assistance programs. The company works to minimize patient financial burden while ensuring access to genomic testing. Physicians should verify coverage for specific patients before ordering.
How long does it take to get Tempus results?+
Tempus typically delivers genomic testing results within 7-14 days from sample receipt at the laboratory. Turnaround time depends on sample quality, test type, and whether additional analyses are needed. Rush processing may be available for urgent cases. Results are delivered through a secure online portal accessible to ordering physicians, with comprehensive reports including genomic findings, therapy recommendations, and clinical trials matches. Tempus also provides genetic counselor support to help interpret results and discuss implications with patients.
What sample is needed for Tempus testing?+
Tempus requires a tumor tissue sample (typically formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded or FFPE blocks/slides from biopsy or surgery) along with a blood sample for germline comparison. The tumor sample should be recent and contain adequate tumor cellularity. Archived tissue from prior biopsies can often be used. Liquid biopsy using circulating tumor DNA from blood is available for some indications when tissue is unavailable. Tempus provides sample collection kits and detailed instructions to referring physicians. Pathology review ensures sample adequacy before processing.
Will Tempus results change my cancer treatment?+
Tempus identifies actionable genomic alterations in approximately 30-40% of cancer patients tested. Of these, 15-20% receive treatment modifications based on molecular findings. The impact varies by cancer type - some malignancies like lung cancer and melanoma have many targetable mutations, while others have fewer actionable targets. Even when specific targeted therapies aren't available, genomic profiling can identify clinical trials, predict immunotherapy response, or guide treatment selection based on similar patients' outcomes. Your oncologist interprets Tempus results in context of your overall clinical situation to determine appropriate treatment.
Is Tempus data secure and private?+
Yes, Tempus maintains HIPAA compliance and implements industry-standard security measures to protect patient data. Clinical and genomic information is stored securely with encryption and access controls. When data is used for research or pharmaceutical partnerships, it is de-identified to remove personal information. Patients can consent to having their de-identified data contribute to research that advances cancer treatment, or opt out if preferred. Tempus is transparent about data use and provides detailed privacy policies. The platform's security infrastructure undergoes regular third-party audits to ensure patient confidentiality.
