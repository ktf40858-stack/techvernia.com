अवलोकन

Juniper Mist AI represents a fundamentally different approach to network management. Unlike traditional vendors who added AI features to existing products, Mist was built from the ground up as an AI-native platform. Acquired by Juniper Networks in 2019, Mist has become the cornerstone of Juniper's AI-driven enterprise strategy.

The platform's crown jewel is Marvis — a virtual network assistant that uses natural language processing to answer questions, troubleshoot problems, and provide insights. Instead of digging through dashboards and logs, you can simply ask Marvis "Why is the conference room WiFi slow?" and get an actionable answer.

Mist covers the full network stack: wireless (Wi-Fi 6/6E), wired switching, and SD-WAN. Its cloud-native architecture means no controllers to deploy — everything is managed through a single cloud portal with AI continuously analyzing your network data.

Meet Marvis: Your AI Network Assistant

"Why are users in Building A complaining about video calls?"Marvis analyzes the data and responds: "3 APs in Building A are experiencing high channel utilization during video calls. Suggested action: Enable band steering and add AP in Conference Room 2."

Marvis automatically correlates events across the network to identify root causes. No more manual log analysis or guessing.

Wi-Fi Assurance

AI-driven wireless optimization including dynamic channel and power management, client steering, and interference mitigation.

WAN Assurance

Extend AI insights to SD-WAN deployments. Marvis monitors circuit health, application performance, and automatically routes around issues.

Location Services

Patented vBLE (virtual Bluetooth LE) technology for indoor positioning. Enable wayfinding, asset tracking, and location analytics.

Cloud-Native

100% cloud-managed with no controllers to deploy. Updates happen automatically, and AI models improve continuously with global learning.

मूल्य निर्धारण

Mist AI uses subscription licensing based on device count and feature tiers. All licenses include the cloud management and AI features — there's no "AI add-on" to purchase separately.

License Structure

  • Wi-Fi Assurance: ~$150-200/AP/year (includes Marvis for wireless)
  • Wired Assurance: ~$50-100/switch/year (adds Marvis for wired)
  • WAN Assurance: ~$100-200/edge/year (SD-WAN with Marvis)
  • Premium Analytics: ~$30-50/user/year (advanced location services)

Pricing varies based on term length (1, 3, or 5 years) and volume. Contact Juniper or a partner for accurate quotes.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

Can Mist work offline? +
The network itself continues to function if cloud connectivity is lost — APs and switches will operate using cached policies. However, AI features, management, and Marvis require cloud connectivity. There's no on-premise deployment option.
How does Marvis compare to Cisco AI? +
Marvis is generally considered more advanced for conversational troubleshooting and natural language queries. Cisco's DNA Center AI is stronger for automated remediation in complex Cisco environments. Marvis wins on user experience; Cisco wins on integration depth.
Do I need Juniper switches for Mist? +
No, Mist wireless works with any switch infrastructure. However, to get the full "wired assurance" AI features and Marvis visibility into the wired network, you need Juniper EX switches managed through Mist.
What about data sovereignty? +
Mist offers regional cloud instances (US, EU, APAC) to address data sovereignty requirements. Your network data stays within the selected region. For highly regulated industries, discuss specific compliance needs with Juniper.
