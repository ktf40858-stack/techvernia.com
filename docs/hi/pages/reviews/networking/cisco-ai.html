Cisco AI Network Analytics Review

by Cisco Systems • Enterprise AI-Powered Networking

★★★★★4.7/5
Updated: November 2024
12 min read
Enterprise Focus

अवलोकन

Cisco AI Network Analytics is the premier choice for enterprises with significant Cisco infrastructure. The predictive analytics, automated remediation, and wireless optimization are industry-leading. However, the high cost and Cisco-centric nature mean it's not suitable for multi-vendor environments or smaller organizations. If you're a Cisco shop, it's worth the investment.

The platform analyzes data from across your Cisco infrastructure — switches, routers, wireless controllers, and endpoints — to provide predictive insights, detect anomalies before they cause outages, and automate troubleshooting. For organizations running Cisco Catalyst or Meraki infrastructure, it's the natural choice for AI-powered network operations.

What sets Cisco AI apart is its deep integration with Cisco hardware and software. Unlike third-party monitoring tools, Cisco AI understands the specific capabilities of Cisco devices and can take automated remediation actions through DNA Center's policy engine.

Intent-Based Networking

Define your business intent (e.g., "prioritize video conferencing traffic"), and Cisco AI translates it into network configurations across your entire infrastructure automatically.

मुख्य विशेषताएं

Predictive Analytics

Machine learning models predict network issues before they impact users. Identifies capacity constraints, device failures, and performance degradation trends.

Anomaly Detection

Automatically detects unusual patterns in network behavior. Identifies security threats, misconfigurations, and performance issues in real-time.

Auto Remediation

Automatically fixes common issues without human intervention. Can reboot access points, adjust QoS policies, and reconfigure switches based on detected problems.

Wireless Optimization

AI-driven wireless network optimization. Automatically adjusts channel assignments, power levels, and roaming parameters for optimal client experience.

Client 360

Complete visibility into client behavior and experience. Track individual devices across wired and wireless networks with health scores and issue history.

Assurance Dashboard

Single-pane-of-glass view of network health. Color-coded dashboards show issues at a glance with drill-down capabilities for root cause analysis.

Meraki Integration

Extends AI analytics to Meraki cloud-managed networks. Unified view across Catalyst and Meraki infrastructure with consistent AI insights.

ThousandEyes Integration

Combines internal network insights with internet and SaaS monitoring. End-to-end visibility from user to cloud application.

Certification Path Recommendation

For DNA Center administrators, we recommend starting with CCNA for networking fundamentals, followed by DevNet Associate for automation skills. Network engineers should pursue CCNP Enterprise with wireless or advanced infrastructure concentration exams. For enterprise architects designing large-scale DNA Center deployments, CCIE Enterprise Infrastructure provides the comprehensive expertise needed.

All certifications are available through Pearson VUE testing centers and include digital badges through Credly. Cisco offers official training courses, learning labs, and practice exams through the Cisco Learning Network.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

Do I need DNA Center for Cisco AI Analytics? +
Yes, Cisco AI Network Analytics runs as part of DNA Center (now Catalyst Center). You need to deploy the DNA Center appliance on-premise to use these features.
Does it work with non-Cisco devices? +
Limited. While DNA Center can discover and monitor some third-party devices, the AI analytics features work best with Cisco Catalyst and Meraki hardware. For multi-vendor environments, consider alternatives like Datadog or Splunk.
What's the difference from Meraki AI? +
Meraki has its own cloud-based AI features. DNA Center/Catalyst Center provides similar capabilities for on-premise Catalyst equipment. The two can integrate through the DNA-Meraki integration for unified visibility.
How long does deployment take? +
Initial DNA Center deployment typically takes 2-4 weeks for a large enterprise. Getting full value from AI analytics requires historical data collection, so expect 1-3 months before predictive features are fully trained.
