Attack Signal AI

개요

Vectra AI is a leading network detection and response (NDR) platform that uses AI to identify hidden attackers in real-time. Its Attack Signal Intelligence focuses on behaviors that matter, reducing noise and highlighting genuine threats.

The platform extends beyond traditional network monitoring to cover cloud, SaaS, and identity environments. It excels at detecting lateral movement, command and control activity, and data exfiltration attempts.

Vectra's approach prioritizes attacker behaviors over signatures, making it effective against novel and sophisticated attacks that evade traditional security tools.
