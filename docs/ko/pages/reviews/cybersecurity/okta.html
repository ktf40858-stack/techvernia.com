개요

Okta is the leading identity and access management platform, providing secure authentication for employees, customers, and partners. Its cloud-native architecture and extensive integration ecosystem make it the de facto choice for enterprise IAM.

ThreatInsight uses AI to detect and block credential-based attacks in real-time, while adaptive MFA adjusts authentication requirements based on risk signals. The platform's zero trust architecture helps organizations implement modern security frameworks.

With the Auth0 acquisition, Okta now offers comprehensive identity solutions for both workforce and customer identity use cases.

주요 기능

Single Sign-On

SSO to 7000+ pre-built app integrations with SAML and OIDC support.

Adaptive MFA

Context-aware multi-factor authentication with risk-based step-up.

ThreatInsight

AI-powered detection of credential stuffing and brute force attacks.

Lifecycle Management

Automated user provisioning and deprovisioning across applications.

Universal Directory

Centralized identity store with integration to AD, LDAP, and HR systems.

Passwordless

FIDO2/WebAuthn support for passwordless authentication.
