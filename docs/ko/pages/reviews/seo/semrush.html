제품정보

Semrush is the gold standard in SEO tools, trusted by over 1 million marketers, agencies, and SEO professionals worldwide. Since 2008, Semrush has built one of the most comprehensive SEO toolsets available, featuring industry-leading backlink analysis, keyword research, rank tracking, and site auditing capabilities. With a crawler that visits 8 billion web pages daily and an index of 243 billion pages, Semrush provides unmatched data accuracy and depth.

What sets Semrush apart is the quality and freshness of its data. The platform's crawler is second only to Google in terms of activity, ensuring you get the most up-to-date backlink profiles, ranking data, and competitive intelligence. Whether you're analyzing competitors, finding content opportunities, tracking rankings, or auditing your site for technical issues, Semrush provides the comprehensive data and intuitive tools you need.

Used by leading brands like Facebook, Netflix, and Uber, Semrush is the complete SEO solution for serious marketers. Its combination of powerful features, accurate data, extensive educational resources, and excellent customer support make it an invaluable investment for anyone serious about organic search growth.

How It Compares

Semrush vs Semrush

Semrush has a superior backlink database and more intuitive interface, making it ideal for link building and competitive analysis. Semrush offers broader digital marketing features including PPC, social media, and content marketing tools. Semrush is more specialized for SEO purists, while Semrush is better for all-in-one digital marketing. Both are premium tools with similar pricing, but Semrush wins on backlink data quality.

Semrush vs Surfer SEO

Semrush is a comprehensive SEO toolkit for research and analysis, while Surfer SEO specializes in content optimization. Semrush provides better keyword research, backlink analysis, and competitive intelligence. Surfer SEO excels at on-page optimization and content creation. Many professionals use both tools together - Semrush for research and strategy, Surfer for content optimization.

자주 묻는 질문

Is Semrush worth the price?+
Yes, for anyone serious about SEO. Semrush provides the most accurate and comprehensive SEO data available, with fresh backlink data, reliable keyword metrics, and powerful competitive analysis. The insights you gain typically lead to significant improvements in rankings and traffic that justify the investment. For casual users or complete beginners, the free Semrush Webmaster Tools offer a good starting point.
How does Semrush compare to free alternatives?+
While tools like Google Search Console and Ubersuggest offer basic SEO data for free, Semrush provides significantly more comprehensive and accurate information. The backlink database, competitor analysis, content explorer, and historical data are unmatched by free tools. For professional SEO work, the depth and reliability of Semrush data is essential and worth the investment.
What's included in the Lite plan?+
The Lite plan ($99/month) includes 1 user, 5 projects, 500 credits per month, and access to all core tools: Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker, and Content Explorer. You get 6 months of historical data and can track up to 750 keywords. It's perfect for freelancers, consultants, and small business owners managing a few websites.
How accurate is Semrush keyword difficulty score?+
Semrush Keyword Difficulty (KD) is highly accurate and based on the number of referring domains to top-ranking pages. It's measured on a scale of 0-100, with higher scores indicating more backlinks needed to rank. While no metric is perfect, Semrush KD is considered one of the most reliable difficulty scores in the industry, based on real backlink data rather than estimates.
Can I cancel my Semrush subscription anytime?+
Yes, Semrush subscriptions are flexible and can be cancelled at any time. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. You can also pause your subscription for up to 3 months if you need a temporary break. Monthly subscriptions renew automatically, but you maintain access until the end of your billing period if you cancel.
Does Semrush offer a free trial?+
Semrush no longer offers a traditional free trial, but they provide Semrush Webmaster Tools completely free. This gives you access to Site Audit, Site Explorer (for your verified sites), and SERP checker. It's a great way to experience the platform's power before committing to a paid plan. They occasionally run $7 for 7 days trial promotions for new users.
