Residential Focus

Aperçu

Maket AI is a generative AI platform specifically designed for residential architectural design. Launched in 2023, it has rapidly become one of the most popular AI floor plan generators, with over 100,000 designs created. The platform focuses on making professional-quality residential design accessible to architects, builders, and property developers.

What makes Maket AI stand out is its incredible speed and ease of use. You can generate complete floor plans in seconds by simply entering basic parameters like lot size, number of bedrooms, and architectural style. The AI understands residential design conventions and generates layouts that are both aesthetically pleasing and functionally sound.

The platform has been particularly well-received by residential architects who need to quickly explore design options for client presentations, and by real estate developers looking to visualize different unit configurations. The quality of the AI-generated plans is impressive, often requiring minimal manual refinement.

Questions Fréquemment Posées

Can I use Maket AI for commercial projects?
Yes, Professional and Studio plans include full commercial licensing. You can use AI-generated floor plans in client projects, for sale, or in real estate development without additional fees.
How accurate are the floor plans?
Maket AI generates dimensionally accurate floor plans suitable for concept presentations and preliminary design. However, they should be reviewed and refined by a licensed architect before construction.
Can I import my own site plans?
Currently, you input lot dimensions manually. Direct import of CAD or PDF site plans is not yet supported but is on the roadmap for future releases.
Does Maket AI work for commercial buildings?
No, Maket AI is specifically optimized for residential architecture. For commercial projects, consider alternatives like Hypar or TestFit.
Can I edit the generated floor plans?
You can regenerate with different parameters and choose from variations, but in-app editing is limited. Most users export to CAD or BIM software for detailed modifications.
What file formats can I export?
Currently, high-quality PDF is the primary export format. DWG and Revit export are planned features. The PDFs include dimensions and can be imported into most CAD software.
