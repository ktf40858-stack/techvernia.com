What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an AI image generator that runs entirely through Discord. Founded by David Holz (former Leap Motion founder), it launched in July 2022 and quickly became the gold standard for AI-generated art.

Unlike DALL-E 3 (integrated into ChatGPT) or Stable Diffusion (open-source software), Midjourney operates as a Discord bot. You type commands like /imagine prompt: a serene mountain landscape at sunset and Midjourney generates four image variations in 30-60 seconds.

Key Stats (2026):

  • 20+ million users worldwide
  • V6 model (released late 2023, refined through 2024-2025)
  • Discord-only interface (no web app, mobile via Discord app)
  • Pricing: $10/month (Basic), $30/month (Standard), $60/month (Pro), $120/month (Mega)
  • Commercial use: Allowed on paid plans
  • No free tier (ended in 2023 due to abuse)

Midjourney V6: What's New in 2026

Midjourney V6, the current flagship model, represents a massive leap over V5. Here's what improved:

V6 Improvements:

  • Photorealism: V6 can generate near-photographic images when prompted correctly
  • Prompt adherence: Better understanding of detailed, longer prompts (up to ~350 words)
  • Text rendering: Improved but still not perfect (Ideogram beats it here)
  • Anatomy & hands: Significantly better human anatomy and hand rendering
  • Coherence: More consistent style across upscaled and varied images
  • Aspect ratios: Support for custom aspect ratios beyond standard 16:9, 1:1, etc.

V6 also introduced style references (--sref) and character references (--cref), allowing you to maintain consistent styles or characters across generations.

Detailed Feature Breakdown

1. Image Quality & Artistic Style

Winner: Midjourney

Midjourney's defining characteristic is consistent artistic excellence. Even simple prompts produce aesthetically pleasing results. The model has a natural bias toward:

  • Cinematic lighting: Dramatic, professionally lit scenes
  • Rich color palettes: Vibrant yet balanced color choices
  • Artistic composition: Rule of thirds, leading lines, depth
  • Texture detail: Impressive surface detail and materials

This makes Midjourney ideal for:

  • Concept art for games, films, books
  • Marketing visuals and hero images
  • Book covers and editorial illustrations
  • Social media content that needs to "pop"
  • Fine art prints and gallery work

2. Discord-Based Workflow

Unique to Midjourney (Pro/Con)

Midjourney's Discord-only interface is controversial. Here's the reality:

Why Discord:

  • Community exposure: See what others are creating, learn from their prompts
  • Rapid iteration: Discord's speed suits fast experimentation
  • Social learning: Public channels teach prompt engineering through observation

Discord Downsides:

  • Learning curve: Memorizing slash commands (/imagine, /blend, /describe, etc.)
  • No traditional interface: No drag-and-drop, no visual editor
  • Image management: Finding old generations requires scrolling through Discord history
  • Privacy: Generations are public by default (private mode requires Pro plan or higher)

Workaround: Use Direct Messages with the Midjourney Bot for private generation, or upgrade to a plan with Stealth Mode ($60/month Pro plan).

3. Prompt Control & Parameters

Advanced but learnable

Midjourney uses parameters to control output. Key parameters include:

  • --ar (aspect ratio): --ar 16:9, --ar 1:1, --ar 3:2
  • --s (stylize): How "artistic" vs literal (0-1000, default 100)
  • --v (version): --v 6 for V6, --v 5.2 for V5.2, etc.
  • --chaos: Variation randomness (0-100)
  • --weird: Unconventional, experimental results (0-3000)
  • --sref (style reference): Match a reference image's style
  • --cref (character reference): Maintain consistent characters

Example prompt: /imagine a cyberpunk street market at night, neon lights, rain --ar 16:9 --s 750 --v 6

4. V6 Photorealism & Accuracy

Dramatically improved in V6

V6 introduced photorealistic capabilities. To trigger photorealism:

  • Use descriptive, detailed prompts (like DALL-E 3)
  • Specify photography terms: "shot on Canon EOS R5, 85mm f/1.4, natural lighting"
  • Lower stylization: --s 0 to --s 250

V6 photorealism rivals DALL-E 3

5. Upscaling & Variations

Flexible iteration workflow

Midjourney generates 4 variations per prompt. From there:

  • U1-U4 buttons: Upscale (increase resolution) any of the 4 images
  • V1-V4 buttons: Create 4 new variations similar to chosen image
  • Vary (Strong/Subtle): V6 feature for controlled variations
  • Zoom Out: Expand the image canvas
  • Pan arrows: Extend image in specific directions

This workflow encourages exploration and iteration.

7. Text Rendering Capabilities

Improved but not best-in-class

V6 improved text rendering but Midjourney still struggles with:

  • Complex typography (Ideogram is better)
  • Long text passages (often garbled)
  • Precise spelling (hits and misses)

For logos, posters, or designs requiring perfect text, use Ideogram instead. For artistic images with minimal text, V6 is adequate.

8. Community & Learning Resources

Massive, active community

Midjourney's 20M+ user community is a huge asset:

  • Public Discord channels: Watch others' prompts and results in real-time
  • /describe command: Reverse-engineer prompts from images
  • Third-party sites: MidjourneyPrompts.com, PromptoMania, PromptHero
  • YouTube tutorials: Thousands of advanced technique videos

The community accelerates learning dramatically.

Tips for Getting the Most from Midjourney

1. Master Prompt Engineering

Specific, detailed prompts yield better results. Instead of "a dragon," try "a majestic red dragon perched on a medieval castle tower at sunset, dramatic clouds, fantasy art style, highly detailed scales."

2. Use Style References (--sref)

Maintain consistent branding by using --sref [image URL] to match a reference style across multiple generations.

3. Leverage /describe

Upload an image and use /describe to see how Midjourney interprets it. Use the generated prompts as templates.

4. Experiment with Stylize Values

  • --s 0 to --s 250: More literal, photorealistic
  • --s 500 to --s 750: Balanced (most use this range)
  • --s 1000: Maximum artistic interpretation

5. Use Relax Mode Strategically

On Standard+ plans, use Fast Mode for urgent work, Relax Mode for exploration. Relax is unlimited, so experiment freely without burning GPU hours.

よくある質問

Can I use Midjourney images commercially?

Yes! All paid plans (Basic, Standard, Pro, Mega) include commercial use rights. You own the images you generate (subject to Midjourney's Terms of Service). This makes it viable for client work, products, and sales.

Is there a Midjourney free trial or free tier?

No. Midjourney discontinued free trials in March 2023 due to abuse. The cheapest option is the Basic plan at $10/month. However, you can cancel anytime, so try it for one month risk-free.

Can I use Midjourney without Discord?

No. Midjourney is Discord-only. There's been speculation about a web interface, but as of December 2026, Discord is the only way to access Midjourney. You can use Discord's mobile app for on-the-go generation.

How does Midjourney V6 compare to DALL-E 3?

Midjourney V6 produces more consistently artistic, visually stunning images. DALL-E 3 is better at understanding complex natural language prompts and has better text rendering. DALL-E 3 is easier to use (integrated into ChatGPT), while Midjourney requires learning Discord commands. Quality-wise, they're comparable, with Midjourney having a slight edge on pure aesthetics.

What's the difference between Fast and Relax mode?

Fast Mode uses dedicated GPU resources and generates images in ~60 seconds. It consumes your monthly GPU hours (3.3 hrs on Basic, 15 hrs on Standard, etc.). Relax Mode is unlimited but queued—images take 5-10 minutes depending on server load. Relax is only available on Standard, Pro, and Mega plans.

Can I get private/stealth generation on Midjourney?

Yes, but only with the Pro plan ($60/month) or Mega plan ($120/month). These include Stealth Mode, which makes your generations invisible to other users. On Basic and Standard plans, your images are public in community feeds (though you can DM the bot for semi-privacy).

Does Midjourney have an API?

No. Midjourney does not offer a public API as of December 2026. If you need programmatic image generation, use DALL-E 3 (via OpenAI API), Stable Diffusion (via Stability AI API or self-hosted), or Ideogram (limited API in beta).
