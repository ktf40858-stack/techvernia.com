Cylance Review

by BlackBerry • Predictive AI Endpoint Protection

★★★★☆4.5/5
Updated: November 2026
ML Prevention

概要

Cylance, now part of BlackBerry, pioneered the use of artificial intelligence in endpoint security. Its predictive AI approach prevents malware and threats before they can execute, rather than relying on signatures or behavioral analysis after the fact.

The platform uses mathematical models trained on billions of file characteristics to identify malicious code with high accuracy. This approach requires minimal system resources and works effectively even without internet connectivity.

CylancePROTECT provides prevention, while CylanceOPTICS adds EDR capabilities for detection and response. Together they form a comprehensive endpoint security solution suitable for organizations of all sizes.

よくある質問

What makes Cylance different?

Cylance was a pioneer in using AI/ML for malware prevention without signatures. The lightweight agent works offline, making it ideal for air-gapped systems, IoT devices, and environments with limited connectivity.

Is Cylance now part of BlackBerry?

Yes. BlackBerry acquired Cylance in 2019. The product is now called BlackBerry Cylance and integrates with BlackBerry's UEM and security portfolio, though it remains a standalone endpoint protection solution.

Does Cylance work without internet?

Yes. Cylance's AI model runs locally on the endpoint, providing protection even when offline. This makes it excellent for industrial environments, air-gapped networks, and remote locations with poor connectivity.

How does Cylance compare to CrowdStrike?

Cylance excels at prevention and costs less (~$29/yr vs ~$100/yr). CrowdStrike offers superior EDR, threat intelligence, and detection capabilities. Choose Cylance for budget-friendly prevention, CrowdStrike for comprehensive security operations.

Is Cylance suitable for small businesses?

Yes. Cylance's affordability (~$29/endpoint/year), simple deployment, and low resource usage make it ideal for small businesses needing effective protection without complexity or high costs.
