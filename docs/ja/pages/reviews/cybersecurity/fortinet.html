Fortinet FortiGate Review

by Fortinet • AI-Powered Next-Gen Firewall

★★★★☆4.7/5
Updated: November 2026
FortiAI

概要

Fortinet FortiGate is a leading next-generation firewall that combines high performance with AI-powered threat detection. The platform leverages FortiGuard AI security services for real-time protection against emerging threats.

FortiGate's Security Fabric architecture enables unified visibility and automated response across the entire digital attack surface, including network, endpoint, cloud, and OT environments.

With integrated SD-WAN capabilities, FortiGate offers a converged solution for organizations looking to consolidate network and security functions.
